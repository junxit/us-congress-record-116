# Vote on Blumenfeld Nomination (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Crapo, Mike (C000880) [R-ID], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                     Vote on Blumenfeld Nomination

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, all postcloture time 
has expired on the Blumenfeld nomination.
  The question is, Shall the Senate advise and consent to the 
Blumenfeld nomination?
  Mr. CRAPO. Mr. President, I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk called the roll.
  Mr. THUNE. The following Senator is necessarily absent: the Senator 
from West Virginia (Mrs. Capito).
  Mr. DURBIN. I announce that the Senator from Delaware (Mr. Coons), 
the Senator from California (Ms. Harris), and the Senator from Vermont 
(Mr. Sanders) are necessarily absent.
```
