# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 21, 2020

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                 ORDERS FOR MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 21, 2020

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 3 p.m., Monday, 
September 21; further, that following the prayer and pledge, the 
morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved 
to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and morning business be closed; further, that following 
leader remarks, the Senate proceed to executive session to resume 
consideration of the Meyers nomination; and finally, that 
notwithstanding rule XXII, the cloture motions filed during today's 
session ripen at 5:30 p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
