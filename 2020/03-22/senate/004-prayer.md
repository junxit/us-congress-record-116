# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, open our eyes to Your movements in our midst. Remind 
our lawmakers that You have brought us through times more challenging 
than what we now face. Keep them from being intimidated by this global 
pandemic, as they cling to Your promises and trust the unfolding of 
Your triumphant providence.
  Lord, continue to protect them and to order their steps. Clothe them 
with the honor of integrity. Shield them with Your truth and guide them 
with Your power.
  We pray in Your strong Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
