# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, we praise You with our whole hearts. We refuse to 
forget how You have led our Nation in the past and trust You to guard 
our future.
  Lord, encourage our lawmakers to be a part of Your solutions and not 
a part of the problems that confront our land. Give them the courage to 
carry on knowing that nothing is too difficult for Your sovereign 
might.
  May the light of Your truth illuminate their way as they find in You 
a sure guide. Help them to commit their lives to those that will cause 
justice to roll down like waters and righteousness like a mighty 
stream.
  We pray in Your sacred Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
