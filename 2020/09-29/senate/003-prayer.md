# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, sovereign source of wisdom, hear our prayers. Listen as 
we lift our hearts to You. In our times of distress, answer us quickly.
  Strengthen our lawmakers. Keep them restless until they find rest in 
You. Keep them dissatisfied until they find their satisfaction in You.
  Lord, give them the wisdom to make wise choices that will glorify 
Your Name. Help them to keep their hearts and minds responsive to You, 
enabling them to help make a better Nation and world.
  Remind them that You keep a protective eye on the godly so that they 
may be surrounded with the shield of Your divine favor. May our 
Senators, with faith, expect You to provide them with strength for each 
task.
  We pray in Your great Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
