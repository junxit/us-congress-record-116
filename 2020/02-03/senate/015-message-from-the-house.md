# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** S. 153, S. 3201

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE


                         enrolled bills signed

  At 3:47 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Novotny, one of its reading clerks, announced that the Speaker 
pro tempore (Ms. Matsui) has signed the following enrolled bills:

       S. 153. An act to promote veteran involvement in STEM 
     education, computer science, and scientific research, and for 
     other purposes.
       S. 3201. An act to extend the temporary scheduling order 
     for fentanyl-related substances, and for other purposes.

  The enrolled bills were subsequently signed by the President pro 
tempore (Mr. Grassley).

                          ____________________
```
