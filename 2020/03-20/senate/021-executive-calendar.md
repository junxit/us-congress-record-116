# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
Foreign Relations Committee be discharged from further consideration of 
PN1611 and that the Senate proceed to consideration of that nomination.
  There being no objection, the committee was discharged and the Senate 
proceeded to consider the nomination.
  Mr. McCONNELL. I ask unanimous consent that the Senate vote on the 
nomination with no intervening action or debate; that, if confirmed, 
the motion to reconsider be considered made and laid upon the table; 
and that the President be immediately notified of the Senate's action.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The question is, Will the Senate advise and consent to the nomination 
of Alma L. Golden, of Texas, to be an Assistant Administrator of the 
United States Agency for International Development?
  The nomination was confirmed.

                          ____________________
```
