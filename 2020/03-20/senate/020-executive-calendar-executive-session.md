proceed to executive session for the consideration of Executive 
Calendar Nos. 604 and 606 through 628 and all nominations on the 
Secretary's desk in the Air Force, Army, Marine Corps, and Navy; that 
the nominations be confirmed; that the motions to reconsider be 
considered made and laid upon the table with no intervening action or 
debate; and that the President be immediately notified of the Senate's 
action.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
