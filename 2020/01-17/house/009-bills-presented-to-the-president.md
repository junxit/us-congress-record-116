# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 263, H.R. 434

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Cheryl L. Johnson, Clerk of the House, reported that on January 16, 
2020, she presented to the President of the United States, for his 
approval, the following bills:

       H.R. 434. To amend the National Trails System Act to 
     provide for the study of the Emancipation National Historic 
     Trail, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 263. To rename the Oyster Bay National Wildlife Refuge 
     as the Congressman Lester Wolff Oyster Bay National Wildlife 
     Refuge.

                          ____________________
```
