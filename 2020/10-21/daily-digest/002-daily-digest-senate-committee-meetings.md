# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
PASSENGER AND FREIGHT RAIL
Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation: Committee concluded 
a hearing to examine passenger and freight rail, focusing on the 
current status of the rail network and the track ahead, after receiving 
testimony from William Flynn, National Railroad Passenger Corporation 
(Amtrak), Ian N. Jefferies, Association of American Railroads, and 
Dennis R. Pierce, Teamsters Rail Conference, all of Washington, D.C.; 
Paul Tuss, Bear Paw Development Corporation, Havre, Montana; Frank 
Chirumbole, Olin Corporation, Houston, Texas, on behalf of the American 
Chemistry Council; Randy Gordon, National Grain and Feed Association, 
Arlington, Virginia; and Kent Fountain, National Cotton Council, 
Surrency, Georgia.
VA MISSION ACT
Committee on Veterans' Affairs: Committee concluded a hearing to 
examine the VA MISSION Act, focusing

[[Page D933]]

on assessing progress implementing Title I, after receiving testimony 
from Lt. Gen. Patricia D. Horoho, USA (Ret.), OptumServe, Reunion, 
Florida; David J. McIntyre, Jr., TriWest Healthcare Alliance, 
Scottsdale, Arizona; and Steve Schwab, Washington, D.C., Jennie Beller, 
Indianapolis, Indiana, and Molly Ramsey, Arlington, Virginia, all of 
the Elizabeth Dole Foundation.
```
