# RANKING A MEMBER OF A CERTAIN STANDING COMMITTEE OF THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

**Speaking:** Cheney, Liz (C001109) [R-WY]

**Measures:** H.Res. 903

```
   RANKING A MEMBER OF A CERTAIN STANDING COMMITTEE OF THE HOUSE OF 
                            REPRESENTATIVES

  Ms. CHENEY. Mr. Speaker, by direction of the Republican Conference, I 
offer a privileged resolution and ask for its immediate consideration.

[[Page H1692]]

  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

                              H. Res. 903

       Resolved, That the following named Member be, and is 
     hereby, ranked as follows on the following standing committee 
     of the House of Representatives:
       Committee on the Judiciary: Mr. Jordan, to rank before Mr. 
     Collins of Georgia.

  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
