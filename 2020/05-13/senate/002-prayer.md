# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, the author of unity, remind our lawmakers that people 
accomplish laudable goals more through unity than division. May our 
Senators remember that all of us are more effective than any one of us.
  As our legislators pursue effectiveness in their work, give them the 
wisdom to forge alliances that make the difficult become easy and the 
impossible become probable. In their search for common purposes, 
inspire them with the conviction that nothing is impossible for those 
who believe.
  We pray in Your omnipotent Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
