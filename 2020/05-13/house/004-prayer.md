# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Patrick J. Conroy, offered the following 
prayer:
  Compassionate and merciful God, thank You for giving us another day.
  In the days that come the House will be considering another massive 
economic bill to address the coronavirus' effect on the health of 
Americans and our economy.
  These will be days of considerable debate. The differences and 
disagreements among the Members will be open, clear, and public.
  Send Your spirit of calm and wisdom, that each Member will be 
inspired by insight and understanding, but also respect and a shared 
commitment to facing our Nation's greatest challenge in nearly a 
century.
  Bless their efforts, and in the days to come, may everything done be 
for Your greater honor and glory.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
