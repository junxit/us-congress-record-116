# RECOGNIZING MARY BALDWIN

**Speaking:** Frankel, Lois (F000462) [D-FL]

```
                        RECOGNIZING MARY BALDWIN

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. LOIS FRANKEL

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, August 21, 2020

  Ms. FRANKEL. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize Mary Baldwin, a 
local hero from Florida's 21st Congressional District who has gone 
above and beyond for students in our community.
  Mary has worked at Wellington Landing Middle School since 1999, and 
currently serves as their Exceptional Student Education (ESE) 
Administrator and Assistant Principal. As an educator of over 35 years, 
Mary is known for going the extra mile with her students, often 
counseling her kids all the way through their time at Wellington 
Landing and sometimes even keeping in touch with them through high 
school and beyond. During the COVID-19 public health crisis, Mary has 
gone even further to support our local students by establishing a 
summer school program designed to support 8th graders who struggled 
with the new distance learning protocols implemented during the spring 
semester. Mary worked one-on-one with each student in the program and 
everyone enrolled was able to pass and move on to high school.
  Mary's selflessness and compassion during this difficult time is 
truly humbling. Her outstanding work has touched countless lives, and I 
am so thankful for her dedication to our community. I am proud to honor 
her today.

                          ____________________
```
