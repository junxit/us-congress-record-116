# TRIBUTE TO THE ARMSTEAD FAMILY

**Speaking:** Moolenaar, John R. (M001194) [R-MI]

```
                     TRIBUTE TO THE ARMSTEAD FAMILY

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. JOHN R. MOOLENAAR

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 19, 2020

  Mr. MOOLENAAR. Madam Speaker, I rise today to pay tribute to the 
Armstead Family to commemorate their service and contributions to 
Michigan and the United States of America.
  As residents of Michigan, Peter Armstead, Earl Armstead, and Robert 
Armstead all answered the call to serve and stood ready to pay the 
ultimate sacrifice for our nation. Their strength and courage will be 
marked by their burial in the Great Lakes National Cemetery, resting 
peacefully side-by-side. Their family has paid a great tribute to this 
country and they will be commemorated with this esteemed and well-
deserved honor.
  With a long family history dating back to the 1800s, Peter, Earl and 
Robert represent three generations of Armstead Family patriotism and 
dedication to serving on the front lines with the United States 
Military. Having served in three significant wars in American history--
Peter as a Civil War Veteran, Earl as a World War I Veteran, and Robert 
Armstead as a World War II Veteran--the Armsteads have displayed 
enduring selflessness and tremendous sacrifice.
  This exemplary service and unprecedented legacy has earned the 
Armstead family the esteemed honor of having Peter, Earl, and Robert 
buried with full military honors. On behalf of the Fourth Congressional 
District of Michigan, I am honored today to recognize the Armstead 
Family and extend my highest appreciation for their sacrifice and 
commitment to Michigan and the United States of America.

                          ____________________
```
