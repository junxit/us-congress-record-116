# HONORING THE LIFE OF KEITH COLE

**Speaking:** Kelly, Trent (K000388) [R-MS]

```
                    HONORING THE LIFE OF KEITH COLE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. TRENT KELLY

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 19, 2020

  Mr. KELLY of Mississippi. Madam Speaker, I rise today in celebration 
of the life of Trooper Keith Cole who passed away last week after a 
courageous battle with cancer. Keith Cole was dedicated to his family, 
career, and community, and was a proud protector of the people of Lee 
County. I thank Mr. Cole for his commitment to the people of 
Mississippi, and for being a symbol of honor and integrity throughout 
his life.
  Keith Cole was an active member of the Tupelo community. Graduating 
from Mooreville High School before attending Itawamba Community 
College, Mr. Cole pursued his lifelong dream of a career in law 
enforcement. He graduated from the Tupelo Police Academy and served as 
a Mississippi Highway Patrol Officer. Mr. Cole was dedicated to serving 
the people of Mississippi, but he was an ever-present husband and 
father to his wife of 17 years, Monica Jenkins Cole and his son 
Michael. He leaves behind a loving family who will carry on his memory.
  Trooper Cole was a valiant officer of the law, a loving husband and 
father, and a pillar of his community. While we mourn his loss and 
honor his memory, we recognize that his impact on Lee County has been 
immeasurable. I wish my sincerest condolences to the family and friends 
of Trooper Cole.

                          ____________________
```
