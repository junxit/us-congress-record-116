# SWEARING IN OF THE HONORABLE THOMAS P. TIFFANY, OF WISCONSIN, AS A MEMBER OF THE HOUSE

**Speaking:** Sensenbrenner, F. James, Jr. (S000244) [R-WI]

```
  SWEARING IN OF THE HONORABLE THOMAS P. TIFFANY, OF WISCONSIN, AS A 
                          MEMBER OF THE HOUSE

  Mr. SENSENBRENNER. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the 
gentleman from Wisconsin, the Honorable Thomas P. Tiffany, be permitted
```
