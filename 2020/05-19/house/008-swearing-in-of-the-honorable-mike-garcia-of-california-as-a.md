**Speaking:** Sherman, Brad (S000344) [D-CA]

```
SWEARING IN OF THE HONORABLE MIKE GARCIA, OF CALIFORNIA, AS A MEMBER OF 
                               THE HOUSE

  Mr. SHERMAN. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the 
gentleman from California, the Honorable Mike Garcia, be permitted to
```
