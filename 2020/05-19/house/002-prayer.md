# prayer

```
                                 prayer

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Patrick J. Conroy, offered the following 
prayer:
  Compassionate and merciful God, thank You for giving us another day.
  Inspire the men and women of Congress during this time of social 
distancing to find ways of serving their constituents and our Nation as 
best they can. May their efforts, and cooperative work with local 
government efforts, help to lift those Americans who struggle most in 
these days.
  May each American rise to our shared effort to remind ourselves that 
we are all responsible for the greatness of our Nation, and to make the 
sacrifices necessary to emerge better in virtue and stronger in 
national well-being. With Your grace, O Lord, may it be so.
  May everything done this day be for Your greater honor and glory.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
