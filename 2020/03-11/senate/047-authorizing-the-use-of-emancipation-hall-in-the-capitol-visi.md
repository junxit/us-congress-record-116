# AUTHORIZING THE USE OF EMANCIPATION HALL IN THE CAPITOL VISITOR CENTER FOR A CEREMONY TO PRESENT THE CONGRESSIONAL GOLD MEDAL COLLECTIVELY TO THE CHINESE-AMERICAN VETERANS OF WORLD WAR II

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 91

```
AUTHORIZING THE USE OF EMANCIPATION HALL IN THE CAPITOL VISITOR CENTER 
FOR A CEREMONY TO PRESENT THE CONGRESSIONAL GOLD MEDAL COLLECTIVELY TO 
             THE CHINESE-AMERICAN VETERANS OF WORLD WAR II

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the consideration of H. Con. Res. 91, which was received 
from the House.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the concurrent 
resolution by title.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

       A concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 91) authorizing the 
     use of Emancipation Hall in the Capitol Visitor Center for a 
     ceremony to present the Congressional Gold Medal collectively 
     to the Chinese-American veterans of World War II.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
resolution.
  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
resolution be agreed to and the motion to reconsider be considered made 
and laid upon the table with no intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 91) was agreed to.
```
