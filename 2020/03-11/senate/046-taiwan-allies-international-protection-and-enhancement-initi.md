# TAIWAN ALLIES INTERNATIONAL PROTECTION AND ENHANCEMENT INITIATIVE (TAIPEI) ACT OF 2019

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** S. 1678

```
   TAIWAN ALLIES INTERNATIONAL PROTECTION AND ENHANCEMENT INITIATIVE 
                          (TAIPEI) ACT OF 2019

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Chair 
lay before the Senate the message to accompany S. 1678.
  The Presiding Officer laid before the Senate the following message 
from the House of Representatives:

       Resolved, That the bill from the Senate (S. 1678) entitled 
     ``An Act to express United States support for Taiwan's 
     diplomatic alliances around the world.'', do pass with an 
     amendment.


                            Motion to Concur

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I move to concur in the House 
amendment, and I ask unanimous consent that the motion be agreed to and 
that the motion to reconsider be considered made and laid upon the 
table.
```
