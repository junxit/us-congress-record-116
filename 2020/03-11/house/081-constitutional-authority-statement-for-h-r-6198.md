# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 6198

**Measures:** H.R. 6198

```
           By Mrs. MURPHY of Florida:
       H.R. 6198.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 1, which gives Congress the 
     power to provide for the general welfare of the United 
     States, and Article I, Section 8, Clause 3, which gives 
     Congress the power to regulate commerce among the several 
     states.
```
