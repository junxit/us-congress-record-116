# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA], Blackburn, Marsha (B001243) [R-TN]

**Measures:** S. 5045

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the nomination.

[[Page S7568]]

  The bill clerk read the nomination of Zachary N. Somers, of the 
District of Columbia, to be a Judge of the United States Court of 
Federal Claims for a term of fifteen years.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Iowa.
  (The remarks of Mr. GRASSLEY pertaining to the introduction of S. 
5045 are printed in today's Record under ``Statements on Introduced 
Bills and Joint Resolutions.'')
  Mr. GRASSLEY. I yield the floor.
  I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mrs. BLACKBURN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Young). Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
```
