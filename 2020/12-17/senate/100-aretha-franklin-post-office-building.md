# ARETHA FRANKLIN POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 3976

```
                  ARETHA FRANKLIN POST OFFICE BUILDING

       A bill (H.R. 3976) to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 12711 East Jefferson Avenue 
     in Detroit, Michigan, as the ``Aretha Franklin Post Office 
     Building'' was ordered to a third reading, was read the third
```
