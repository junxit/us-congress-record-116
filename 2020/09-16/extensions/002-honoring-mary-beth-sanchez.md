# HONORING MARY BETH SANCHEZ

**Speaking:** Pence, Greg (P000615) [R-IN]

```
                       HONORING MARY BETH SANCHEZ

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. GREG PENCE

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                     Wednesday, September 16, 2020

  Mr. PENCE. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor a mother, friend and 
family member, my cousin, Mary Beth Sanchez, who will be celebrating 
her 60th birthday on October 9, 2020.
  Mary Beth is a kind, caring and loving woman whose presence always 
lights up a room. Family means the world to Mary and she always goes 
out of her way to show it.
  Happy 60th Birthday, Mary Beth.
  We love her.

                          ____________________
```
