                          personal explanation

  Mr. O'HALLERAN. Mr. Speaker, on September 16, 2020, I did not have a 
chance to cast my vote for two bills on the floor of the House of 
Representatives. I would like the record to reflect that I would have 
voted in favor of H.R. 7909, the Ensuring Children and Child Care 
Workers Are Safe Act, legislation that I have cosponsored. 
Additionally, I would have voted against the motion to recommit and in 
favor of final passage for H.R. 2574, the Equity and Inclusion 
Enforcement Act.
