# Nomination of Michael Pack (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Risch, James E. (R000584) [R-ID]

```
                       Nomination of Michael Pack

  Mr. RISCH. Mr. President and fellow Senators, we are about to do the 
final vote on Michael Pack. This man is uniquely qualified to hold this 
position. He has done an outstanding job. Everyone should look at the 
most recent documentary he did on the Supreme Court. It was just 
outstanding.
  There has been a political fight over him for 2 years and 1 day. 
Today is the moment of truth. It is time to vote on Mr. Pack. Debate is 
closed.
  I yield the floor.
```
