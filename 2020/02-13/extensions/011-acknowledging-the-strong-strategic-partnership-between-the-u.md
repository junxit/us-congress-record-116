# ACKNOWLEDGING THE STRONG STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP BETWEEN THE UNITED STATES AND INDIA

**Speaking:** King, Peter T. (K000210) [R-NY]

```
   ACKNOWLEDGING THE STRONG STRATEGIC PARTNERSHIP BETWEEN THE UNITED 
                            STATES AND INDIA

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. PETER T. KING

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, February 13, 2020

  Mr. KING of New York. Madam Speaker, I rise today to acknowledge the 
strong strategic partnership between the United States and India. A 
relationship that is based on shared values of democracy. Our 
cooperation extends across many sectors including financial, defense 
and counterterrorism. The region of Kashmir has long been subjected to 
conflict and instability. I support Prime Minister Modi in his efforts 
to bring stability to the region.

                          ____________________
```
