# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 79, H.R. 2546

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 1:16 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bill and joint resolution, in which it requests 
the concurrence of the Senate:

       H.R. 2546. An act to designate certain lands in the State 
     of Colorado as components of the National Wilderness 
     Preservation System, and for other purposes.
       H.J. Res. 79. Joint resolution removing the deadline for 
     the ratification of the equal rights amendment.

                          ____________________
```
