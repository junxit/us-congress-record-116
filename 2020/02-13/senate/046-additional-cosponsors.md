                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 206

  At the request of Mr. Tester, the name of the Senator from

of the Army Signal Corps, known as the ``Hello Girls''.

(Mrs. Blackburn) was added as a cosponsor of S. 296, a bill to amend
