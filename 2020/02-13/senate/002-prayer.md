# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, a light to a dark world, we honor and praise Your Name.
  Lord, continue to guide our Senators. Use them to bring a little more 
light and truth to our Nation. Help them to remember that Your timing 
is not their timing, but Your providence will prevail. May they embrace 
the demands of a life of integrity, a life that refuses to give in to 
fear, hypocrisy, and hatred. Lord, You are our strength and shield, and 
we trust You to guide our steps.
  We pray in Your strong Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
