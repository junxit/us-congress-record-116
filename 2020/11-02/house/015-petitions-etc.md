# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       PT-147. The SPEAKER presented a petition of Mr. Gregory D. 
     Watson, a citizen of Austin, TX, relative to requesting 
     enactment of Federal legislation that would incentivize 
     American manufacturers to fabricate disposable gloves, hard 
     hats, face masks, face shields--and other forms of personal 
     protective equipment--within the United States and produced 
     by American workers; which was referred to the Committee on 
     Energy and Commerce.
```
