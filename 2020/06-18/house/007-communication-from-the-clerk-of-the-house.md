# COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

**Measures:** S. 712, H.R. 1957, S. 3731

```
               COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

  The SPEAKER laid before the House the following communication from 
the Clerk of the House of Representatives:

                                              Office of the Clerk,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                    Washington, DC, June 18, 2020.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Madam Speaker: Pursuant to the permission granted in 
     Clause 2(h) of Rule II of the Rules of the U.S. House of 
     Representatives, the Clerk received the following message 
     from the Secretary of the Senate on June 18, 2020, at 9:15 
     a.m.:
       That the Senate passed S. 712.
       That the Senate passed S. 3731.
       That the Senate passed with amendments H.R. 1957.
       With best wishes, I am,
           Sincerely,
                                                 Robert F. Reeves,
     Deputy Clerk.

                          ____________________
```
