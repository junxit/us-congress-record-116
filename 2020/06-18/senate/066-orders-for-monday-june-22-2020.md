# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JUNE 22, 2020

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JUNE 22, 2020

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 3 p.m., Monday, 
June 22; further, that following the prayer and pledge, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and morning business be closed; further, that following leader 
remarks, the Senate proceed to executive session to resume 
consideration of the Wilson nomination. Finally, I ask unanimous 
consent that notwithstanding the provisions of rule XXII, the cloture 
vote on the Wilson nomination occur at 5:30 p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
