                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

                 Week of June 16 through June 19, 2020

                             Senate Chamber

  On Tuesday, Senate will continue consideration of H.R. 1957, Taxpayer 
First Act (the legislative vehicle for the Great American Outdoors 
Act), post-cloture.
  During the balance of the week, Senate may consider any cleared 
legislative and executive business.
