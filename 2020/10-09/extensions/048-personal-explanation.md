# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Huizenga, Bill (H001058) [R-MI]

**Measures:** H.Res. 1153, H.Res. 1154

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. BILL HUIZENGA

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 9, 2020

  Mr. HUIZENGA. Madam Speaker, I rise today regarding missed votes. Had 
I been present for roll call vote number 215, On Ordering the Previous 
Question for Providing for consideration of the resolution H. Res. 1153 
condemning unwanted, unnecessary medical procedures on individuals 
without their full, informed consent, and providing for consideration 
of the resolution H. Res. 1154 condemning QAnon and rejecting the 
conspiracy theories it promotes, I would have voted ``nay.'' Had I been 
present for roll call vote number 217 H. Res. 1153 condemning unwanted, 
unnecessary medical procedures on individuals without their full, 
informed consent, I would have voted ``yay .'' Had I been present for 
roll call vote number 218 H. Res. 1154 condemning QAnon and rejecting 
the conspiracy theories it promotes, I would have voted ``yay.''
```
