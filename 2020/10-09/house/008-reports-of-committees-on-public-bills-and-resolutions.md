# REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.R. 2795, H.R. 5179

```
         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XIII, reports of committees were delivered to 
the Clerk for printing and reference to the proper calendar, as 
follows:

       Mr. GRIJALVA: Committee on Natural Resources. H.R. 2795. A 
     bill to establish National Wildlife Corridors to provide for 
     the protection and restoration of certain native fish, 
     wildlife, and plant species, and for other purposes; with an 
     amendment (Rept. 116-558, Pt. 1). Referred to the Committee 
     of the Whole House on the state of the Union.
       Mr. GRIJALVA: Committee on Natural Resources. H.R. 5179. A 
     bill to require the Secretary of the Interior to establish 
     Tribal Wildlife Corridors, and for other purposes (Rept. 116-
     559, Pt. 1). Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on 
     the state of the Union.


                         discharge of committee

  Pursuant to clause 2 of rule XIII, the Committees on Agriculture, 
Armed Services, and Transportation and Infrastructure discharged from 
further consideration. H.R. 2795 referred to the Committee of the Whole 
House on the state of the Union.
  Pursuant to clause 2 of rule XIII, the Committee on Agriculture 
discharged from consideration. H.R. 5179 referred to the Committee of 
the Whole House on the state of the Union.
```
