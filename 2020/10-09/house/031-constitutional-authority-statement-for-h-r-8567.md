# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 8567

**Measures:** H.R. 8567

```
           By Mrs. MURPHY of Florida:
       H.R. 8567.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, which gives Congress the power to 
     provide for the general welfare of the United States; to 
     regulate commerce among the several states; and to make all 
     laws which shall be necessary and proper for carrying into 
     execution the foregoing powers.
```
