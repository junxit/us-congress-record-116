# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Patrick J. Conroy, offered the following 
prayer:
  Loving God, we give You thanks for giving us another day.
  In the waning days of this 116th Congress, we ask Your blessing upon 
the Members of this people's House and, most especially, upon the 
leadership. It is on their shoulders the most important negotiations of 
this Congress have been placed.
  They have been entrusted by their fellow Americans with the awesome 
privilege and responsibility of sustaining the great experiment of 
democratic self-government. Give them wisdom, grace, insight, and 
courage to forge legislation, especially during these trying times of 
coronavirus pandemic, that allows us all to move forward toward an 
encouraging future.
  May all that is done this day be for Your greater honor and glory.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
