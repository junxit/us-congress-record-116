# NORMAN DUNCAN POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                   NORMAN DUNCAN POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 4971) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 15 East Market Street in Leesburg, Virginia, 
as the ``Norman Duncan Post Office Building'' was ordered to a third
```
