# Vote on Waller Nomination (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Gardner, Cory (G000562) [R-CO], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                       Vote on Waller Nomination

  Under the previous order, all postcloture time has expired.
  The question is, Will the Senate advise and consent to the Waller 
nomination?
  Mr. GARDNER. Mr. President, I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk called the roll.
  Mr. THUNE. The following Senators are necessarily absent: the Senator 
from South Carolina (Mr. Graham), the Senator from Georgia (Mrs. 
Loeffler), and the Senator from Georgia (Mr. Perdue).
  Mr. DURBIN. I announce that the Senator from California (Ms. Harris) 
and the Senator from Vermont (Mr. Sanders) are necessarily absent.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mrs. Fischer). Are there any other Senators in 
the Chamber desiring to vote?
  The result was announced--yeas 48, nays 47, as follows:
```
