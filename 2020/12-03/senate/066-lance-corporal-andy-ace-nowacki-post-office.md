# LANCE CORPORAL ANDY `ACE' NOWACKI POST OFFICE

**Measures:** H.R. 3275

```
             LANCE CORPORAL ANDY `ACE' NOWACKI POST OFFICE

  A bill (H.R. 3275) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 340 Wetmore Avenue in Grand River, Ohio, as 
the ``Lance Corporal Andy `Ace' Nowacki Post Office'' was ordered to a 
third reading, was read the third time, and passed.
```
