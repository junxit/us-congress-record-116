# REVEREND CURTIS WEST HARRIS POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 3847

```
            REVEREND CURTIS WEST HARRIS POST OFFICE BUILDING

  A bill (H.R. 3847) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 117 West Poythress Street in Hopewell, 
Virginia, as the ``Reverend Curtis West Harris Post Office Building''
```
