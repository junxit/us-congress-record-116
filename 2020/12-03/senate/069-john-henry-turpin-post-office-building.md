# JOHN HENRY TURPIN POST OFFICE BUILDING

```
                 JOHN HENRY TURPIN POST OFFICE BUILDING

  A bill (H.R. 4034) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 602 Pacific Avenue in Bremerton, Washington, 
as the ``John Henry Turpin Post Office Building'' was ordered to a 
third reading, was read the third time, and passed.
```
