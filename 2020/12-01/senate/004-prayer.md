# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal Master, continue to be our shelter from life's storms and 
winds. You have provided us with Your presence and a reminder in 
Hebrews 13:5 that You will never leave or forsake us.
  May this knowledge of Your watch-care sustain our Senators through 
this challenging season. Grant that their work will become more 
meaningful and effective as they cultivate a greater awareness of Your 
divine companionship.
  We pray in Your loving Name. Amen.
```
