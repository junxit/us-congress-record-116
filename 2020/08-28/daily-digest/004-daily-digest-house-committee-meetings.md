# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
PROTECTING AMERICA'S DEMOCRACY: ENSURING EVERY VOTE COUNTS
Committee on Homeland Security: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``Protecting America's Democracy: Ensuring Every Vote Counts''. 
Testimony was heard from Jocelyn Benson, Secretary of State, Michigan; 
Michael Adams, Secretary of State, Kentucky; and public witnesses.
VOTING SAFELY IN A PANDEMIC
Committee on House Administration: Full Committee held a hearing 
entitled ``Voting Safely in a Pandemic''. Testimony was heard from Alex 
Padilla, Secretary of State, California; Donald L. Palmer, 
Commissioner, U.S. Election Assistance Commission; Julie Wise, Director 
of Elections, King County, Washington; and public witnesses.
COORDINATING TRANSITION RESOURCES
Committee on Veterans' Affairs: Subcommittee on Economic Opportunity 
held a hearing entitled ``Coordinating Transition Resources''. 
Testimony was heard from Ray Flores, Executive Director, San Diego 
Veterans Coalition, Office of Military and Veterans Affairs, Health and 
Human Services Agency, San Diego County, California; Brigadier General 
Paul Lebidine (Ret.), U.S. Marine Corps; and public witnesses.

Joint Meetings
  No joint committee meetings were held.
```
