# CELEBRATING THE FOUNDING OF COMMONWEALTH OF FAITH CHURCH

**Speaking:** Tlaib, Rashida (T000481) [D-MI]

```
        CELEBRATING THE FOUNDING OF COMMONWEALTH OF FAITH CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RASHIDA TLAIB

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, August 28, 2020

  Ms. TLAIB. Madam Speaker, I rise today in tribute to Nehemiah Word of 
Faith Full Gospel Baptist Church and its partner World Harvest United 
Churches for their work to bring resources to the families of Wayne 
County, Michigan.
  Under the leadership of Presiding Bishop Calvin B. Davis, Sr., Bishop 
George W. Thigpen III, Bishop J. V. Williams, Bishop Kenneth W. Jones, 
Sr., Pastor Rachelle Gibson, Pastor Jackie Taylor, and Pastor Kathy 
Deane, faith-based organizations will converge with the goal of sharing 
meals, supplies, and fellowship to our most vulnerable populations. 
During this time of unprecedented challenges, this group, united in 
their faith, are taking up the mantle in service to others. More than 
providing comforts of meal, this day of service brings a greater sense 
of kinship and spirituality.
  Please join me in recognizing Nehemiah Word of Faith Full Gospel 
Baptist Church and World Harvest United Church's day of community 
service.

                          ____________________
```
