# RECOGNIZING THE TURKISH AMERICAN NATIONAL STEERING COMMITTEE (TASC)

**Speaking:** Trone, David J. (T000483) [D-MD]

```
  RECOGNIZING THE TURKISH AMERICAN NATIONAL STEERING COMMITTEE (TASC)

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. DAVID J. TRONE

                              of maryland

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, August 28, 2020

  Mr. TRONE. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Turkish 
American National Steering Committee (TASC), which is lead by Potomac 
resident Gunay Evinch, and the Turkish Exporters Assembly, which is 
lead by Mr. Ismail Gulle, for their humanitarianism and generosity in 
donating critical personal protective equipment (PPE) to the State of 
Maryland. On July 20, these two organizations delivered more than 
100,000 surgical masks and 1,000 surgical suits to the Maryland Office 
of the Secretary of State.

[[Page E799]]

  Along with other residents of Maryland's Sixth District, I am 
grateful for the generosity of Turkish Americans and their gracious 
support to our fellow Marylanders. These PPE deliveries complement 
shipments made by the Turkish Republic, which sent two military cargo 
plane loads of PPE to the United States, including surgical masks, 
surgical suits, disinfectant, goggles, N-95 masks, and face shields. 
Our frontline healthcare workforce has been heroic throughout this 
pandemic, and I am heartened to see contributions like those made by 
the Turkish American community.
  As TASC says, ``Be Negative on COVID, Stay Positive on Life.''

                          ____________________
```
