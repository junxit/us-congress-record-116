# HONORING THE LIFE OF MARIA McENEANY

**Speaking:** Levin, Mike (L000593) [D-CA]

```
                  HONORING THE LIFE OF MARIA McENEANY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE LEVIN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, August 28, 2020

  Mr. LEVIN of California. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor the 
life of Maria Desiderio McEneany, a constituent of the 49th 
Congressional District who recently passed away.
  Maria spent her life in service to her family and community. With a 
ready smile and a helping hand, Maria was always ready to use her 
talent, knowledge, and experience to assist others. Often with her 
husband Paul at her side, Maria organized events including weddings at 
St. James Church and fundraisers for her favorite causes.
  Maria's heart was large enough to embrace causes from Jubilee USA to 
fight for international debt relief to the San Diego County Commission 
on the Status of Women and Girls where she elevated her voice to fight 
the scourge of human trafficking and promote opportunity and equity.
  Maria will long be remembered as a long-serving executive board 
member of the Rancho Santa Fe Democratic Club where her welcoming 
spirit, collaborative style, and hard work contributed to the growth 
and success of the club. She also served as a member of the San Diego 
County Democratic Party Central Committee and North Area Caucus at a 
pivotal time when the changing political landscape led to the election 
of many of the candidates who she worked tirelessly to elect.
  Maria will long be remembered and loved by all those whose life she 
touched.

                          ____________________
```
