# HONORING RODERICK BRADFORD

**Speaking:** Thompson, Bennie G. (T000193) [D-MS]

```
                       HONORING RODERICK BRADFORD

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 12, 2020

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor a 
tenacious and ambitious man, Mr. Roderick Bradford. Roderick has shown 
what can be done through hard work, dedication and a desire to serve 
others.
  Roderick is a graduate of Grambling State University, where he earned 
a Bachelor of Science degree in Management in 1999.
  He has more than 20 years of management experience and seven-plus 
years of specialized experience in the financial services industry, 
after working for both Capital One and Chase Home Mortgage.
  Roderick serves in the Men's Ministry at Calvary Baptist Church and 
is an active member of the Omega Psi Phi Fraternity, Inc. Roderick is a 
veteran of the United States Air Force, where he served his country 
from 1989 to 1991.
  Madam Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Mr. 
Roderick Bradford for his passion and dedication to serving Warren 
County and desire to make a difference in the community.

                          ____________________
```
