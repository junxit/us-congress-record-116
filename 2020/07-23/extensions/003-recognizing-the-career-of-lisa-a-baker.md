# RECOGNIZING THE CAREER OF LISA A. BAKER

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

```
                RECOGNIZING THE CAREER OF LISA A. BAKER

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 23, 2020

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Madam Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Lisa A. 
Baker, Chief Executive Officer of Yolo County Housing on her retirement 
from the agency after 14 successful years. While Ms. Baker was with 
Yolo Housing, the agency was twice named HUD's PHA of the Year, added 
264 affordable partnership units, 104 affordable managed units, 400 
voucher units, received 23 awards from HUD, and nearly doubled their 
annual revenue. It has been my pleasure to work with Ms Baker 
throughout her tenure at Yolo Housing, and I look forward to seeing her 
next chapter in life.
```
