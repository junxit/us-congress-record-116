# CORRECTING THE ENROLLMENT OF S. 3312

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 52, S. 3312

```
                  CORRECTING THE ENROLLMENT OF S. 3312

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask the Chair to lay before the 
Senate the House message to accompany S. Con. Res. 52.
  The Presiding Officer laid before the Senate the following message 
from the House of Representatives:

       Resolved, That the resolution from the Senate (S. Con. Res. 
     52) entitled ``Concurrent resolution to correct the 
     enrollment of S. 3312.'', do pass with an amendment.


                            Motion to Concur

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I move to concur in the House amendment 
and I ask unanimous consent that the motion be agreed to, and the 
motion to reconsider be considered made and laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
