# IN HONOR OF LIEUTENANT COLONEL MAYNARD H. GRAY

**Speaking:** Joyce, John (J000302) [R-PA]

```
             IN HONOR OF LIEUTENANT COLONEL MAYNARD H. GRAY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOHN JOYCE

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, December 18, 2020

  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Madam Speaker, I rise to congratulate 
Lieutenant Colonel Maynard H. Gray of the Pennsylvania State Police on 
his upcoming retirement on January 8, 2021.
  Lieutenant Colonel Gray joined the Pennsylvania State Police in 1984. 
After graduating from the academy, his first assignment was based in 
Lykens, Pennsylvania. During his career, he also has served in 
Philipsburg, McConnellsburg, Philadelphia, Clearfield, Harrisburg, and 
Hollidaysburg. In Hollidaysburg, he served as the troop commander of 
Troop G. Additionally, he also has served in the Systems and Process 
Review Division in the Bureau of Integrity and Professional Standards 
and on the Bureau of Gaming Enforcement.
  He had served as a corporal, a sergeant, a lieutenant, a captain, and 
a major before being promoted to a lieutenant colonel and the Deputy 
Commissioner of Staff in February of 2020. He also graduated from the 
231st Session of the FBI National Academy in 2007.
  Lieutenant Colonel Gray and his wife Susan have six children, and 
three of them followed in his footsteps to join the Pennsylvania State 
Police. On behalf of Pennsylvania's 13th Congressional District, I 
thank Lieutenant Colonel Gray for his service to our Commonwealth, and 
I wish the Gray family every continued success.

                          ____________________
```
