# NEVAEH GROSSMAN

**Speaking:** Perlmutter, Ed (P000593) [D-CO]

```
                            NEVAEH GROSSMAN

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ED PERLMUTTER

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, April 17, 2020

  Mr. PERLMUTTER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize and applaud 
Nevaeh Grossman for receiving the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service 
Ambassadors for Youth award.
  Nevaeh Grossman is a student at Wayne Carle Middle School and 
received this award

[[Page E371]]

because her determination and hard work have allowed her to overcome 
adversities.
  The dedication demonstrated by Nevaeh Grossman is exemplary of the 
type of achievement that can be attained with hard work and 
perseverance. It is essential students at all levels strive to make the 
most of their education and develop a work ethic which will guide them 
for the rest of their lives.
  I extend my deepest congratulations to Nevaeh Grossman for winning 
the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassador for Youth award. I have no 
doubt she will exhibit the same dedication and character in all of her 
future accomplishments.

                          ____________________
```
