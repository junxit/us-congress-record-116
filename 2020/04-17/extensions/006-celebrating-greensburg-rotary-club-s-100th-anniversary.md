# CELEBRATING GREENSBURG ROTARY CLUB'S 100TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Pence, Greg (P000615) [R-IN]

```
         CELEBRATING GREENSBURG ROTARY CLUB'S 100TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. GREG PENCE

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, April 17, 2020

  Mr. PENCE. Madam Speaker, I would like to congratulate the Greensburg 
Rotary Club for celebrating 100 years of service.
  For a century now, the Greensburg Rotary Club has been a beacon of 
service in the Greensburg and Decatur County community.
  The Rotary Club continues to raise funds for the Decatur County 
Special Olympics and supports local service projects around the 
community.
  I want to thank the members of the Rotary Club for their service and 
dedication to the people of Greensburg and congratulate them on their 
100th anniversary.

                          ____________________
```
