# RECOGNIZING THE OUTSTANDING ACCOMPLISHMENTS OF MR. TRUC HO

**Speaking:** Rouda, Harley (R000616) [D-CA]

```
       RECOGNIZING THE OUTSTANDING ACCOMPLISHMENTS OF MR. TRUC HO

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. HARLEY ROUDA

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, April 17, 2020

  Mr. ROUDA. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize the remarkable 
accomplishments of Mr. Truc Ho. Mr. Ho is the General Director of SBTN, 
the first and largest Vietnamese 24-hour television network in the 
United States.
  Mr. Ho is a prominent musician and media activist within the 
Vietnamese American community. His tireless dedication to his 
community, and his tremendous work with SBTN musicians, has culminated 
in what many would consider the perfect rendition of Vietnamese 
culture. In conjunction with SBTN, Mr. Ho produced a music DVD, ``The 
Forsaken Soldiers,'' honoring the soldiers of The Army of the Republic 
of Vietnam and commemorating the 45th Anniversary of Black April.
  His passionate and inspirational leadership will forever cement Mr. 
Truc Ho as a hero and champion of Vietnamese culture, both within the 
Vietnamese American community and abroad.
  I ask that all Members join me in honoring the extraordinary 
accomplishments of Mr. Truc Ho.
```
