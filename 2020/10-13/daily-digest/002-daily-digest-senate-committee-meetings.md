# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet.)
NOMINATION
Committee on the Judiciary: On Monday, October 12, 2020, Committee held 
hearings to examine the nomination of Amy Coney Barrett, of Indiana, to 
be an Associate Justice of the Supreme Court of the United States, the 
nominee, who was introduced by Senators Young and Braun, and Patricia 
O'Hara, Notre Dame Law School, Notre Dame, Indiana, testified in her 
own behalf.
  Hearings recessed subject to the call will meet again on Tuesday, 
October 13, 2020.
NOMINATION
Committee on the Judiciary: Committee continued hearings to examine the 
nomination of Amy Coney Barrett, of Indiana, to be an Associate Justice 
of the Supreme Court of the United States, and the nominee testified 
and answered questions in her own behalf.
  Hearings recessed subject to the call will meet again on Wednesday, 
October 14, 2020.
```
