# SENATE RESOLUTION 801--AUTHORIZING THE USE OF THE ATRIUM IN THE PHILIP A. HART SENATE OFFICE BUILDING FOR A PIANO PERFORMANCE BY SENATOR LAMAR ALEXANDER

**Measures:** S.Res. 801

```
                         SUBMITTED RESOLUTIONS

                                 ______
                                 

 SENATE RESOLUTION 801--AUTHORIZING THE USE OF THE ATRIUM IN THE PHILIP 
A. HART SENATE OFFICE BUILDING FOR A PIANO PERFORMANCE BY SENATOR LAMAR 
                               ALEXANDER

  Mr. BLUNT submitted the following resolution; which was considered 
and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 801

       Resolved, That the atrium in the Philip A. Hart Senate 
     Office Building is authorized to be used from 3:30 p.m. until 
     6:30 p.m. on one day during December 14, 2020 through 
     December 18, 2020, for a piano performance by Senator Lamar 
     Alexander.
```
