# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 1548

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 10:14 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Novotny, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bill, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 1548. An act for the relief of Maria Carmen Castro 
     Ramirez and J. Refugio Carreno Rojas.

  The message further announced that pursuant to section 732(a) of the 
United States-Mexico-Canada Agreement Implementation Act (Public Law 
116-113), and the order of the House of January 3, 2019, the Speaker 
appoints the following individuals on the part of the House of 
Representatives to the Independent Mexico Labor Expert Board for a term 
of 6 years: Ms. Catherine Feingold of Takoma Park, Maryland and Mr. 
Frederick Gibson Ross of Berkeley, California.

                          ____________________
```
