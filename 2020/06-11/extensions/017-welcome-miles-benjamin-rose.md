# WELCOME MILES BENJAMIN ROSE

**Speaking:** Stevens, Haley M. (S001215) [D-MI]

```
                      WELCOME MILES BENJAMIN ROSE

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. HALEY M. STEVENS

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 11, 2020

  Ms. STEVENS. Madam Speaker, I am happy to congratulate the Honorable 
Max Rose and his wife Leigh on the arrival of their new baby boy, Miles 
Benjamin Rose. Miles Benjamin Rose was born on March 14, 2020, weighing 
seven pounds and seven ounces and measuring in at 21 inches long.
  I would also like to congratulate Miles' grandparents, Lisa and Hal 
Rose, Luke Byrne and Carol Westermann, and Dr. Linda Marsanico. 
Congratulations to the entire family as they welcome their newest 
addition of pure pride and joy.
```
