# WELCOME KITTRIDGE CHARLES & ADLER ELNORA LOPATA-PAGAN

**Speaking:** Stevens, Haley M. (S001215) [D-MI]

```
         WELCOME KITTRIDGE CHARLES & ADLER ELNORA LOPATA-PAGAN

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. HALEY M. STEVENS

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 11, 2020

  Ms. STEVENS. Madam Speaker, I am happy to congratulate State 
Representative Kristy Pagan and Karl Lopata of Canton, Michigan, on the 
birth of their babies, Kittridge Charles and Adler Elnora Lopata-Pagan. 
Kittridge Charles was born on Martin Luther King Jr. Day on January 20, 
2020, weighing six pounds and fourteen ounces and measuring in at 19. 7 
inches long. Adler Elnora was born on January 21, 2020, weighing five 
pounds and twelve ounces and measuring in at 18.9 inches long. 
Kittridge and Adler were born at Von Voigtlander Women's Hospital at 
the University of Michigan.
  I would also like to congratulate Kittridge and Adler's grandparents, 
Nick and Kathy Pagan of Plymouth, Michigan, and Karl and Judy Lopata of 
Canton, Michigan, as well as aunts and uncles Jenna and Bill Pagan-
DeYoung of Kalamazoo, Michigan (cousin Evelyn Pagan-DeYoung, cousin 
Elliot Pagan-DeYoung), Kimberly Pagan of Arlington, Virginia, and K.C. 
Lopata of Ann Arbor, Michigan. Congratulations to the entire family as 
they welcome the new loves and lights of their lives.

                          ____________________
```
