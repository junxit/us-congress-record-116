# WELCOME JULIAN WINTER GERMAN

**Speaking:** Stevens, Haley M. (S001215) [D-MI]

```
                      WELCOME JULIAN WINTER GERMAN

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. HALEY M. STEVENS

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 11, 2020

  Ms. STEVENS. Madam Speaker, I am happy to congratulate Aaron and 
Kristi German on the birth of their new baby boy, Julian Winter German. 
Julian Winter German was born on May 9, 2020, at Sentara RMH Medical 
Center in Harrisonburg, Virginia. Julian weighed eight pounds and three 
ounces and measured 20 inches long.
  I would also like to congratulate Julian's grandparents, John and 
Barbara German of Harrisonburg, Virginia, and Montie and Karen 
Armentrout of Harrisonburg, Virginia. Congratulations to the entire 
family as they welcome their newest addition of pure pride and joy.

                          ____________________
```
