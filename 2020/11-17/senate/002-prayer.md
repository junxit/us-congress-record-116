# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Mighty God, help our lawmakers to not put their complete trust in 
mere humans, who are as frail as each breath. Instead, give them the 
wisdom to totally trust You to guide them in doing what is best for our 
Nation and world.
  Lord, remind them that eventually all will be well for those who 
pursue Godliness and that You reward those who diligently seek You.
  May they remember that You will judge their work and reward their 
faithfulness, for they are accountable to You.
  Inspire them to continue to plant and water the seeds of peace, 
knowing that a bountiful harvest will surely come. Keep their motives 
pure, as they seek to glorify You.
  We pray in Your Merciful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
