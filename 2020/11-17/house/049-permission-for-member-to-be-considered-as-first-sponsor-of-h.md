# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 4292

**Speaking:** Rose, John W. (R000612) [R-TN]

**Measures:** H.R. 4292

```
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 4292

  Mr. JOHN W. ROSE of Tennessee. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent 
that I may hereafter be considered to be the first sponsor of H.R. 
4292, a bill originally introduced by Representative Mark Meadows of 
North Carolina, for the purpose of adding cosponsors and requesting 
reprintings pursuant to clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mrs. Trahan). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Tennessee?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
