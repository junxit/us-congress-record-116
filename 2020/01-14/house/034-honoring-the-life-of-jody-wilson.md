# HONORING THE LIFE OF JODY WILSON

**Speaking:** O'Halleran, Tom (O000171) [D-AZ]

```
                              {time}  1400
                    HONORING THE LIFE OF JODY WILSON

  (Mr. O'HALLERAN asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute.)
  Mr. O'HALLERAN. Madam Speaker, I rise today to pay my respects to 
Jody Wilson, a veteran who passed away last month.
  Jody was dearly loved by her friends, family, and community. She 
served bravely in the United States Army for 9 years.
  After her military service, Jody served her community as a clerk, a 
nurse, and later as a letter carrier for the United States Postal 
Service. Her kindness, sense of humor, and infectious smile were 
sources of joy for those who knew and loved her.
  Arizona has lost an incredible community member, veteran, and public 
servant.
  Pat and I are keeping Jody's family, friends, and community in our 
prayers as we mourn her passing.

                          ____________________
```
