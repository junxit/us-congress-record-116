# DAVID PINALES

**Speaking:** Perlmutter, Ed (P000593) [D-CO]

```
                             DAVID PINALES

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ED PERLMUTTER

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, April 24, 2020

  Mr. PERLMUTTER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize and applaud 
David Pinales for receiving the Adams County Mayors and Commissioners 
Youth Award.
  David Pinales is a 10th grader at Bollman Technical Education Center/
Legacy High School and received this award because his determination 
and hard work have allowed him to overcome adversities.
  The dedication demonstrated by David Pinales is exemplary of the type 
of achievement that can be attained with hard work and perseverance. It 
is essential students at all levels strive to make the most of their 
education and develop a work ethic which will guide them for the rest 
of their lives.
  I extend my deepest congratulations to David Pinales for winning the 
Adams County Mayors and Commissioners Youth Award. I have no doubt he 
will exhibit the same dedication and character in all of his future 
accomplishments.
```
