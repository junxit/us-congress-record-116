# ISABELLA SOLIS

**Speaking:** Perlmutter, Ed (P000593) [D-CO]

```
                             ISABELLA SOLIS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ED PERLMUTTER

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, April 24, 2020

  Mr. PERLMUTTER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize and applaud 
Isabella Solis for receiving the Adams County Mayors and Commissioners 
Youth Award.
  Isabella Solis is a 10th grader at Bollman Technical Education Center 
and received this award because her determination and hard work have 
allowed her to overcome adversities.
  The dedication demonstrated by Isabella Solis is exemplary of the 
type of achievement that can be attained with hard work and 
perseverance. It is essential students at all levels strive to make the 
most of their education and develop a work ethic which will guide them 
for the rest of their lives.
  I extend my deepest congratulations to Isabella Solis for winning the 
Adams County Mayors and Commissioners Youth Award. I have no doubt she 
will exhibit the same dedication and character in all of her future 
accomplishments.

                          ____________________
```
