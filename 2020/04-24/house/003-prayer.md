# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Patrick J. Conroy, offered the following 
prayer:
  Compassionate God, thank You for giving us another day.
  The House is again silent, and all the Nation awaits the influx of 
assistance on the local level to address the ongoing pandemic.
  Please empower and inspire all people of good will who are now 
responsible to ensure that assistance is distributed most effectively 
and equitably.
  Continue to bless those whose life's work is in service to public 
safety, be it police, medical professionals, or those producing and 
delivering our daily necessities.
  May everything done this day be for Your greater honor and glory.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
