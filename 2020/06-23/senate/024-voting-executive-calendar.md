# Voting (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Klobuchar, Amy (K000367) [D-MN], Wyden, Ron (W000779) [D-OR]

```
                                 Voting

  Ms. KLOBUCHAR. Mr. President, I rise today and thank my colleague 
from Oregon and also thank him for his work on the voting bill. We just 
had a discussion with Senator Blunt about that, and while we didn't 
agree on the bill, there is a discussion that is ongoing--as we head 
into another COVID package, possibly, which we really believe we need 
to--on working on some funding issues for the States as we look at more 
and more balloting from home and how important that is.
  I want to thank Senator Wyden for his leadership for so long on that 
issue. I brought up his home State in a speech about an hour or so ago 
and the work that Oregon has done with ballot-by-home and, I think, 
ballots from home.
  I actually think I used the number of percentage of fraud as 
0.0000001, or something like that, to show that what the President said 
is not true; that we have had, actually, in many States across the 
country--including Utah, a redder State--a big success with voting from 
home.
```
