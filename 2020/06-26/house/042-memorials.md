# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** S.Res. 122

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       176. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the Senate of the 
     State of Michigan, relative to Senate Resolution No. 122, to 
     unequivocally condemn and denounce the violent actions of 
     extremist organizations as unacceptable and to memorialize 
     the Congress of the United States to redouble its efforts, 
     using all available and appropriate tools, to combat the 
     spread of all forms of domestic terrorism; which was referred 
     to the Committee on the Judiciary.
```
