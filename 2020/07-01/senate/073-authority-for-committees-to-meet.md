# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Inhofe, James M. (I000024) [R-OK]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

  Mr. INHOFE. Mr. President, I have 7 requests for committees to meet 
during today's session of the Senate. They have the approval of the 
Majority and Minority leaders.

Senate, the following committees are authorized to meet during today's 
session of the Senate:


                      Committee on Armed Services

  The Committee on Armed Services is authorized to meet during the

conduct a hearing.

to meet during the session of the Senate on Wednesday, July 1, 2020, at 
10 a.m., to conduct a hearing.


               Committee on Environment and Public Works

during the session of the Senate on Wednesday, July 1, 2020, at 9:45 
a.m., to conduct a hearing on nominations.

                     Committee on Veterans' Affairs
```
