# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JULY 2, 2020

**Speaking:** Inhofe, James M. (I000024) [R-OK]

```
                   ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JULY 2, 2020

  Mr. INHOFE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m., 
Thursday, July 2; further, that following the prayer and the pledge, 
the morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their 
use later in the day and morning business be closed; further, that 
following leader remarks, the Senate proceed to executive session to 
resume consideration of Calendar No. 718; finally, that notwithstanding 
rule XXII, the cloture vote on the Vought nomination occur at 1:30 p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
