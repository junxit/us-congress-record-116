# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Patrick J. Conroy, offered the following 
prayer:
  Loving God, thank You for giving us another day.
  We ask Your continued protection and empowerment of those who tend to 
the sick and those who seek medical solutions to this dangerous 
pandemic.
  We thank You for the work of the House these last few days to bring 
to votes support for the Department of Agriculture. During these times, 
food production, delivery, and access have been among the many 
challenges facing our Nation.
  Whatever the particulars of this legislation, may it proceed through 
the legislative process and ultimately redound to the benefit of all 
Americans.
  We ask also Your continued protection and empowerment for those who 
live heroic lives during this time. Give them strength and perseverance 
in their service.
  May all that is done this day be for Your greater honor and glory.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
