# WITHDRAWALS

```
                              WITHDRAWALS

  Executive Message transmitted by the President to the Senate on 
August 3, 2020 withdrawing from further Senate consideration the 
following nominations:

       Michael P, O'Rielly, of New York, to be a Member of the 
     Federal Communications Commission for a term of five years 
     from July 1, 2019 (Reappointment), which was sent to the 
     Senate on March 18, 2020.
       Anthony J. Tata, of Virginia, to be Under Secretary of 
     Defense for Policy, Vice John C. Rood, resigned, which was 
     sent to the Senate on June 11, 2020
```
