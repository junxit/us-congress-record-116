# Daily Digest/NEW PUBLIC LAWS

**Measures:** H.R. 263, H.R. 434, S. 457

```
                            NEW PUBLIC LAWS

      (For last listing of Public Laws, see Daily Digest, p. D82)
  H.R. 263, to rename the Oyster Bay National Wildlife Refuge as the 
Congressman Lester Wolff Oyster Bay National Wildlife Refuge. Signed on 
January 27, 2020. (Public Law 116-110)
  H.R. 434, to amend the National Trails System Act to provide for the 
study of the Emancipation National Historic Trail. Signed on January 
27, 2020. (Public Law 116-111)
  S. 457, to require that $1 coins issued during 2019 honor President 
George H. W. Bush and to direct the Secretary of the Treasury to issue 
bullion coins during 2019 in honor of Barbara Bush. Signed on January 
27, 2020. (Public Law 116-112)
```
