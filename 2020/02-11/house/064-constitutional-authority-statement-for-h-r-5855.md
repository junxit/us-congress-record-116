# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 5855

**Measures:** H.R. 5855

```
            By Mr. RUPPERSBERGER:
        H.R. 5855.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       According to Article I, Section 8, Clause 18 of the 
     Constitution, Congress has the power to institute legislation 
     which is necessary and proper for the protection of the 
     American people.
```
