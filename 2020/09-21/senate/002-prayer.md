# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, who has given us the gift of life, consecrate with Your 
presence the way our lawmakers work today. Since they don't know what a 
day will bring, help them to strive to serve You in faithfulness each 
moment. In all things, draw their minds to the goal of seeking to 
please You. As they draw near to You, illuminate their paths with Your 
wisdom and grace. Lord, show them how to unselfishly serve Your great 
purposes for humanity, proving themselves worthy of Your manifold 
blessings.
  And, Lord, as millions mourn Supreme Court Justice Ruth Bader 
Ginsburg's death, send the solace of Your comfort.
  We pray in Your unifying Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
