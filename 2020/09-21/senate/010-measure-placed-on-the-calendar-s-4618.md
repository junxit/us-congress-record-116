# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 4618

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY], Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

**Measures:** S. 4618

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 4618

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I understand there is a bill at the 
desk due a second reading.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will read bill by title 
for the second time.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 4618) making emergency supplemental 
     appropriations for disaster relief for the fiscal year ending 
     September 30, 2020, and for other purposes.

  Mr. McCONNELL. In order to place the bill on the calendar under the 
provisions of rule XIV, I would object to further proceedings.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Objection having been heard, the 
bill will be placed on the calendar.
  Mr. McCONNELL. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will call the roll.
  The bill clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
