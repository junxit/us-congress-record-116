# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MARCH 3, 2020

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** S. 2657

```
                   ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MARCH 3, 2020

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m., Tuesday, 
March 3; further, that following the prayer and pledge, the morning 
hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and morning business be closed; further, that following leader 
remarks, the Senate resume consideration of the motion to proceed to S. 
2657 and that the Senate recess from 12:30 to 2:15 p.m. for the weekly 
conference meetings; finally, that notwithstanding rule XXII, all time 
during recess, adjournment, morning business, and leader remarks count 
postcloture on the motion to proceed to S. 2657.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
