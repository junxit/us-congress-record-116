# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, petitions and papers were laid on the 
clerk's desk and referred as follows:

       85. The SPEAKER presented a petition of the Council of the 
     District of Columbia, relative to Council Resolution 23-342

     Resources.
       86. Also, a petition of the Hammond, Indiana Common 
     Council, relative to Resolution No. R34, in support of 
     deferred action for childhood arrivals (DACA) and the Dream 
     Act; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
```
