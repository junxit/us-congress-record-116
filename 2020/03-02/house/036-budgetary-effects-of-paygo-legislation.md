# BUDGETARY EFFECTS OF PAYGO LEGISLATION

**Measures:** H.R. 5003

```
                 BUDGETARY EFFECTS OF PAYGO LEGISLATION

  Pursuant to the Statutory Pay-As-You Act of 2010 (PAYGO), Mr. Yarmuth 
hererby submits, prior to the vote on passage, for printing in the 
Congressional Record, that H.R. 5003, the Fair Debt Collection 
Practices for servicemembers Act, as amended, would have no significant 
effect on the deficit, and therefore, the budgetary effects of such 
bill are estimated as zero.
```
