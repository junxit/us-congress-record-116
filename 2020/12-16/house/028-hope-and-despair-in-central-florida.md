# HOPE AND DESPAIR IN CENTRAL FLORIDA

**Speaking:** Soto, Darren (S001200) [D-FL]

```
                  HOPE AND DESPAIR IN CENTRAL FLORIDA

  (Mr. SOTO asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, there is a mix of hope and despair in central 
Florida.
  This week, the first vaccines arrived to help protect our healthcare 
professionals. But unemployment remains around 10 percent; food banks 
lay bare; many are behind on rent; and coronavirus cases continue to 
rise.
  The message is clear in both Florida and across our Nation: Americans 
need help.
  It is time for this Congress to come together and pass meaningful 
coronavirus emergency relief to defeat the virus; to help small 
businesses and the unemployed; and to ensure food, housing, and direct 
stimulus checks for our constituents.
  The solution is within our grasp. We just need to come together this 
week to get the job done.
  Mr. Speaker, I urge all of my colleagues: Don't be an Ebenezer 
Scrooge. Embrace the Christmas spirit. Join me and vote ``yes.''

                          ____________________
```
