# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Patrick J. Conroy, offered the following 
prayer:
  Loving God, we thank You for giving us another day.
  Please send Your spirit upon all those in elected leadership in our 
Nation, most especially the Members of the people's House, as they 
listen to the voices and concerns of their constituents at home.
  Our citizens continue to struggle with the threat of the coronavirus 
and the economic difficulties that have resulted throughout our 
communities. Pour forth Your wisdom upon all who are chosen to care for 
the common welfare of our Nation.
  May all that is done be for Your greater honor and glory.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
