# DIRECTING THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES TO MAKE A CORRECTION IN THE ENROLLMENT OF H.R. 1520

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 128, H.R. 1520

```
     DIRECTING THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES TO MAKE A 
               CORRECTION IN THE ENROLLMENT OF H.R. 1520

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of H. Con. Res. 128, which was 
received from the House.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the concurrent 
resolution by title.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 128) directing the 
     clerk of the House of Representatives to make a correction in 
     the enrollment of H.R. 1520.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
concurrent resolution.
  Mr. McCONNELL. I ask unanimous consent that the resolution be agreed 
to and that the motion to reconsider be considered made and laid upon 
the table with no intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 128) was agreed to.

                          ____________________
```
