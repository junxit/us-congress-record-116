# SIGNING AUTHORITY

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD], Boozman, John (B001236) [R-AR]

```
                           SIGNING AUTHORITY

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the senior 
Senator from South Dakota, the senior Senator from Kansas, and the 
senior Senator from Missouri be authorized to sign duly enrolled bills 
and joint resolutions from December 21, 2020, to January 3, 2021.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. BOOZMAN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
