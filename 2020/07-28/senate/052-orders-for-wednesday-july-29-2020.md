# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JULY 29, 2020

**Speaking:** Cramer, Kevin (C001096) [R-ND]

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JULY 29, 2020

  Mr. CRAMER. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m., 
Wednesday, July 29; further, that following the prayer and pledge, the 
morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved 
to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day and morning business be closed; finally, that following 
leader remarks, the Senate proceed to executive session to resume 
consideration of the Kan nomination under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
