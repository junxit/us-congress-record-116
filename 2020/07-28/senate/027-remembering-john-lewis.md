# REMEMBERING JOHN LEWIS

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                         REMEMBERING JOHN LEWIS

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, yesterday, I was privileged to be able to 
pay my respects to Representative John Lewis as he lay in state in the 
Capitol Rotunda--a fitting place for an American hero.
  His death is a loss, but his dauntless courage and deep conviction 
have carved out for him a permanent place in American history. When we 
tell stories of those who have made America greater, John Lewis's name 
will always be among them.

                          ____________________
```
