# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 1 P.M. TOMORROW

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                   ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 1 P.M. TOMORROW

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. Chief Justice, I ask unanimous consent that the 
trial adjourn until 1 p.m., Tuesday, January 28, and that this order 
also constitute the adjournment of the Senate.
  There being no objection, at 9:02 p.m. the Senate, sitting as a Court 
of Impeachment, adjourned until Tuesday, January 28, 2020, at 1 p.m.
```
