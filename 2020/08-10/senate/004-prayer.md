# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, who rules the raging of the sea, we seek Your face in 
these turbulent times.
  Lord, help us to so live that the generations to come will know of 
Your mighty acts.
  Today, give our lawmakers the hearts to seek, find, and follow Your 
will so that their legacy will be exemplary. Guide them in the path You 
have created, inspiring them with the potency of Your powerful 
presence. May they trust Your laws of sowing and reaping, knowing that 
they will receive a bountiful harvest if they persevere. Keep them from 
underestimating the power of Your great Name.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
