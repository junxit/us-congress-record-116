# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, You are our rock and fortress. Keep us from dishonor. 
Only by walking in Your precepts can our lawmakers remain within the 
circle of Your protection and blessings. Lord, turn their ears to 
listen to Your admonition, as You infuse them with the courage to obey 
Your commands. We have trusted You since the birth of this land we 
love. That is why we will declare Your glory as long as we have breath. 
Lord, as our Senators prepare to gather for today's impeachment trial, 
we declare that You alone are our hope.
  We pray in Your mighty Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
