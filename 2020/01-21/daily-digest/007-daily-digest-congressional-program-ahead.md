# Daily Digest/CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

```
                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

              Week of January 22 through January 24, 2020

                             Senate Chamber

  During the balance of the week, Senate expects to continue 
consideration of the articles of impeachment against President Trump.


                           Senate Committees

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)
  Committee on Commerce, Science, and Transportation: January 22, to 
hold hearings to examine the 5G workforce and obstacles to broadband 
deployment, 10 a.m., SH-216.
  Committee on Environment and Public Works: January 22, to hold an 
oversight hearing to examine the Economic Development 
Administration, 10 a.m., SD-406.
  Select Committee on Intelligence: January 22, to receive a closed 
briefing on certain intelligence matters, 10 a.m., SH-219.


                            House Committees

  No hearings are scheduled.
```
