# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 6228

**Measures:** H.R. 6228

```
           By Mr. AMODEI:
       H.R. 6228.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority on which this bill rests is 
     the power of Congress to make rules for the government and 
     regulation of land and naval forces, as enumerated in Article 
     I, Section 8, Clause 14 of the United States Constitution.
```
