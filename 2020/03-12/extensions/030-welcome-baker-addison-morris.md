# WELCOME BAKER ADDISON MORRIS

**Speaking:** Wilson, Joe (W000795) [R-SC]

```
                      WELCOME BAKER ADDISON MORRIS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOE WILSON

                           of south carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, March 12, 2020

  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Madam Speaker, I am happy to 
congratulate Alexander Fancourt Morris and Rebekah Livingston Morris, 
of Gaston, South Carolina, on the birth of their new baby boy, Baker 
Addison Morris. Baker was born at 4:48 a.m. on Tuesday, March 10, 2020, 
at Lexington Medical Center in West Columbia, South Carolina. He 
weighed eight pounds and fifteen ounces and measures twenty-one inches 
long.
  Congratulations to his grandparents Debra Morris of Columbia, and 
Major General (Ret) Robert Livingston and Barbara Livingston of Gaston. 
He will join his older brother, Alexander Fancourt Morris, Jr.

                          ____________________
```
