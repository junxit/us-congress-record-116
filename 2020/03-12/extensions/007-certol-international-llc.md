# CERTOL INTERNATIONAL LLC

**Speaking:** Perlmutter, Ed (P000593) [D-CO]

```
                        CERTOL INTERNATIONAL LLC

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ED PERLMUTTER

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, March 12, 2020

  Mr. PERLMUTTER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize and applaud 
Certol International LLC for receiving Commerce City's Business 
Appreciation 2020 Business on the Move Award.
   The Business on the Move Award recognizes businesses bringing new 
employment, growth in sales or new capital investment to the city in 
the last year. Founded in 1979, and located in Commerce City since 
2003, Certol International was an early pioneer in the manufacturing of 
infection prevention products for the medical and dental markets. 
Creating innovative products in these markets remains a core competency 
today. The company works with distribution partners to ship to 
customers in national and international destinations.
   Certol International operates out of a 46,000 square-foot building 
in Commerce City with a staff of 38. In 2019, they hired two more 
employees and contributed over a million dollars in capital investment.
   I extend my deepest congratulations to Certol International LLC for 
this well-deserved recognition from Commerce City.
```
