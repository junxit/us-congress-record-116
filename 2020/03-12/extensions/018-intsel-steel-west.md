# INTSEL STEEL WEST

**Speaking:** Perlmutter, Ed (P000593) [D-CO]

```
                           INTSEL STEEL WEST

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ED PERLMUTTER

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, March 12, 2020

  Mr. PERLMUTTER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize and applaud 
Intsel Steel West for receiving Commerce City's Business Appreciation 
2020 Economic Development Award for Leadership.
   The Economic Development Award for Leadership honors a business or 
individual that has been a catalyst toward economic vitality in 
Commerce City through creative leadership, innovation, facilitation, 
collaboration or through contribution of resources. Triple-S Steel, the 
parent company of Intsel Steel West, was founded in 1960, and sells 
nearly 700,000 tons of steel per year for bridges, stadiums, barges, 
and large buildings throughout North America, Central and South America 
and Asia. Triple-S Steel consolidated and expanded the company's 
existing operations in Denver and at 6300 Clermont St. in Commerce City 
with a new 234,000 square-foot facility at 8573 Ulster St. The Commerce 
City operations are now a subsidiary called Intsel Steel West, 
employing nearly 100 people.
   I extend my deepest congratulations to Intsel Steel West for this 
well-deserved recognition from Commerce City.
```
