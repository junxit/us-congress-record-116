MISCELLANEOUS MEASURES
Committee on Appropriations: On July 14, 2020, Full Committee held a 
markup on the FY 2021 Defense Appropriations Bill; the FY 2021

and the FY 2021 Departments of Transportation, and Housing and Urban 
Development, and Related Agencies Appropriations Bill. The FY 2021

markup on the FY 2021 Department of Homeland Security Appropriations

Appropriations Bill; and the Report on the Revised Suballocation of

General Government Appropriations Bill were ordered reported, as 
amended. The Report on the Revised Suballocation of Budget Allocations
