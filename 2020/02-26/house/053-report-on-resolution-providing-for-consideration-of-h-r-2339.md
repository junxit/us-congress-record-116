# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2339, REVERSING THE YOUTH TOBACCO EPIDEMIC ACT OF 2019

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2339, 
            REVERSING THE YOUTH TOBACCO EPIDEMIC ACT OF 2019
```
