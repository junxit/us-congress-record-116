# REMEMBERING WILLIAM DAVID MARTIN

**Speaking:** Hyde-Smith, Cindy (H001079) [R-MS]

```
                    REMEMBERING WILLIAM DAVID MARTIN

  Mrs. HYDE-SMITH. Mr. President, let me first honor the life of 
William David Martin, a paramedic with American Medical Response of 
Southwest Mississippi, serving on the frontlines of Mississippi's 
healthcare as COVID-19 began to spread.
  Sadly, in April, he passed away from complications due to the virus. 
Mr. Martin is a true hero and one example of the extent to which first 
responders and healthcare workers are working to overcome this national 
emergency
```
