# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Patrick J. Conroy, offered the following 
prayer:
  Eternal God, we give You thanks for giving us another day. As the 
House lies silent, bless all Members as they encounter constituents in 
their home districts.
  Bless the people of this great Nation with wisdom, knowledge, and 
understanding that they might responsibly participate in our American 
democracy by making their interests known to their representatives. 
Please keep all who work for the people's House in good health that 
they may faithfully fulfill the great responsibility given them in 
their service to the work of the Capitol.
  May all that is done be for Your greater honor and glory.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
