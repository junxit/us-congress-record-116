# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 6148

**Measures:** H.R. 6148

```
            By Mr. COLE:
        H.R. 6148.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 9, Clauses 12 and 13, which grant 
     Congress the power to establish a military, and Clause 18, 
     which grants Congress the necessary and proper powers to 
     carry out its other enumerated powers
```
