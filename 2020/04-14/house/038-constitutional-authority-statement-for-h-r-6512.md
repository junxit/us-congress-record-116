# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 6512

**Measures:** H.R. 6512

```
           By Mr. PHILLIPS:
       H.R. 6512.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 4 of the Constitution, which states that 
     Congress may at any time by Law make or alter [regulations 
     related to Times, Places and Manner of holding elections].
       Additionally Article I, Section 8 of the Constitution 
     provides that Congress make make all laws which shall be 
     necessary and proper to carrying out the execution of its 
     powers, such as those described in Article I, Section 4.
```
