# RECOGNIZING JESSIE CRAFT OF REAGAN HIGH SCHOOL

**Speaking:** Foxx, Virginia (F000450) [R-NC]

```
             RECOGNIZING JESSIE CRAFT OF REAGAN HIGH SCHOOL

  (Ms. FOXX of North Carolina asked and was given permission to address 
the House for 1 minute.)
  Ms. FOXX of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
Jessie Craft, an exemplary Latin teacher at Reagan High School in 
Pfafftown, North Carolina.
  Simply put, Jessie goes the extra mile to provide his students with a 
high-quality education. Instead of relying only on textbook 
instruction, he crafts lesson plans that meet the unique needs of his 
students. From creating online language tutorial videos to reading and 
speaking entirely in Latin, Jessie keeps his students engaged while 
unlocking new ways to help them learn pertinent material.
  His novel approach serves as a reminder that when educational 
innovation is deliberately exercised, students succeed.
  Reagan High School is fortunate to have Jessie Craft as a teacher, 
and I am confident that he will continue to impact the lives of 
students now and in the future.
```
