# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

**Speaking:** Fischer, Deb (F000463) [R-NE]

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

  Mrs. FISCHER. Mr. President, I have 2 requests for committees to meet 
during today's session of the Senate. They have the approval of the 
Majority and Minority leaders.
  Pursuant to rule XXVI, paragraph 5(a), of the Standing Rules of the 
Senate, the following committees are authorized to meet during today's 
session of the Senate:

[[Page S859]]

  



                    Select Committee on Intelligence

  The Select Committee on Intelligence is authorized to meet during the 
session of the Senate on Tuesday, February 4, 2020, at 9 a.m., to 
conduct a closed briefing.


               Subcommittee on Transportation and Safety

  The Subcommittee on Transportation and Safety of the Committee on 
Commerce, Science, and Transportation is authorized to meet during the 
session of the Senate on Tuesday, February 4, 2020, at 10 a.m., to 
conduct a hearing.
```
