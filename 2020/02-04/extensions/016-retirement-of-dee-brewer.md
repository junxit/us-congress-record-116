# RETIREMENT OF DEE BREWER

**Speaking:** Crawford, Eric A. "Rick" (C001087) [R-AR]

```
                        RETIREMENT OF DEE BREWER

                                 ______
                                 

                     HON. ERIC A. ``RICK'' CRAWFORD

                              of arkansas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, February 4, 2020

  Mr. CRAWFORD. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor Deloris Berry 
Brewer, a lifelong resident of Stone County, Arkansas, who has 
announced her intention to retire her position at Stone Bank after 10 
years of cheerful and active service. Mrs. Brewer was the founder and 
coordinator of a monthly ceremony recognizing the service of Stone 
County military veterans which has to date honored more than 100 
veterans.
  Mrs. Brewer's dedication to area veterans has grown into the largest 
annual Veterans Day ceremony in the region, and she has faithfully and 
enthusiastically promoted the efforts of Stone Bank since its creation 
as Ozark Heritage Bank in 2009 to the extent that her co-workers have 
named her position ``Eternal Sunshine.''
  I thank Mrs. Brewer for her incredible service to Stone County 
veterans and to the state of Arkansas.

                          ____________________
```
