# IN RECOGNITION OF AT LAW GROUP

**Speaking:** Dingell, Debbie (D000624) [D-MI]

```
                     IN RECOGNITION OF AT LAW GROUP

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. DEBBIE DINGELL

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, February 4, 2020

  Mrs. DINGELL. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize AT Law Group. 
The firm's positive impact and contributions to our community are 
worthy of commendation.
  Based in Dearborn, AT Law Group is one of the leading law firms in 
the state of Michigan. The firm provides unmatched counsel, advocacy, 
and support to a diverse client base and is committed to protecting the 
rights, liberties, and the interests of all they serve. The firm 
maintains a mission to provide the highest level of service possible 
and strives to achieve excellence and the most favorable legal 
outcomes. With professionalism as a key pillar of their practice, AT 
Law Group ensures all their clients feel that they are heard, 
respected, and treated with dignity.
  Beyond offering exceptional legal services, AT Law Group has become 
an important voice in our community. They carry a large presence in 
outreach work for people in need in Washtenaw County, and their 
generosity has improved the lives of many. In addition, the firm has 
been a strong advocate for undocumented immigrants in our region. They 
have assisted many immigrants through difficult legal challenges, 
always ensuring they were afforded their Constitutional rights 
regardless of legal status. Undoubtably, AT Law Group has shown a true 
commitment to our community. Their presence has made a real and 
meaningful difference.
  Madam Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring AT Law 
Group. The firm is a critical resource for our community and their 
services help many get through the toughest of legal challenges. I am 
grateful for their dedication and wish them continued success in the 
years ahead.

                          ____________________
```
