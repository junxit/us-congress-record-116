# Cloture Motion (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. McCONNELL. Madam President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of James Ray Knepp II, of Ohio, to be United States District 
     Judge for the Northern District of Ohio.
         Mitch McConnell, James E. Risch, Joni Ernst, Marsha 
           Blackburn, Mike Crapo, James Lankford, Thom Tillis, Roy 
           Blunt, Roger F. Wicker, Pat Roberts, Mike Rounds, John 
           Cornyn, John Hoeven, Jerry Moran, Lamar Alexander, Mike 
           Braun, David Perdue.
  Mr. McCONNELL. I ask unanimous consent that the mandatory quorum call 
be waived.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. McCONNELL. Madam President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. McCONNELL. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
order for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
