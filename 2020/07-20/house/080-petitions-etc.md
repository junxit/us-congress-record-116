# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, petitions and papers were laid on the 
clerk's desk and referred as follows:

       117. The SPEAKER presented a petition of the Legislative 
     Council of the Louisville/Jefferson County Metro Government, 
     relative to Resolution No. 049, Series 2020, proclaiming the 
     existence of a local emergency and requesting the United 
     States Congress to provide direct rental housing assistance 
     for Covid-19 Pandemic; to the Committee on Financial 
     Services.
       118. Also, a petition of the Board of Supervisors of the 
     City and County of San Francisco, CA, relative to Resolution 
     No. 228-20, urging Congress to support $100 billion relief 
     funding for child care in the next Covid-19 relief package; 
     to the Committee on Education and Labor.
       119. Also, a petition of the Chenango County, NY Board of 
     Supervisors, relative to Resolution No. 107-20, calling upon 
     United States Congressional Representatives to demand direct 
     assistance to County and Local Governments, and to upgrade 
     rural Internet services as the next Covid-19 response package 
     is negotiated; to the Committee on Oversight and Reform.
```
