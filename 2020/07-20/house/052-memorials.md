# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 8, S.Res. 124

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       180. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the General 
     Assembly of the State of Ohio, relative to House Concurrent 
     Resolution No. 8, urging Congress to encourage the 
     Administrator of the FMCSA to change the crash indicator 
     BASIC regulation standards to consider only crashes in which 
     the driver was at fault for the crash; to the Committee on 
     Transportation and Infrastructure.
       181. Also, a memorial of the Senate of the State of 
     Michigan, relative to Senate Resolution No. 124, to 
     memorialize the Congress of the United States to help the 
     state of Michigan, schools, and local governments address 
     revenue shortfalls during the Covid-19 Pandemic; jointly to 
     the Committees on Oversight and Reform and Education and 
     Labor.

                          ____________________
```
