# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Jackson Lee, Sheila (J000032) [D-TX]

**Measures:** H.Res. 967, H.Res. 1054

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Ms. JACKSON LEE. Madam Speaker, I move that the House do now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to; accordingly (at 8 o'clock and 42 minutes 
p.m.), under its previous order, pursuant to section 4(b) of House 
Resolution 967 and pursuant to House Resolution 1054, the House 
adjourned until tomorrow, Tuesday, July 21, 2020, at 9 a.m., for 
morning-hour debate and 10 a.m. for legislative business, as a further 
mark of respect to the memory of the late Honorable John Lewis.
```
