# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 6967

**Measures:** H.R. 6967

```
           By Mr. CUNNINGHAM:
        H.R. 6967.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 1 of the U.S. Constitution, 
     ``The Congress shall have Power To . . . pay the Debts and 
     provide for the common Defence and general Welfare of the 
     United States;'';
       Clause 14, ``To make Rules for the Government and 
     Regulation of the land and naval Forces;''; and
       Clause 18, ``To make all Laws which shall be necessary and 
     proper for carrying into Execution the foregoing Powers . .''
```
