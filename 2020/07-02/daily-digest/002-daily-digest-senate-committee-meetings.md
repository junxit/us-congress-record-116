# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

**Measures:** S. 3398

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
OPERATION WARP SPEED
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Departments of Labor, 
Health and Human Services, and Education, and Related Agencies 
concluded a hearing to examine Operation Warp Speed, focusing on 
researching, manufacturing, and distributing a safe and effective 
coronavirus vaccine, after receiving testimony from Francis Collins, 
Director, National Institutes of Health, Robert R. Redfield, Director, 
Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, and Gary Disbrow, Acting 
Director, Biomedical Advanced Research and Development Authority, all 
of the Department of Health and Human Services.
BUSINESS MEETING
Committee on the Judiciary: Committee ordered favorably reported S. 
3398, to establish a National Commission on Online Child Sexual 
Exploitation Prevention, with an amendment in the nature of a 
substitute.
BUSINESS MEETING
Select Committee on Intelligence: On Wednesday, July 1, 2020, Committee 
ordered favorably reported the nomination of Peter Michael Thomson, of 
Louisiana, to be Inspector General, Central Intelligence Agency.

INTELLIGENCE
Select Committee on Intelligence: Committee met in closed session to 
receive a briefing on certain intelligence matters from officials of 
the intelligence community.
```
