# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 4049

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** S. 4049

```
                  UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 4049

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the filing 
deadline for first-degree amendments with respect to the cloture 
motions filed during today's session be at 5 p.m., Monday, July 20. I 
further ask that no second-degree amendments be in order to the 
following amendments: 2252, 2411, 1788, 2244, 1729, and 1972, as 
modified.
```
