# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, petitions and papers were laid on the 
clerk's desk and referred as follows:

       115. The SPEAKER presented a petition of the Borough of 
     Butler, Morris County, NJ, relative to Resolution No. 2020-
     57, urging the state of New Jersey to provide direct 
     stabilization funding to Morris County from the Coronavirus 
     Relief Fund; which was referred to the Committee on Oversight 
     and Reform.
       116. Also, a petition of the Lafayette, WI County Board of 
     Supervisors, relative to Resolution 5-20, supporting a 
     constitutional amendment to limit corporate campaign 
     contributions under the First Amendment of the U.S. 
     Constitution; which was referred to the Committee on the 
     Judiciary.
```
