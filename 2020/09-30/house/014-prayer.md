# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Patrick J. Conroy, offered the following 
prayer:

[[Page H5061]]

  God of the universe, Father to us all, we give You thanks for giving 
us another day.
  During these few days before the House breaks for the final weeks of 
the campaign, send us Your spirit of peace. Inspire the Members to 
complete their charge to promote the common good of our Nation. Comfort 
those who continue to suffer from the coronavirus, and protect those 
who labor still to treat the disease and bring an effective vaccine to 
the world.
  May all that is done this day be for Your greater honor and glory.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
