# RECESS

```
                                 RECESS

  The SPEAKER. Pursuant to clause 12(a) of rule I, the Chair declares 
the House in recess subject to the call of the Chair.


 =========================== NOTE =========================== 

  
  December 28, 2020, on page H9153, the following appeared: RECESS 
The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 12(a) of rule I, the 
Chair declares the House in recess subject to the call of the 
Chair.
  
  The online version has been corrected to read: RECESS The 
SPEAKER. Pursuant to clause 12(a) of rule I, the Chair declares 
the House in recess subject to the call of the Chair.


 ========================= END NOTE ========================= 


  Accordingly (at 2 o'clock and 2 minutes p.m.), the House stood in 
recess.

                          ____________________
```
