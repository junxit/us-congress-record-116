# TRIBUTE TO BENJAMIN NEWELL

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO BENJAMIN NEWELL

● Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I would like to take the 
opportunity to express my appreciation to Benjamin for his hard work as 
an intern in my Casper office. I recognize his efforts and 
contributions to my office as well as to the State of Wyoming.
  Benjamin is a native of Nebraska. He is a student at the University 
of Wyoming, where he is studying political science and communications. 
He has demonstrated a strong work ethic, which has made him an 
invaluable asset to our office. The quality of his work is reflected in 
his great efforts over the last several months.
  I want to thank Benjamin for the dedication he has shown while 
working for me and my staff. It is a pleasure to have him as part of 
our team. I know he will have continued success with all of his future 
endeavors. I wish him all my best on his journey.

                          ____________________
```
