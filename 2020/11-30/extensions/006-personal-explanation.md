# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Lee, Barbara (L000551) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 8294

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. BARBARA LEE

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, November 30, 2020

  Ms. LEE of California. Madam Speaker, due to a family obligation, I 
was absent from the House on November 20, 2020. Had I been present for 
Roll Call Vote 225 on House Amendment 901, I would have voted ``yea.'' 
Had I been present for Roll Call Vote 226 on House Amendment 902, I 
would have voted ``nay.'' Had I been present for Roll Call Vote 227 on 
Final Passage of H.R. 8294, the National Apprenticeship Act of 2020, I 
would have voted ``yea.'' I am pleased that this legislation to 
modernize our nation's system of registered apprenticeships passed 
decisively in the House.
```
