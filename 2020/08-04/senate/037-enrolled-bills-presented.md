# ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

**Measures:** S. 2163, S. 3607, S. 3637

```
                        ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on today, August 4, 2020, 
she had presented to the President of the United States the following 
enrolled bills:

       S. 2163. An act to establish the Commission on the Social 
     Status of Black Men and Boys, to study and make 
     recommendations to address social problems affecting Black 
     men and boys, and for other purposes.
       S. 3607. An act to extend public safety officer death 
     benefits to public safety officers whose death is caused by 
     COVID-19, and for other purposes.
       S. 3637. An act to amend the Servicemembers Civil Relief 
     Act to extend lease protections for servicemembers under stop 
     movement orders in response to a local, national, or global 
     emergency, and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
