# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of John Peter Cronan, of New York, to be United States 
     District Judge for the Southern District of New York.
         Mitch McConnell, Richard C. Shelby, Lamar Alexander, Pat 
           Roberts, Mike Crapo, Marsha Blackburn, David Perdue, 
           Kevin Cramer, John Cornyn, Shelley Moore Capito, John 
           Thune, Cindy Hyde-Smith, Cory Gardner, Roy Blunt, 
           Martha McSally, John Barrasso, John Boozman.

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
mandatory quorum call be waived.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
