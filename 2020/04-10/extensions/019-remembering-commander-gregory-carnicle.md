# REMEMBERING COMMANDER GREGORY CARNICLE

**Speaking:** Lesko, Debbie (L000589) [R-AZ]

```
                 REMEMBERING COMMANDER GREGORY CARNICLE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DEBBIE LESKO

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, April 10, 2020

  Mrs. LESKO. Madam Speaker, I rise today with great sadness over the 
passing of Commander Gregory Carnicle from Peoria, Arizona.
  He was beloved by many. He was a loving husband to Ann, father to 
Rachel, Cecilia, John and Veronica and grandfather to four with two 
more on the way.
  He began his career as a patrol officer in June 1989 and was a 31-
year veteran with the Phoenix Police Department. He is the first 
Phoenix Police Commander to lose his life in the line of duty. He was a 
well-respected leader and hero to many. We are eternally grateful for 
his service to our community and state.
  On this day, we as a community honor him and ask those who knew him 
to pray for his family during this time of mourning.

                          ____________________
```
