# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 6489

**Measures:** H.R. 6489

```
           By Mr. THORNBERRY:
       H.R. 6489.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 14 of the United States 
     Constitution, which provides Congress with the power to make 
     rules for the government and regulation of the land and naval 
     forces.
```
