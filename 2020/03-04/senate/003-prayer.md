# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, our Father, through these days of Lent, give us a 
continuous awareness of Your presence.
  May each lawmaker remember Your promise to never leave or forsake us. 
Finding power in Your presence, give our Senators the ability to 
discover solutions to the problems that confront our Nation and world. 
May they strive to make a positive difference for all Americans, 
permitting Your light to illumine the way. Lord, provide our 
legislators with a new vision of faith and a fresh venture of hope as 
they seek creative ways to help a troubled world.
  We pray in Your powerful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
