# IN HONOR OF LEE-SCOTT ACADEMY WINNING THE AISA CLASS AAA BASKETBALL CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Rogers, Mike D. (R000575) [R-AL]

```
  IN HONOR OF LEE-SCOTT ACADEMY WINNING THE AISA CLASS AAA BASKETBALL 
                              CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE ROGERS

                               of alabama

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, March 4, 2020

  Mr. ROGERS of Alabama. Madam Speaker, I rise to recognize the Lee-
Scott Academy girls basketball team for winning the Alabama Independent 
School Association (AISA) Class AAA basketball championship.
  The Warriors defeated Glenwood 44 to 26 in the championship game on 
February 15th at the Crampton Bowl in Montgomery.
  Madam Speaker, please join me in congratulating the students and 
faculty of Lee-Scott Academy, the coaches, the players and all the 
Warrior fans on this exciting achievement. Go Warriors.

                          ____________________
```
