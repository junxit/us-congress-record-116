# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal Father, our souls long for You, for we find strength and joy 
in Your presence.
  Guide our lawmakers to put their trust in You, seeking in every 
undertaking to know and do Your will. When they go through 
difficulties, may they remember that, with Your help, they can 
accomplish the seemingly impossible. Lord, give them a faith that will 
trust you even when the darkness is blacker than a thousand midnights. 
May they always find strength in Your providential meaning.
  We pray in Your strong Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
