# LETTER SUBMITTED PURSUANT TO SECTION 4(b) OF HOUSE RESOLUTION 965, 116TH CONGRESS

**Measures:** H.Res. 965

```
  LETTER SUBMITTED PURSUANT TO SECTION 4(b) OF HOUSE RESOLUTION 965, 
                             116TH CONGRESS


                          committee on ethics

                                         House of Representatives,


                                          Committee on Ethics,

                                     Washington, DC, July 1, 2020.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     Speaker of the House,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Speaker Pelosi: Pursuant to section 4(b) of House 
     Resolution 965, we are writing to inform you that the 
     Committee on Ethics has met the requirements for conducting a 
     business meeting outlined in regulation E.l of the remote 
     committee proceedings regulations, inserted into the 
     Congressional Record on May 15, 2020, and that the committee 
     is prepared to conduct a remote meeting and permit remote 
     participation.
       In meeting these requirements, the committee held a non-
     public business meeting rehearsal on June 17, 2020 and two 
     full committee hearings with remote participation on June 30, 
     2020.
       Thank you,
     Theodore E. Deutch,
       Chairman.
     Kenny Marchant,
       Ranking Member.
     Grace Meng,
       Member of Congress.
     Susan Wild,
       Member of Congress.
     Dean Phillips,
       Member of Congress.
     Anthony Brown,
       Member of Congress.

                          ____________________
```
