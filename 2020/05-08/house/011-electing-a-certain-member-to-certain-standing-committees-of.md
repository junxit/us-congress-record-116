# ELECTING A CERTAIN MEMBER TO CERTAIN STANDING COMMITTEES OF THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

**Measures:** H.Res. 954

```
 ELECTING A CERTAIN MEMBER TO CERTAIN STANDING COMMITTEES OF THE HOUSE 
                           OF REPRESENTATIVES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the Chair lays before the 
House the following resolution (H. Res. 954) electing a certain member 
to certain standing committees of the House of Representatives.
  The Clerk read the title of the resolution.
  There was no objection.
  The text of the resolution is as follows:

                              H. Res. 954

       Resolved, That the following named Member be, and is 
     hereby, elected to the following standing committees of the 
     House of Representatives:
       Committee on Oversight and Reform: Mr. Mfume (to rank 
     immediately after Mr. Rouda).
       Committee on Small Business: Mr. Mfume (to rank immediately 
     after Ms. Davids of Kansas).

  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
