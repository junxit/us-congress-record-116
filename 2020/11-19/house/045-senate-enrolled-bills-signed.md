# SENATE ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

**Measures:** S. 327, S. 3147, S. 3587

```
                      SENATE ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

  The Speaker on Thursday, November 19, 2020, announced her signature 
to enrolled bills of the Senate of the following titles:

       S. 327.--An Act to amend the Federal Lands Recreation 
     Enhancement Act to provide for a lifetime National 
     Recreational Pass for any veteran with a service-connected 
     disability.
       S. 3147.--An Act to require the Secretary of Veterans 
     Affairs to submit to Congress reports on patient safety and 
     quality of care at medical centers of the Department of 
     Veterans Affairs, and for other purposes.
       S. 3587.--An Act to require the Secretary of Veterans 
     Affairs to conduct a study on the accessibility of websites 
     of the Department of Veterans Affairs to individuals with 
     disabilities, and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
