```
        TRIAL OF DONALD J. TRUMP, PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES

  The CHIEF JUSTICE. The Senate will convene as a Court of Impeachment.
  The Chaplain will lead us in prayer.


 =========================== NOTE =========================== 

  
  On page S443, January 22, 2020, first column, the following 
appears: The CHIEF JUSTICE. The Senate will convene as a Court of 
Impeachment. The Chaplain will offer a prayer.

The Senate will convene as a Court of Impeachment. The Chaplain 
will lead us in prayer.
```
