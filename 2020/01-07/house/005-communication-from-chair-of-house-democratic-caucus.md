# COMMUNICATION FROM CHAIR OF HOUSE DEMOCRATIC CAUCUS

          COMMUNICATION FROM CHAIR OF HOUSE DEMOCRATIC CAUCUS

the Chair of the House Democratic Caucus:

                                    Congress of the United States,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                  Washington, DC, January 7, 2020.

     Rule X, Clause 5(b)(1), I am writing to inform you that
