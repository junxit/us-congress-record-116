# IN HONOR OF PIEDMONT WINNING THE AHSAA CLASS 3A FOOTBALL CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Rogers, Mike D. (R000575) [R-AL]

```
 IN HONOR OF PIEDMONT WINNING THE AHSAA CLASS 3A FOOTBALL CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE ROGERS

                               of alabama

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, January 7, 2020

  Mr. ROGERS of Alabama. Madam Speaker, I rise to recognize the 
Piedmont High School football team for winning the Alabama High School 
Athletic Association (AHSAA) Class 3A football championship.
  The Bulldogs defeated Mobile Christian 26-24 in the championship game 
on December 5th played at Jordan-Hare Stadium at Auburn University.
  Madam Speaker, please join me in congratulating the students and 
faculty of Piedmont High School, Coach Smith along with all the 
coaches, the players and all the Bulldog fans on this exciting 
achievement. Go Bulldogs.

                          ____________________
```
