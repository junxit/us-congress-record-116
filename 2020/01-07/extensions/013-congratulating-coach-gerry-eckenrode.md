# CONGRATULATING COACH GERRY ECKENRODE

**Speaking:** Joyce, John (J000302) [R-PA]

```
                  CONGRATULATING COACH GERRY ECKENRODE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOHN JOYCE

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, January 7, 2020

  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Madam Speaker, I rise today to 
congratulate Altoona native Gerry Eckenrode upon his induction into the 
Adams County Chapter of the Pennsylvania Sports Hall of Fame.
  Throughout his coaching career at Delone Catholic in McSherrystown, 
Coach Eckenrode has established a strong record of excellence. During 
his 20 year tenure as head coach of the girls' basketball team, Coach 
Eckenrode has won 466 games, including leading the Squirettes to three 
straight PIAA Class 2A state championships from 2003 to 2005 and a 
Class 3A state championship in 2019.
  Coach Eckenrode not only stands out for his winning record, but also 
his dedicated service and leadership as he teaches his team what it 
takes to succeed both on and off the court. It is my pleasure to 
congratulate fellow Bishop Guilfoyle High School graduate Gerry 
Eckenrode on this honor, and I wish the Eckenrode Family continued 
success.

                          ____________________
```
