# REPLACING JUSTICE RUTH BADER GINSBURG

**Speaking:** Adams, Alma S. (A000370) [D-NC]

```
                 REPLACING JUSTICE RUTH BADER GINSBURG

  (Ms. ADAMS asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Ms. ADAMS. Mr. Speaker, Ruth Bader Ginsburg was the conscience of the 
Court and one of the greatest fighters American women have ever had. 
Justice Ginsburg's dissents spoke for all of us--with a righteous 
anger, a moral clarity, and an eye to equality.
  Her lifetime of advocacy for women extended far beyond her service on 
the bench.
  Though she is irreplaceable, Justice Ginsburg will have a successor. 
But Americans deserve better than an impeached President's last-minute 
nomination just weeks before the election.
  We must honor Justice Ginsburg's dying wish that she not be replaced 
until the next President is installed. This nominee will shape the 
future of our country, and the American people deserve a say.

                          ____________________
```
