**Speaking:** Huizenga, Bill (H001058) [R-MI]

                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. BILL HUIZENGA

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, December 2, 2020

  Mr. HUIZENGA. Madam Speaker, I rise today regarding missed votes due 
to a death in the family. Had I been present for rollcall vote number 
225, On Agreeing to the Rep. Levin of Michigan Part B Amendment No. 9 
for the National Apprenticeship Act of 2020, I would have voted 
``nay.'' Had I been present for roll call vote number 226, On Agreeing 
to the Rep. Smucker of Pennsylvania Part B Amendment No. 15 for the 
National Apprenticeship Act of 2020, I would have voted ``yea.'' Had I 
been present for roll call vote No. 227, On Passage of the bill 
National Apprenticeship Act of 2020, and for other purposes, I would 
have voted ``nay.''
