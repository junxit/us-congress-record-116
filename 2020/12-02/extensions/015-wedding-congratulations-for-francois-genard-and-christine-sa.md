# WEDDING CONGRATULATIONS FOR FRANCOIS GENARD AND CHRISTINE SASTRI

**Speaking:** Cox, TJ (C001124) [D-CA]

```
    WEDDING CONGRATULATIONS FOR FRANCOIS GENARD AND CHRISTINE SASTRI

                                 ______
                                 

                              HON. TJ COX

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, December 2, 2020

  Mr. COX of California. Madam Speaker, I rise to recognize and 
congratulate Mr. Francois Genard and Ms. Christine Sastri on their 
upcoming wedding in September of 2021. I would not be here today as a 
Member of Congress but for Francois' efforts, talent and capabilities. 
I am honored to call him a friend and colleague. Together with Chrissy, 
they serve as an example of the character, capability and 
professionalism that serves our nation well.
  As the Chief of Staff, Francois has shown great skill in leading our 
team to best serve the interests of the Central Valley. His tireless 
work serving all our communities is invaluable, and his strong 
leadership and foresight should be emulated by all. Francois' efforts 
have turned our office into a family, and our team is grateful for all 
he has done.
  I offer my warmest congratulations and best wishes to Francois and 
Chrissy as they begin this new chapter in their lives. We all join 
their family and friends in celebrating this wonderful milestone. I ask 
my colleagues in the U.S. House of Representatives to join me in 
celebrating this couple as they continue to build a happy, healthy life 
together.

                          ____________________
```
