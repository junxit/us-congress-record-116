# WEDDING CONGRATULATIONS FOR JOHN LYNCH AND ALEXA PAPADOPOULOS

**Speaking:** Cox, TJ (C001124) [D-CA]

```
     WEDDING CONGRATULATIONS FOR JOHN LYNCH AND ALEXA PAPADOPOULOS

                                 ______
                                 

                              HON. TJ COX

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, December 2, 2020

  Mr. COX of California. Madam Speaker, I rise to recognize and 
congratulate Mr. John Lynch and Ms. Alexa Papadopoulos on their 
upcoming wedding. John was an instrumental part of my team and I am 
honored to call him a friend and a colleague. He and Alexa are fine 
examples of young men and women who have stepped up to work in 
government to make a positive difference in the lives of all Americans 
through their efforts, capability and professionalism.
  As our Senior Legislative Assistant, John has shown great 
attentiveness and care for some of the most pressing issues of the 
Central Valley--water infrastructure, economic growth and public 
policy. John's tireless work serving our communities is invaluable, and 
his work on water policy will certainly carry on helping thousands of 
people here in the Valley. John ties our team together with his 
personality and humor, all while continuing his vital service to so 
many individuals and communities.
  I offer my warmest congratulations and best wishes to John and Alexa 
as they begin this new chapter in their lives. We all join their family 
and friends in celebrating this wonderful milestone. I ask my 
colleagues in the U.S. House of Representatives to join me in 
celebrating this couple. We wish them well as they continue to build a 
happy, healthy life together.

                          ____________________
```
