# COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

**Measures:** S. 1153, S. 1982, H.R. 6395

```
               COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Butterfield) laid before the House the 
following communication from the Clerk of the House of Representatives:

                                              Office of the Clerk,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                 Washington, DC, December 2, 2020.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     The Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Madam Speaker: Pursuant to the permission granted in 
     Clause 2(h) of Rule II of the Rules of the U.S. House of 
     Representatives, the Clerk received the following message 
     from the Secretary of the Senate on December 2, 2020, at 
     12:26 p.m.:
       That the Senate passed S. 1153.
       That the Senate agrees to the House amendment to the bill 
     S. 1982.
       That the Senate agrees to Conference with the House of 
     Representatives H.R. 6395.
       With best wishes, I am
           Sincerely,
                                                Cheryl L. Johnson,
     Clerk.

                          ____________________
```
