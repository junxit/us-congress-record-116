# ADMINISTRATION OF OATH OF OFFICE

```
                    ADMINISTRATION OF OATH OF OFFICE

  The VICE PRESIDENT. If the Senator-elect will now present himself to 
the desk, the Chair will administer the oath of office.
  The Senator-elect, Mark Kelly, escorted by Ms. Sinema, advanced to 
the desk of the Vice President; the oath prescribed by law was 
administered to him by the Vice President; and he subscribed to the 
oath in the Official Oath Book.
  The VICE PRESIDENT. Congratulations, Senator.
```
