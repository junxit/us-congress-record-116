# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Righteous God, refresh our lawmakers as a river in the desert and as 
the cool shadows of large trees in a hot and weary land. May our 
Senators find in You a hope that illuminates the paths they travel as 
You guide them with Your great love.
  Lord, fill them with such wisdom that they will solve our Nation's 
most challenging problems, making the rough places smooth and the 
crooked places straight. Deal favorably with them because of Your great 
love and mercy. Inspire them to live lives that will permit You to 
bless our Nation and world.
  We pray in Your mighty Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
