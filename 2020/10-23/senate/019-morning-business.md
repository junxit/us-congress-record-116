         RECOGNIZING THE ROTARY CLUB OF BOWLING GREEN, KENTUCKY

● Mr. PAUL. Mr. President, for 100 years, the Rotary Club of 
Bowling Green, KY, has been committed to the highest standards of 
humanitarian service. The results of this century of engagement can be 
seen in successful projects throughout my hometown of Bowling Green, 
but also abroad, where our local Rotary Club has partnered with Rotary 
International to globally eradicate polio and to meet other enormous 
public health and safety needs. I was privileged to participate, along 
with one of my sons, in a project to bring clean, chlorinated water to 
an underserved community in Guatemala and to see, firsthand, the impact 
that the Rotary Club of Bowling Green is having in the lives of 
families and children in that country. This is a tremendous 
organization that deserves our recognition. I commend its current 
president, Alan Palmer, his board, and the current members--who give 
generously of their time, resources, and talents--for leading this 
organization into a new century of service.
