# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal King, You are great and marvelous. You alone are God. Without 
Your wondrous deeds, our Nation and planet could not survive. You 
continue to perform wonders on our behalf, rescuing us from ourselves.
  Lord, teach our lawmakers Your precepts so that they may walk in Your 
truth, experiencing the reverential awe that comes when You are near. 
Provide them with the inspiration and knowledge that will bring peace 
and stability to our land. May we not conceal our sins but confess and 
forsake them. Sovereign God, keep our Senators on the path of wisdom.
  We pray in Your mighty Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
