                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       152. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the Senate of the 
     State of New Jersey, relative to Senate Resolution No. 163, 
     urging the President and Congress of the United States to 
     enact legislation establishing a safe daily level of 
     cannabidiol consumption; to the Committee on Energy and 
     Commerce.
       153. Also, a memorial of the General Assembly of the State 
     of New Jersey, relative to Assembly Resolution No. 244, 
     urging the President and Congress of the United States to 
     enact a law prohibiting an airline from

     carry-on limit or restricting passengers from carrying breast 
     milk onto the aircraft; to the Committee on Transportation 
     and Infrastructure.

     of Ohio, relative to Amended House Concurrent Resolution No. 
     10, urging the federal government to designate certain drug 
     cartels operating as foreign terrorist organizations; jointly 
     to the Committees on the Judiciary and Foreign Affairs.
