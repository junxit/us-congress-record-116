# MOTHER FRANCES XAVIER CABRINI POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 4794

```
           MOTHER FRANCES XAVIER CABRINI POST OFFICE BUILDING

  A bill (H.R. 4794) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 8320 13th Avenue in Brooklyn, New York, as 
the ``Mother Frances Xavier Cabrini Post Office Building,'' was ordered 
to a third reading, was read the third time, and passed.
```
