# JULIUS L. CHAMBERS CIVIL RIGHTS MEMORIAL POST OFFICE

**Measures:** H.R. 4981

```
          JULIUS L. CHAMBERS CIVIL RIGHTS MEMORIAL POST OFFICE

  A bill (H.R. 4981) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 2505 Derita Avenue in Charlotte, North 
Carolina, as the ``Julius L. Chambers Civil Rights Memorial Post 
Office,'' was ordered to a third reading, was read the third time, and
```
