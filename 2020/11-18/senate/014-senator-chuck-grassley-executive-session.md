# Senator Chuck Grassley (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                         Senator Chuck Grassley

  Madam President, as I begin today, I just want to say that our 
thoughts are with Senator Chuck Grassley after his coronavirus 
diagnosis. It was a strange day in the Senate yesterday with Chuck 
Grassley not voting, because he broke a 27-year-long streak of showing 
up for every single vote. We are praying for his swift recovery and his 
speedy return to the Senate
```
