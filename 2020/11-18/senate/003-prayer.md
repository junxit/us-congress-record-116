# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Holy God, continue to be our strength and shield. Extend Your 
powerful hands to aid and deliver us.
  Lord, inspire our Senators to believe that You continue to work for 
the good of those who love You. Let Your favor shine upon our lawmakers 
and sustain them with Your unfailing love.
  Lord, release to them the blessings You have stored for those who 
honor You. You are our God, and our future is in Your hands.
  Remind us all to seek first to understand before we strive to be 
understood.
  We pray in Your Sacred Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
