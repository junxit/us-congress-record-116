# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS PRIMARY SPONSOR OF H.R. 3025

**Speaking:** Lee, Susie (L000590) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 3025

```
 PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS PRIMARY SPONSOR OF H.R. 3025

  Mrs. LEE of Nevada. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may 
hereafter be considered the primary sponsor of H.R. 3025, You Are Not 
Forgotten Act, a bill originally introduced by Representative Meadows 
of North Carolina, for the purpose of adding cosponsors and requesting 
reprintings under clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentlewoman from Nevada?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
