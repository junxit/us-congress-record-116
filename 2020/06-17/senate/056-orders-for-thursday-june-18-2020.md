# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JUNE 18, 2020

**Speaking:** Portman, Rob (P000449) [R-OH]

```
                   ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JUNE 18, 2020

  Mr. PORTMAN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m., 
Thursday, June 18; further, that following the prayer and the pledge, 
the morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their 
use later in the day, and morning business be closed; further, that 
following leader remarks, the Senate proceed to executive session to 
resume consideration of the Walker nomination; finally, notwithstanding 
rule XXII, postcloture time on the Walker nomination expire at 1:30 
p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
