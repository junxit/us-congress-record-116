# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  O God, our refuge and strength, give us reverence for Your greatness. 
Guide our Senators around the pitfalls of their work, enabling them to 
have hearts sustained by Your peace. May they surrender their will to 
You as they trust You to direct their steps.
  Lord, remind them that leadership can work miracles with cooperation 
but rarely accomplishes much with conflict. Inspire our lawmakers to be 
quick to listen, slow to speak, and slow to get angry. Help them to 
strive to live with such integrity that they give You the honor due 
Your Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
