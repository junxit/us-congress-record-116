# TRIBUTE TO TERRY TOBINESS

**Speaking:** Daines, Steve (D000618) [R-MT]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO TERRY TOBINESS

● Mr. DAINES. Mr. President, it is my honor to recognize Terry 
Tobiness for her 50 years of dedication to her country, her patients, 
and her fellow veterans.
  A graduate of the Helena College of Technology, Terry's first job out 
of college was at St. John's Hospital in Helena.
  From then on, Terry went on to work at Crest Nursing Home in Butte, 
holding various roles over 18 years as a medical nurse, physical 
therapy aide, and assistant director of nursing and social services 
representative.
  In 1980, Terry continued her service and joined the Army Reserves, 
deploying to the United Arab Emirates during the first gulf war as part 
of Operation Desert Storm in the 1990s.
  As a fellow veteran herself and a dedicated health care hero, Terry 
spent the last 10 years of her nursing career in Great Falls, providing 
care at the Great Falls VA clinic in the Neurology and Pain Department 
as a nurse.
  Terry's commitment to helping others is exemplary of the rich legacy 
of service we have in Montana. I know that all of Terry's patients over 
the years are thankful for her lifetime of service to the community, 
the State of Montana, and our country.●

                          ____________________
```
