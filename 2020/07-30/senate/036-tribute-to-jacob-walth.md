# TRIBUTE TO JACOB WALTH

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO JACOB WALTH

  Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Jacob Walth, an intern in 
my Aberdeen, SD, office, for all of the hard work he has done for me, 
my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past several weeks.
  Jacob is a graduate of Aberdeen Central High School in Aberdeen, SD. 
Currently, he is attending St. Cloud State University in St. Cloud, MN, 
where he is majoring in finance and real estate. He is a hard worker 
who has been dedicated to getting the most out of his internship 
experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Jacob for all of the 
fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the years to 
come.
  (At the request of Mr. Durbin, the following statement was ordered to 
be printed in the Record.)

                          ____________________
```
