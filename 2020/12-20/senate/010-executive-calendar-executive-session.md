# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY], Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will 
proceed to executive session to resume consideration of the following 
nomination, which the clerk will report.
  The bill clerk read the nomination of Brian Noland, of Tennessee, to 
be a Member of the Board of Directors of the Tennessee Valley Authority 
for a term expiring May 18, 2024.
  Mr. McCONNELL. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The bill clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
