# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Patrick J. Conroy, offered the following 
prayer:
  Lord of all creation, thank You for giving us another day.
  Americans can see on television trucks carrying the first rollout of 
vaccinations in our country. For many this is a great sign of hope, for 
others a moment of concern. Give Your people hope, and courage where 
needed, to take advantage of this tremendous breakthrough.
  We praise Your holy name that You have gifted so many men and women 
who have labored to bring this day to fruition: Brilliant medical 
scientists, down to logistical professionals, to delivery personnel. We 
are all served so well by these generous brothers and sisters.
  Continue to strengthen the resolve of legislators here in the Capitol 
in their efforts to provide an economic relief bill and funding the 
government.
  May all that is done be for Your greater honor and glory.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
