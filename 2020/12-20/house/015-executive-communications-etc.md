     401(c); (90 Stat. 1257) and 50 U.S.C. 1703(c); Public Law 95-

     Affairs.

     Reform.

     Transportation, transmitting the Department's Semiannual 
     Report of the Office of Inspector General for the period 
     ending September 30, 2020; to the Committee on Oversight and 
     Reform.
       EC-5980. A letter from the Chair, Equal Employment 
     Opportunity Commission, transmitting the Commission's 
     Inspector General's Semiannual Report to Congress for the 
     period ending October 31, 2020; to the Committee on Oversight 
     and Reform.
       EC-5981. A letter from the Secretary, Department of 
     Commerce, transmitting the Fiscal Year 2019 Annual Report of
