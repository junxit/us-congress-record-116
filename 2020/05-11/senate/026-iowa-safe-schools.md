# IOWA SAFE SCHOOLS

**Speaking:** Ernst, Joni (E000295) [R-IA]

```
                           IOWA SAFE SCHOOLS

● Ms. ERNST. Mr. President, I would like to recognize Iowa Safe 
Schools as they hold their 15th annual Iowa Governor's Conference on 
LGBTQ Youth today, May 11, 2020.
  This annual tradition, which began in 2006, is an effort to help end 
bullying in our schools and to create leadership opportunities for 
LGBTQ youth students from not just Iowa but from States across the 
Midwest.
  Thank you to Iowa Safe Schools for continuing your mission of 
providing a safe environment and community for Iowa's LGBTQ youth to 
learn and grow.●
```
