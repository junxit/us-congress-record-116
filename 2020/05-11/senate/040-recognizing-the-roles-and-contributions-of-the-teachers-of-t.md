**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** S.Res. 568

```
 RECOGNIZING THE ROLES AND CONTRIBUTIONS OF THE TEACHERS OF THE UNITED 
  STATES IN BUILDING AND ENHANCING THE CIVIC, CULTURAL, AND ECONOMIC 
                    WELL-BEING OF THE UNITED STATES

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the consideration of S. Res. 568, submitted earlier today.

       A resolution (S. Res. 568) recognizing the roles and

debate.

  The resolution (S. Res. 568) was agreed to.
  The preamble was agreed to.
```
