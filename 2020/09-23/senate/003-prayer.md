# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Gracious God, empower our lawmakers to grow in grace. Make them 
gentle yet brave, confident yet humble, wise yet dependent on Your 
guidance. Lord, give them the wisdom to cultivate a faith that 
perseveres, keeping them from growing weary in doing what is right. 
Strengthen their ability to see among their colleagues Your divine 
image. May our Senators increase in favor with You and humanity. Fill 
them with a passion to live for Your glory.
  We praise You for being our helper, and we desire to magnify Your 
Holy Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
