**Speaking:** Manchin, Joe, III (M001183) [D-WV], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the nomination.
  The senior assistant bill clerk read the nomination of Jocelyn 
Samuels, of Maryland, to be a Member of the Equal Employment 
Opportunity Commission for a term expiring July 1, 2021.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, all postcloture time 
has expired.
  The question is, Will the Senate advise and consent to the Samuels 
nomination?
  Mr. MANCHIN. Madam President, I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk called the roll.
   Mr. THUNE. The following Senators are necessarily absent: the 
Senator from West Virginia (Mrs. Capito), the Senator from Wisconsin 
(Mr. Johnson), and the Senator from Kansas (Mr. Moran).
  Mr. DURBIN. I announce that the Senator from California (Ms. Harris) 
is necessarily absent.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Are there any other Senators in the Chamber 
desiring to vote?
```
