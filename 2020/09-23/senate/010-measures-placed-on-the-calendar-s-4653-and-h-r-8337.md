# MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 4653 AND H.R. 8337

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** S. 4653, H.R. 8337

```
         MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 4653 AND H.R. 8337

  Mr. McCONNELL. Madam President, I understand there are two bills at 
the desk due a second reading en bloc.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bills by title for the 
second time.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 4653) to protect the healthcare of hundreds of 
     millions of people of the United States and prevent efforts 
     of the Department of Justice to advocate courts to strike 
     down the Patient Protection and Affordable Care Act.
       A bill (H.R. 8337) making continuing appropriations for 
     fiscal year 2021, and for other purposes.

  Mr. McCONNELL. In order to place the bills on the calendar under the 
provisions of rule XIV, I would object to further proceedings, en bloc.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection having been heard, the bills will be 
placed on the calendar.

                          ____________________
```
