# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 9, H.Con.Res. 64, S. 521

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       178. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the House of 
     Representatives of the State of Louisiana, relative to House 
     Concurrent Resolution No. 64, expressing support for the 
     annual Gulf Hypoxia Mapping Cruise conducted by the Louisiana 
     Universities Marine Consortium; to the Committee on Natural 
     Resources.
       179. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Louisiana, relative to House Concurrent 
     Resolution No. 9, memorializing the United States Congress to 
     take such actions as are necessary to review the Government 
     Pension Offset and the Windfall Elimination Provision Social 
     Security benefit reduction and to consider eliminating or 
     reducing them by supporting S. 521 of the 116th Congress, the 
     Social Security Fairness Act; to the Committee on Ways and 
     Means.

                          ____________________
```
