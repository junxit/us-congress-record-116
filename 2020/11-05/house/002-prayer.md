# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Monsignor Stephen J. Rossetti, Catholic University of America, 
Washington, D.C., offered the following prayer:
  Good and gracious God, we invoke the brilliant light of Your son and 
cast out all spirits of darkness, discord, and division.
  May Your holy angels of peace dwell in this place. May we be united 
in bonds of love and mutual respect, across the aisle and across this 
Nation.
  We make this prayer in Your holy name.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
