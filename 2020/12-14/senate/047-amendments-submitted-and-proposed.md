# AMENDMENTS SUBMITTED AND PROPOSED

**Measures:** S. 1636, S. 2597, H.R. 4704

```
                    AMENDMENTS SUBMITTED AND PROPOSED

       SA 2705. Mr. McCONNELL (for Mr. Thune) proposed an 
     amendment to the bill S. 2597, to require the National 
     Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration to make certain 
     operational models available to the public, and for other 
     purposes.
       SA 2706. Mr. McCONNELL (for Mr. Wicker) proposed an 
     amendment to the bill H.R. 4704, to direct the Director of 
     the National Science Foundation to support multidisciplinary 
     research on the science of suicide, and to advance the 
     knowledge and understanding of issues that may be associated 
     with several aspects of suicide including intrinsic and 
     extrinsic factors related to areas such as wellbeing, 
     resilience, and vulnerability.
       SA 2707. Mr. McCONNELL (for Mr. Alexander) proposed an 
     amendment to the bill S. 1636, to amend the Federal Food, 
     Drug, and Cosmetic Act with respect to the scope of new 
     chemical exclusivity.
```
