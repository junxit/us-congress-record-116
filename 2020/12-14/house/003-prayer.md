# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Patrick J. Conroy, offered the following 
prayer:
  Lord of us all, we give You thanks for giving us another day.
  As leaders continue to pursue agreement on omnibus and further 
coronavirus relief legislation, we ask Your generous gifting of wisdom 
for all those so engaged. The day-to-day well being of so many 
Americans depends upon the success of their work.
  As vaccines begin to be distributed throughout the country, we thank 
You again for the wonderful work of medical scientists, and the ongoing 
heroic work of those who care for the sick, and who will be among the 
first inoculated. May the vaccines prove to be efficacious, and thus 
bring hope to our world.
  Today, as well, the electoral college will do its work verifying the 
results of our national election in November. May the genius of our 
Founders be revealed as the world witnesses the glory of self-
government in our electoral system.
  May all that is done this day be for Your greater honor and glory.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
