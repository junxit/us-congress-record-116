# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive Message transmitted by the President to the Senate on March 
5, 2020 withdrawing from further Senate consideration the following 
nomination:

       KATHERINE CAMILLE HENDERSON, OF TENNESSEE, TO BE CHIEF OF 
     PROTOCOL, AND TO HAVE THE RANK OF AMBASSADOR DURING HER 
     TENURE OF SERVICE, VICE SEAN P. LAWLER, RESIGNED, WHICH WAS 
     SENT TO THE SENATE ON FEBRUARY 27, 2020.
```
