# COMMUNICATION FROM THE SERGEANT AT ARMS

```
                COMMUNICATION FROM THE SERGEANT AT ARMS

  The SPEAKER laid before the House the following communication from 
the Sergeant at Arms of the House of Representatives:

                                   Office of the Sergeant at Arms,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                Washington, DC, September 8, 2020.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Madame Speaker: This is to notify you formally, 
     pursuant to rule VIII of the Rules of the House of 
     Representatives, that I, Paul D. Irving, in my capacity as 
     Chair of the Capitol Police Board, have been served with a 
     subpoena for testimony issued by the United States District 
     Court for District of Columbia.
       After consultation with the Office of General Counsel, I 
     have determined that compliance with the subpoena is 
     consistent with the privileges and rights of the House.
           Sincerely,
                                                   Paul D. Irving,
     Sergeant at Arms.

                          ____________________
```
