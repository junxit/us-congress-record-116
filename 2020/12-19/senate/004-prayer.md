# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, our souls long for You, for we find strength and joy in 
Your presence. The Heavens keep telling of your wonders, and the skies 
declare what You have done. We thank You for the star of Bethlehem.
  Guide our lawmakers to put their trust in You, seeking in every 
undertaking to know and do Your will. Lord, when our Senators go 
through difficulties, may they remember that with Your help, they can 
accomplish the seemingly impossible. Inspire them to find strength and 
courage from Your guidance, as they trust You to direct their steps.
  We pray in Your strong Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
