# REMEMBERING OFFICER KAIA GRANT

**Speaking:** Wenstrup, Brad R. (W000815) [R-OH]

```
                     REMEMBERING OFFICER KAIA GRANT

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. BRAD R. WENSTRUP

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, April 7, 2020

  Mr. WENSTRUP. Madam Speaker, I rise to honor fallen Springdale police 
officer and hero Kaia Grant, who gave her life in the line of duty.
  Growing up in Wyoming, Ohio, friends, family, and peers knew Officer 
Grant was destined for greatness. Officer Grant exemplified the best of 
what our nation has to offer--commitment to fighting for justice and 
protecting our people. She was a dedicated public servant who lost her 
life trying to help others.
  The loss of Officer Grant is deeply felt in Wyoming, in Springdale, 
and across Hamilton County. Community members have displayed blue 
hearts on their front doors in memory of her and in solidarity with the 
Springdale Police Department.
  I pray that God will bring comfort to Officer Grant's family, to the 
law enforcement community, and to all those in the Hamilton County 
community grieving her loss. While God has called Kaia Grant away from 
us, the positive impact of her service shall never die.
  God bless.

                          ____________________
```
