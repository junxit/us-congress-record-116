         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XIII, reports of committees were delivered to

     Committee of the Whole House on the state of the Union.
       Mr. McGOVERN: Committee on Rules. House Resolution 981.

     amendments to the bill (H.R. 6172) to amend the Foreign

     production of certain business records, and for other 
     purposes (Rept. 116-426). Referred to the House Calendar.
