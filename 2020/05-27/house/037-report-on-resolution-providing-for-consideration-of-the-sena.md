# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF THE SENATE AMENDMENTS TO H.R. 6172, USA FREEDOM REAUTHORIZATION ACT OF 2020

**Measures:** H.Res. 981, H.R. 6172, S. 6172

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF THE SENATE 
    AMENDMENTS TO H.R. 6172, USA FREEDOM REAUTHORIZATION ACT OF 2020

  Mr. McGOVERN, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 116-426) on the resolution (H. Res. 981) providing 
for consideration of the Senate amendments to the bill (H.R. 6172) to 
amend the Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Act of 1978 to prohibit the 
production of certain business records, and for other purposes, which 
was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
