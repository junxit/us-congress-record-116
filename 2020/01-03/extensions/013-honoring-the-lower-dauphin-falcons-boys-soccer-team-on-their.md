# HONORING THE LOWER DAUPHIN FALCONS' BOYS SOCCER TEAM ON THEIR CHAMPIONSHIP SEASON

**Speaking:** Perry, Scott (P000605) [R-PA]

```
     HONORING THE LOWER DAUPHIN FALCONS' BOYS SOCCER TEAM ON THEIR 
                          CHAMPIONSHIP SEASON

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. SCOTT PERRY

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, January 3, 2020

  Mr. PERRY. Madam Speaker, today I offer my heartfelt congratulations 
to the Lower Dauphin Falcons on their inspiring play at the PIAA 3A 
State Soccer Championship.
  Led by coach Gerry Lynch, the Falcons were a force to be reckoned 
with in their 2019 campaign, winning 18 of 22 contests for a final 
record of 18-3-1.
  This is the Falcons' fourth championship season in five years, and 
their first win since 2017. This year, the Falcons contested 
successfully against Northern York's stout defense, scoring three goals 
to close out the first half. The Falcons held their advantage for the 
remainder of the game.
  These remarkable young men put in their full effort and resolve this 
season--and we are incredibly proud of them for their performance, 
success, sportsmanship and teamwork.
  On behalf of Pennsylvania's Tenth Congressional District, I commend 
and congratulate the Lower Dauphin Falcons on their dedication to 
excellence. We look forward to their continued success.

                          ____________________
```
