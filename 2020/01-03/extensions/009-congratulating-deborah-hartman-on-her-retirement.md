# CONGRATULATING DEBORAH HARTMAN ON HER RETIREMENT

**Speaking:** Meuser, Daniel (M001204) [R-PA]

```
            CONGRATULATING DEBORAH HARTMAN ON HER RETIREMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DANIEL MEUSER

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, January 3, 2020

  Mr. MEUSER. Madam Speaker, it is with great respect that I rise today 
to recognize Deborah A. Hartman of Berks County. Mrs. Hartman is 
retiring after over 40 years of dedicated public service with the 
Bureau of Alcohol, Tobacco & Firearms and the Defense Contract 
Management Agency.
  Mrs. Hartman began her lengthy federal government service in 1975, 
during the Gerald Ford administration with the ATF. In 1985, she joined 
the Defense Contract Management Agency. There she served in a variety 
of positions starting in an administrative role and rising through the 
ranks, to her current position as Management & Program Analyst for the 
Quality Division of Defense Contract Management Agency. Throughout her 
career, Mrs. Hartman has received numerous awards and accolades, her 
dedication to her work and to the safety and our nation is evident.
  On behalf of the U.S. House of Representatives and the citizens of 
Pennsylvania's Ninth Congressional District, I ask my colleagues to 
join me in thanking Mrs. Deborah Hartman for her many years of 
dedicated and honorable service, and I wish her the best in the next 
chapter of her life. I am confident that even though she is retiring, 
she will not slow down and she will continue to make many valuable 
contributions to our Pennsylvania commu-
nities.
```
