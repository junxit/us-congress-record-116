# CONGRATULATING SHERIFF RAY C. GERRINGER ON HIS RETIREMENT

**Speaking:** Meuser, Daniel (M001204) [R-PA]

```
       CONGRATULATING SHERIFF RAY C. GERRINGER ON HIS RETIREMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DANIEL MEUSER

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, January 3, 2020

  Mr. MEUSER. Madam Speaker, it is with great respect that I rise today 
to recognize Sheriff Ray C. Gerringer of Montour County. Sheriff 
Gerringer is retiring after 40 years of dedicated service to the 
Montour County community. His bravery, compassion, and commitment to 
public safety has made our communities safer and stronger.
  A lifelong resident of Montour County, Sheriff Gerringer has been a 
leader in law enforcement throughout his long tenure of service to our 
great Commonwealth. After graduating from Columbia-Montour Vocational-
Technical School, he began serving the citizens of Montour County in 
1977 as a part-time dispatcher. Shortly after he served as a full-time 
correctional officer at the county prison.
  In 1986, Sheriff Gerringer became the Chief County Detective for the 
Montour County District Attorney's office. There he participated in 
hundreds of criminal investigations that resulted in convictions. 
Sheriff Gerringer was elected Montour County Sheriff in 2004 a position 
he has honorably held for the last 15 years.
  Sheriff Gerringer has dedicated his life to protecting and defending 
the Montour County community. The many years he has spent on the 
frontlines of public safety demonstrates his selflessness. He has truly 
answered the call to serve.
  On behalf of the U.S. House of Representatives and the citizens of 
Pennsylvania's Ninth Congressional District, I ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating Sheriff Gerringer on his retirement and thank 
him for his many years of dutiful and honorable service.

                          ____________________
```
