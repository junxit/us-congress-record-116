# HONORING THE LOWER DAUPHIN LADY FALCONS ON AN OUTSTANDING PERFORMANCE IN THE PENNSYLVANIA INTERSCHOLASTIC ATHLETIC ASSOCIATION 3A FIELD HOCKEY CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Perry, Scott (P000605) [R-PA]

```
 HONORING THE LOWER DAUPHIN LADY FALCONS ON AN OUTSTANDING PERFORMANCE 
   IN THE PENNSYLVANIA INTERSCHOLASTIC ATHLETIC ASSOCIATION 3A FIELD 
                          HOCKEY CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. SCOTT PERRY

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, January 3, 2020

  Mr. PERRY. Madam Speaker, today I offer my heartfelt congratulations 
to the Lower Dauphin Lady Falcons on their inspiring play at the PIAA 
3A Field Hockey Championship.
  Led by legendary coach Linda Kreiser, the Lady Falcons were a force 
to be reckoned with in their 2019 campaign, winning 23 of 25 contests 
for a final record of 23-0-2.
  Since 1997, the Lady Falcons have won ten (10) 3A Championships, with 
their most recent win in 2018. Hoping to repeat their success, the Lady 
Falcons fought valiantly towards a 1-1 tie after regulation. The Wilson 
Bulldogs emerged as the victors following a spirited 3-2 shootout.
  The dedication and teamwork of the Lady Falcons says much about their 
character--individually, and as a team. ``Semper Stellas Spectemus''--
literally translated, means, ``may we always reach for the stars.'' 
That's exactly what the Lady Falcons did, and I know will continue to 
do.
  On behalf of Pennsylvania's Tenth Congressional District, I commend 
and congratulate the Lower Dauphin Lady Falcons on their dedication to 
excellence, sportsmanship, teamwork and success.
```
