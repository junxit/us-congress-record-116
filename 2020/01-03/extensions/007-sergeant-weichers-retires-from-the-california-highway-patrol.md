# SERGEANT WEICHERS RETIRES FROM THE CALIFORNIA HIGHWAY PATROL

**Speaking:** Cook, Paul (C001094) [R-CA]

```
      SERGEANT WEICHERS RETIRES FROM THE CALIFORNIA HIGHWAY PATROL

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. PAUL COOK

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, January 3, 2020

  Mr. COOK. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize the outstanding 
law enforcement career of California Highway Patrol Sergeant Todd 
Weichers, who retired on December 27, 2019 after 31 years of service.
  After completing CHP Academy in 1989, Sergeant Weichers was assigned 
as a traffic officer to the Westminster Area. He spent five years at 
this first assignment area before being transferred to the Barstow 
Area. In 2008, Officer Weichers was promoted to Sergeant Weichers, and 
shortly thereafter was transferred to the Mariposa Area. After a brief 
stint back in the Barstow Area, he was transferred to the Victorville 
Area in 2011 where he has spent the remainder of his career.
  On behalf of the U.S. House of Representatives, I offer my 
compliments to Sergeant Weichers for his leadership, hard work, and 
dedication to keeping our roads safe. While retiring from a career is 
never easy, I wish him and his family a relaxing and fulfilling 
retirement.

                          ____________________
```
