# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.Res. 935

```
Committee Meetings
ESTABLISHING A SELECT SUBCOMMITTEE ON THE CORONAVIRUS CRISIS AS A SELECT 
INVESTIGATIVE SUBCOMMITTEE OF THE COMMITTEE ON OVERSIGHT AND REFORM
Committee on Rules: Full Committee held a hearing on H. Res. 935, 
establishing a Select Subcommittee on the Coronavirus Crisis as a select 
investigative subcommittee of the Committee on Oversight and Reform. The 
Committee granted, by record vote of 7-4, a rule that provides for the 
adoption of H. Res. 935, Establishing a Select Subcommittee on the 
Coronavirus Crisis as a select investigative subcommittee of the Committee 
on Oversight and Reform. Testimony was heard from Representative Jordan.
Joint Meetings
  No joint committee meetings were held.
F
```
