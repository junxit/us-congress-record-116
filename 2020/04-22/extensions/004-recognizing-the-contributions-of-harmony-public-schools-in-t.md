# RECOGNIZING THE CONTRIBUTIONS OF HARMONY PUBLIC SCHOOLS IN THE FIGHT AGAINST THE CORONAVIRUS

**Speaking:** Olson, Pete (O000168) [R-TX]

```
 RECOGNIZING THE CONTRIBUTIONS OF HARMONY PUBLIC SCHOOLS IN THE FIGHT 
                        AGAINST THE CORONAVIRUS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. PETE OLSON

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, April 22, 2020

  Mr. OLSON. Madam Speaker, I rise today to commend Harmony Public 
School for their efforts to protect our hospital heroes on the front 
lines fighting the coronavirus.
  As COVID-19 has rocked our communities and pushed our hospitals into 
overdrive, our healthcare professionals have faced a critical shortage 
of personal protective equipment, or PPE, such as face masks and rubber 
gloves. PPE provides critical protection for our doctors and nurses, 
shielding them from diseases like coronavirus and other biohazards.
  Seeing our heroes needing help, Harmony Public School used their 3-D 
printer to create 2,000 plastic face shields over the course of six 
weeks, using modern technology to help medical professionals working to 
save lives.
  On behalf of the Twenty-Second Congressional District of Texas, we 
send our sincerest thanks to the men and women at Harmony Public School 
for doing their part to help our community during these challenging 
times.
```
