# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Lord of Heaven's Army, we find our joy from trusting You. Today we 
are trusting Your promise to supply all our needs from Your celestial 
riches.
  Lord, as we differ in faces, so we are different in our needs. 
Provide for the myriad needs of our Nation and world. Bring healing to 
the sick, comfort to those who grieve, and wisdom to those who seek to 
meet the challenges of a global health crisis.
  Lord, give our lawmakers Your strength. We claim Your promise that 
You will not withhold any good thing from those who do what is right.
  We pray in Your great Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
