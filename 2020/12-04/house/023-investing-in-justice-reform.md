# INVESTING IN JUSTICE REFORM

**Speaking:** Hall, Kwanza (H001092) [D-GA]

```
                      INVESTING IN JUSTICE REFORM

  (Mr. HALL asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. HALL. Mr. Speaker, I rise in support of the MORE Act, and I urge 
our colleagues in the Senate to do the same.
  There is a serious need for justice reform in our country, a focus on 
restorative financial justice. Investment in research, along with 
descheduling, is something that we must lead on.
  COVID innovation zones in our country might be a strategy for 
bringing bipartisan solutions that create jobs but also dealing with 
this terrible pandemic.

                          ____________________
```
