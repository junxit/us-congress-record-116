# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JANUARY 16, 2020

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** H.R. 5430

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JANUARY 16, 2020

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today it adjourn until 9:45 a.m., January 
16; further, that following the prayer and pledge, the morning hour be 
deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the 
time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, 
and morning business be closed; further, that following leader remarks, 
the Senate resume consideration of H.R. 5430 under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
