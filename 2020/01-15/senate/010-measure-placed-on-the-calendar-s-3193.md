# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 3193

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** S. 3193

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 3193

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I understand that there is a bill at 
the desk that is due for a second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the title of the bill for 
the second time.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 3193) to amend the Controlled Substances Act to 
     list fentanyl-related substances as schedule I controlled 
     substances, and for other purposes.

  Mr. McCONNELL. In order to place the bill on the calendar under the 
provisions of rule XIV, I object to further proceedings.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection having been heard, the bill will be 
placed on the calendar.
```
