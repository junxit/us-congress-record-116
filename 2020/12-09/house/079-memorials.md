# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 24, H.Con.Res. 30, H.Res. 283

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       ML-218. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the House of 
     Representatives of the State of Louisiana, relative to House 
     Concurrent Resolution No. 24, memorializing the Congress and 
     the Louisiana congressional delegation to take such actions 
     as are necessary to fully fund the Livestock Indemnity 
     Program in response to the negative impact created by losses 
     to the Louisiana livestock industry as a result of Hurricane 
     Laura and Hurricane Delta; to the Committee on Agriculture.

[[Page H7111]]

       ML-219. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Louisiana, relative to House Concurrent 
     Resolution No. 30, memorializing the Congress to take such 
     action as are necessary to pass a stimulus plan that includes 
     funds for unemployment, housing, local government, struggling 
     businesses, education, and health care; to the Committee on 
     Appropriations.
       ML-220. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Michigan, relative to House Resolution No. 283, 
     urging the Congress of the United States to Allocate Funding 
     for States That Have Established Broadband and Expansion 
     Block Grant Programs; to the Committee on Energy and 
     Commerce.

                          ____________________
```
