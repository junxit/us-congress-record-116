  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the next nomination.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read the nomination of Shana 
M. Broussard, of Louisiana, to be a Member of the Federal Election 
Commission for a term expiring April 30, 2023.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, all postcloture time 
is expired.

nomination?

is necessarily absent.
