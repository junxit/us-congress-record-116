# Vote on Dickerson Nomination (Executive Calendar)

**Speaking:** Hawley, Josh (H001089) [R-MO], Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                      Vote on Dickerson Nomination

  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Sasse). Under the previous order, the 
question is, Will the Senate advise and consent to the Dickerson 
nomination?
  Mr. HAWLEY. Mr. President, I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk called the roll.
  Mr. THUNE. The following Senators are necessarily absent: the Senator 
from Georgia (Mrs. Loeffler), the Senator from Georgia (Mr. Perdue), 
and the Senator from South Dakota (Mr. Rounds).
  Mr. DURBIN. I announce that the Senator from California (Ms. Harris) 
is necessarily absent.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Are there any other Senators in the Chamber 
desiring to vote?
  The result was announced--yeas 49, nays 47, as follows:
```
