# RECOGNIZING AMBER HARBIN

**Speaking:** Hill, J. French (H001072) [R-AR]

```
                        RECOGNIZING AMBER HARBIN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. J. FRENCH HILL

                              of arkansas

                    in the house of representatives

                          Monday, June 8, 2020

  Mr. HILL of Arkansas. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
accomplishments of Amber Harbin, a science teacher at Forest Heights 
Stem Academy.
  Amber was recently named the Little Rock School District's 2020 
Teacher of the Year.
  This award recognizes a devotion to education, with high-quality 
teaching, community service, and leadership.
  As a result of her hard work, Amber was also awarded the Marian G. 
Lacey Educator of the Year Award, a top award within the Little Rock 
School District.
  Amber's dedication to her students has been evident throughout her 
13-year career as a science teacher most recently at Forest Heights 
Stem Academy.
  Amber Harbin's commitment to education is one all Americans and 
Arkansans can admire.
  I'd like to thank Amber, and all of the dedicated educators 
throughout Arkansas, for service and leadership in these trying times 
for central Arkansas's communities.

                          ____________________
```
