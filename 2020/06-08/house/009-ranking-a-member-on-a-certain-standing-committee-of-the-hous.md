# RANKING A MEMBER ON A CERTAIN STANDING COMMITTEE OF THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

**Measures:** H.Res. 995

```
   RANKING A MEMBER ON A CERTAIN STANDING COMMITTEE OF THE HOUSE OF 
                            REPRESENTATIVES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the Chair lays before the 
House the following resolution (H. Res. 995) ranking a Member on a 
certain standing committee of the House of Representatives.
  The Clerk read the title of the resolution.
  There was no objection.
  The text of the resolution is as follows:

                              H. Res. 995

       Resolved, That the following named Member be, and is 
     hereby, ranked as follows on the following standing committee 
     of the House of Representatives:
       Committee on Oversight and Reform: Mr. Mfume (to rank 
     immediately after Mr. Khanna).

  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
