# TRIBUTE TO KYLIE WATSON

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO KYLIE WATSON

● Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I would like to take the 
opportunity to express my appreciation to Kylie for her hard work as an 
intern in my Casper office. I recognize her efforts and contributions 
to my office, as well as to the State of Wyoming.
  Kylie is a native of Casper. She is a student at Casper College, 
where she is studying pre-medicine. She has demonstrated a strong work 
ethic, which has made her an invaluable asset to our office. The 
quality of her work is reflected in her great efforts over the last 
several months.
  I want to thank Kylie for the dedication she has shown while working 
for me and my staff. It was a pleasure to have her as part of our team. 
I know she will have continued success with all of her future 
endeavors. I wish her all my best on her next journey.●

                          ____________________
```
