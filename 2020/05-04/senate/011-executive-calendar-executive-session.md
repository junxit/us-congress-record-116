# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY], Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will 
proceed to executive session to consider the following nomination, 
which the clerk will report.
  The legislative clerk read the nomination of Robert J. Feitel, of 
Maryland, to be Inspector General, Nuclear Regulatory Commission.
  Mr. McCONNELL. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
