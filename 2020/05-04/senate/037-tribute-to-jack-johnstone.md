# TRIBUTE TO JACK JOHNSTONE

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO JACK JOHNSTONE

● Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I would like to take the 
opportunity to express my appreciation to Jack for his hard work as an 
intern in the Environment and Public Works Committee. I recognize his 
efforts and contributions to my office, as well as to the State of 
Wyoming.
  Jack is a native of Colorado. He is a graduate of the University of 
Colorado Boulder, where he studied human geography. He has demonstrated 
a strong work ethic, which has made him an invaluable asset to our 
office. The quality of his work is reflected in his great efforts over 
the last several months.
  I want to thank Jack for the dedication he has shown while working 
for me and my staff. It is a pleasure to have him as part of our team. 
I know he will have continued success with all of his future endeavors. 
I wish him all my best on his journey.●

                          ____________________
```
