# RECOGNITION OF EDWARD WANG, 2019 CONGRESSIONAL APP WINNER

**Speaking:** Biggs, Andy (B001302) [R-AZ]

```
       RECOGNITION OF EDWARD WANG, 2019 CONGRESSIONAL APP WINNER

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ANDY BIGGS

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, January 30, 2020

  Mr. BIGGS. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor Edward Wang, winner 
of the Congressional App Challenge for Arizona's Fifth District. This 
competition is offered to middle and high school students nationwide in 
search for an original app that is programmed to provide a service 
beneficial to society.
  Mr. Wang's app, ProAssign, embraces the App Challenge's mission: 
Inspire, Include, Innovate. ProAssign inspires students to improve 
their math and problem-solving skills by using a modern resource. Mr. 
Wang's submission includes each student's understanding of mathematics 
by assigning additional practice problems to strengthen their skills. 
His app enables teachers to innovate their teaching methods by 
modernizing the learning environment to better relate to today's 
students.
  I have no doubt that Mr. Wang will continue to inspire our nation's 
youth to imagine bold solutions to the educational challenges of the 
twenty-first century. I am honored to have such talent and leadership 
in my district.

                          ____________________
```
