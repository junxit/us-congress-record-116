# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal Redeemer, provide our lawmakers with Your grace, mercy, and 
peace. By Your grace, may they forget the challenges behind them. By 
Your mercy, may they reach for the opportunities that beckon. By Your 
peace, may they possess an equanimity of temperament during life's 
fluctuating intricacies.
  Lord, give them a passion for truth and a reluctance to major in 
minors. Use their exemplary lives to inspire people to live with faith, 
purpose and power.
  Remind us all that though we may plan, You determine what will 
finally happen.
  We pray in Your Great Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
