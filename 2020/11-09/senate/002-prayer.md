# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Sovereign God, You are wisdom without end and mercy without limit. We 
worship You.
  Lord, have compassion on our Nation. Heal our physical, mental, and 
spiritual wounds. We are worn out from this challenging season. Restore 
us to the vision of a liberating freedom that enables justice to 
flourish. Hear the prayers of our Senators, and give them Your peace. 
And, Lord, thank You for the degree of resolution that has occurred in 
our Nation's election process. Remind us all of the wisdom of Mark 3:25 
that says, ``A house divided against itself cannot stand.''
  We pray in Your sovereign Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
