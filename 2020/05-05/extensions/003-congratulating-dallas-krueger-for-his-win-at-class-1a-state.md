# CONGRATULATING DALLAS KRUEGER FOR HIS WIN AT CLASS 1A STATE FINALS FOR WRESTLING AT THE IHSA STATE TOURNAMENT

**Speaking:** Bustos, Cheri (B001286) [D-IL]

```
CONGRATULATING DALLAS KRUEGER FOR HIS WIN AT CLASS 1A STATE FINALS FOR 
                 WRESTLING AT THE IHSA STATE TOURNAMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. CHERI BUSTOS

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                          Tuesday, May 5, 2020

  Mrs. BUSTOS. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize Dallas Krueger, 
a senior from Rockridge High School, who won the Illinois High School 
Association's Class 1A State Finals Wrestling Tournament in the 138 
pound weigh class.
  At 138 pounds, Dallas pinned his opponent in four minutes and forty 
seconds to finish 47-0. As a former athlete, I appreciate the fortitude 
Dallas displayed to achieve his dream of being a state champion and 
commend him for his efforts and attitude. Dallas is an example of the 
importance of dedication and a strong work ethic, and I am proud to see 
him represent Taylor Ridge so well throughout the state with his talent 
and passion.
  It is because of student leaders such as Dallas Krueger that I am 
especially proud to serve Illinois' 17th Congressional District. Madam 
Speaker, I would like to again formally congratulate Dallas Krueger on 
his Class 1A State Finals title in Wrestling.

                          ____________________
```
