# LORRAINE MADDEN

**Speaking:** Norcross, Donald (N000188) [D-NJ]

```
                            LORRAINE MADDEN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. DONALD NORCROSS

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 15, 2020

  Mr. NORCROSS. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor and commend the 
life and legacy of the late Gloucester County, New Jersey resident Mrs. 
Lorraine Madden.
  Born in l933, in the mining town Shamokin, Pennsylvania. Lorraine 
Madden was the third born child of Clarence and Marie Greager. In the 
1940's the family moved to South West Philadelphia where she attended 
catholic school and began working at the age of fifteen to contribute 
to her family.
  Mrs. Lorraine Madden married Fred Madden, Sr. and had two sons, Fred 
Jr. and Thomas. She worked at a local hospital, Bell Telephone, Harleco 
Company, A.E. Price Importers and lastly in human resources for Johns 
Manville. After retirement, Lorraine and Fred relocated to Surfside 
Beach, South Carolina for the next twenty years. After being many miles 
away from their family and friends they decided to move back to New 
Jersey.
  The residents of Gloucester County, New Jersey greatly adored and 
appreciated Mrs. Madden. Her passing is a great loss for our entire 
community. She is the beloved wife of Fred H. Madden, Sr., devoted 
mother of the Honorable Fred. H. Madden, Jr. (Patricia) and Thomas 
Madden, Sr. (Margaret). Loving grandmother of Patricia, Maureen, 
Thomas, Hillary, Tara, Frederick, III, Catherine and Margaret. Proud 
great-grandmother of Jace, Kaylee-Jo, Alyssa, Ava-Jo, Jeffrey, Declan, 
Everly and William.
  Madam Speaker, I ask you to join me in honoring the late Lorraine 
Madden, of Gloucester County, New Jersey, a loved wife, mother and 
grandmother.

                          ____________________
```
