# AUTHORIZING THE PRESIDENT TO POSTHUMOUSLY AWARD THE MEDAL OF HONOR TO ALWYN C. CASHE FOR ACTS OF VALOR DURING OPERATION IRAQI FREEDOM

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** H.R. 8276

```
 AUTHORIZING THE PRESIDENT TO POSTHUMOUSLY AWARD THE MEDAL OF HONOR TO 
    ALWYN C. CASHE FOR ACTS OF VALOR DURING OPERATION IRAQI FREEDOM

  Mr. McCONNELL. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Senate proceed to the immediate consideration of H.R. 8276, which was 
received from the House.

[[Page S6655]]

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 8276) to authorize the President to 
     posthumously award the Medal of Honor to Alwyn C. Cashe for 
     acts of valor during Operation Iraqi Freedom.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mr. McCONNELL. I ask unanimous consent that the bill be considered 
read a third time and passed and the motion to reconsider be considered 
made and laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (H.R. 8276) was ordered to a third reading, was read the 
third time, and passed.
```
