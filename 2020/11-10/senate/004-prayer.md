# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, by Your grace, give authority to the godly that this 
Nation can thrive. Continue to raise up people who have reverence for 
You and desire to do Your will.
  Lord, give our Senators the wisdom to live blameless lives that 
please You. May they find joy in Your presence as they trust the 
unfolding of Your loving providence. Lord, empower them to use their 
integrity to bring stability to our Nation and world.
  And Lord, thank You for the opportunity tomorrow to honor our 
veterans and celebrate their bravery and sacrifice.
  We pray in Your strong Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
