# 2020 ELECTIONS

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                             2020 ELECTIONS

  Mr. THUNE. Madam President, we are now a week on from the election, 
but there are still a lot of outstanding races to determine. Votes are 
still being counted or will soon be recounted in many States. The 
Presidential race has yet to be certified, and it is important to 
remember that while media outlets can predict a winner, official 
results do come later, and there are still House and Senate races that 
have yet to be decided.
  One thing is clear, though: The blue wave that Democrat pollsters and 
the media predicted was coming did not arrive. Americans emphatically 
did not endorse the socialist aims of the far left. Instead, they sent 
a much more moderate message. And I hope that when all the votes are 
counted and the outcomes of all the races are determined, Democrats 
will be able to overcome the virulent partisanship that has 
characterized their behavior over the past 4 years and work with 
Republicans to deliver results for the American people

                          ____________________
```
