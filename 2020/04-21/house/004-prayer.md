# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Patrick J. Conroy, offered the following 
prayer:
  God of mercy, thank You for giving us another day.
  Inspire the Members of this people's House, as they continue to work 
from home, to bring comfort and assistance in their districts as they 
are able to do.
  As the habits and customs of the Nation change, may those who mourn 
the loss of loved ones, those who mourn the loss of lifelong businesses 
look for the open window of futures we never needed to imagine before.
  While some States begin to loosen precautionary measures, give wisdom 
and good judgment to America's citizens.
  Please inspire the governors in this country to make wise judgments, 
mindful of the important balance of concerns for health as well as 
economy.
  May everything done this day be for Your greater honor and glory.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
