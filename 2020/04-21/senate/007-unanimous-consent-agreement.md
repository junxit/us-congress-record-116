# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                      UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent there now be up 
to 15 minutes of debate each for Senators Schumer, Paul, Cardin, Lee, 
and Sullivan; further, that the time be for debate only and upon the 
use or yielding back of their individual times, I be immediately 
recognized with no intervening action.

  Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
