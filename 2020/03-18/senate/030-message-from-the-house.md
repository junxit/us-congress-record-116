# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 6201

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE


                          enrolled bill signed

  At 5:03 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that the Speaker pro 
tempore (Mr. Hoyer) has signed the following enrolled bill:

       H.R. 6201. An act making emergency supplemental 
     appropriations for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2020, 
     and for other purposes.

  The enrolled bill was subsequently signed by the President pro 
tempore (Mr. Grassley).
```
