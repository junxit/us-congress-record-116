# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Holy God, we love You. You are our strength, rock, and protection. 
Use our Senators today to make our Nation safer and better. May they 
often think of the positive legacy they can lead through faithful 
service to You and country.
  Lord, remind them that You have brought our Nation through much 
greater difficulties than the ones we now face. As they strive to unite 
for the common good of this land we love, may they embrace Your promise 
that You will never leave or forsake us.
  We pray in Your merciful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
