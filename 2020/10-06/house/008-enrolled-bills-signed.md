# ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

**Measures:** H.R. 991, H.R. 1812, H.R. 2372, H.R. 4779, H.R. 6168

```
                         ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

  Cheryl L. Johnson, Clerk of the House, reported and found truly 
enrolled bills of the House of the following titles, which were 
thereupon signed by the Speaker:

       H.R. 991. An act to extend certain provisions of the 
     Caribbean Basin Economic Recovery Act until September 30, 
     2030, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 1812. An act to amend title 38, United States Code, to 
     furnish Vet Center readjustment counseling and related mental 
     health services to certain individuals.
       H.R. 2372. An act to direct the Comptroller General of the 
     United States to conduct an assessment of all memoranda of 
     understanding and memoranda of agreement between Under 
     Secretary of Health and non-Department of Veterans Affairs 
     entities relating to suicide prevention and mental health 
     services.
       H.R. 4779. An act to extend the Undertaking Spam, Spyware, 
     And Fraud Enforcement With Enforcers beyond Borders Act of 
     2006, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 6168. An act to increase, effective as of December 1, 
     2020, the rate of compensation for veterans with service-
     connected disabilities and the rates of dependency and 
     indemnity compensation for the survivors of certain disabled 
     veterans, and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
