# SENATE ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

**Measures:** S. 457

```
                      SENATE ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  The Speaker announced her signature to an enrolled bill of the Senate 
of the following title:

       S. 457.--An act to require that $1 coins issued during 2019 
     honor George H.W. Bush and to direct the Secretary of the 
     Treasury to issue bullion coins during 2019 in honor of
```
