# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, for the information of Senators, under 
the previous order, at 12 noon the Senate will receive the managers of 
the House of Representatives to exhibit the Articles of Impeachment 
against Donald John Trump, President of the United States.

quorum being present, the Sergeant at Arms will present the managers on
```
