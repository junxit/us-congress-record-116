# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--FILING TRIAL BRIEFS

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
            UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--FILING TRIAL BRIEFS

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. Chief Justice, I ask unanimous consent that if the 
House of Representatives wishes to file a trial brief, it be filed with 
the Secretary of the Senate by 5 p.m. on Saturday, January 18, 2020; 
further, that if the President wishes to file a trial brief, it be 
filed with the Secretary of the Senate by 12 noon on Monday, January 
20, 2020; further, that if the House wishes to file a rebuttal brief, 
it be filed with the Secretary of the Senate by 12 noon on Tuesday, 
January 21, 2020. Finally, I ask unanimous consent that the Secretary 
of the Senate be authorized to print as a Senate document all documents 
filed by the parties together, to be available for all parties.
  The CHIEF JUSTICE. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
