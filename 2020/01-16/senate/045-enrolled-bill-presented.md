# ENROLLED BILL PRESENTED

**Measures:** S. 457

```
                        ENROLLED BILL PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on today, January 16, 2020, 
she had presented to the President of the United States the following 
enrolled bill:

       S. 457. An act to require that $1 coins issued during 2019 
     honor President George H.W. Bush and to direct the Secretary 
     of the Treasury to issue bullion coins during 2019 in honor 
     of Barbara Bush.
```
