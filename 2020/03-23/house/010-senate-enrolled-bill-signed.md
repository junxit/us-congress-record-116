# SENATE ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

**Measures:** S. 3503

```
                      SENATE ENROLLED BILL SIGNED

  The Speaker pro tempore, Mr. Brown of Maryland, on Friday, March 20, 
2020, announced his signature to an enrolled bill of the Senate of the 
following title:

       S. 3503.--A bill to authorize the Secretary of Veterans 
     Affairs to treat certain programs of education converted to 
     distance learning by reason of emergencies and health-related 
     situations in the same manner as programs of education 
     pursued at educational institutions, and for other purposes.
```
