# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Heavenly Father, give our lawmakers wisdom and spiritual eyes to see 
You at work. Remind them that, in everything, You are working for the 
good of those who love You.
  Guide our Senators to strive to please You by living blameless, holy, 
and disciplined lives.
  As they battle this coronavirus pandemic, may they not forget the 
marginalized.
  Lord, give our lawmakers a hunger for Your words and a desire to 
apply Your knowledge in their daily work.
  We pray in Your merciful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
