# MEASURES REFERRED

**Measures:** H.R. 6168, H.R. 6509

```
                           MEASURES REFERRED

  The following bills were read the first and the second times by 
unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 6168. An act to increase, effective as of December 1, 
     2020, the rates of compensation for veterans with service-
     connected disabilities and the rates of dependency and 
     indemnity compensation for the survivors of certain disabled 
     veterans, and for other purposes; referred to the Committee 
     on Veterans' Affairs.
       H.R. 6509. An act to amend the Omnibus Crime Control and 
     Safe Streets Act of 1968 to provide public safety officer 
     death and disability benefits for certain public safety 
     officers who contract COVID-19, and for other purposes; 
     referred to the Committee on the Judiciary.
```
