# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JUNE 2, 2020

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                    ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JUNE 2, 2020

  Mr. McCONNELL. Now, Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when 
the Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m.; 
further, that following the prayer and pledge, the morning hour be 
deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the 
time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, 
and morning business be closed; further, that following leader remarks, 
the Senate proceed to executive session to resume consideration of the 
Mercado nomination; finally, that the Senate recess following the 
cloture vote on Mercado until 2:15 p.m. to allow for the weekly 
conference meetings.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
