# Introductory Statement on S.J. Res. 65

**Speaking:** Boozman, John (B001236) [R-AR]

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 65

```
      By Mr. BOOZMAN (for himself, Mr. Perdue, and Mr. Leahy):
  S.J. Res. 65. A joint resolution providing for the reappointment of 
John Fahey as a citizen regent of the Board of Regents of the 
Smithsonian Institution; considered and passed.

                              S.J. Res. 65

       Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
     United States of America in Congress assembled, That, in 
     accordance with

[[Page S128]]

     section 5581 of the Revised Statutes (20 U.S.C. 43), the 
     vacancy on the Board of Regents of the Smithsonian 
     Institution, in the class other than Members of Congress, 
     occurring by reason of the expiration of the term of John 
     Fahey of Massachusetts on February 20, 2020, is filled by the 
     reappointment of the incumbent. The reappointment is for a 
     term of six years, beginning on the later of February 20, 
     2020, or the date of the enactment of this joint resolution.
                                 ______
```
