# PROVIDING FOR THE APPOINTMENT OF DENISE O'LEARY AS A CITIZEN REGENT OF THE BOARD OF REGENTS OF THE SMITHSONIAN INSTITUTION

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 66

```
PROVIDING FOR THE APPOINTMENT OF DENISE O'LEARY AS A CITIZEN REGENT OF 
          THE BOARD OF REGENTS OF THE SMITHSONIAN INSTITUTION

  The joint resolution (S.J. Res. 66) providing for the appointment of 
Denise O'Leary as a citizen regent of the Board of Regents of the 
Smithsonian Institution, was ordered to be engrossed for a third 
reading, was read the third time, and passed, as follows:

                              S.J. Res. 66

       Resolved by the Senate and House of Representatives of the 
     United States of America in Congress assembled, That, in 
     accordance with section 5581 of the Revised Statutes (20 
     U.S.C. 43), the vacancy on the Board of Regents of the 
     Smithsonian Institution, in the class other than Members of 
     Congress, occurring by reason of the resignation of Barbara 
     M. Barrett of Arizona on October 17, 2019, is filled by the 
     appointment of Denise O'Leary of Colorado. The appointment is 
     for a term of six years, beginning on the date of the 
     enactment of this joint resolution

                          ____________________
```
