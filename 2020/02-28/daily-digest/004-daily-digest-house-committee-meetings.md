# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 149, H.R. 4351, H.R. 5187, H.R. 5929, H.R. 5930, H.R. 5931, H.R. 5932

```
 Committee Meetings
MISCELLANEOUS MEASURES
Committee on Financial Services: Full Committee concluded a markup on 
H.R. 149, the ``Housing Fairness Act of 2020''; H.R. 4351, the ``Yes In 
My Backyard Act''; H.R. 5187, the ``Housing Is Infrastructure Act''; 
H.R. 5929, the ``Shareholder Political Transparency Act''; H.R. 5930, 
the ``Workforce Investment Disclosure Act''; H.R. 5931, the ``Improving 
FHA Support for Small Dollar Mortgages Act of 2020''; H.R. 5932, the 
``Ensuring Chinese Debt Transparency Act''; Views and Estimates of the 
Committee on Financial Services on Matters to be Set Forth in the 
Concurrent Resolution on the Budget for Fiscal Year 2021 Budget; and a 
resolution electing minority Members to the subcommittees of the 
Committee on Financial Services. H.R. 5931, H.R. 4351, H.R. 149, H.R. 
5929, H.R. 5930, H.R. 5187, and H.R. 5932 were ordered reported, as 
amended. The Views and Estimates for the Fiscal Year 2021 Budget 
Resolution was agreed to, as amended. A resolution electing minority 
Members to the subcommittees of the Committee on Financial Services was 
agreed to.
EVALUATING THE TRUMP ADMINISTRATION'S POLICIES ON IRAN, IRAQ AND THE 
USE OF FORCE
Committee on Foreign Affairs: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``Evaluating the Trump Administration's Policies on Iran, Iraq and the 
Use of Force''. Testimony was heard from Michael R. Pompeo, Secretary, 
Department of State.

Joint Meetings
  No joint committee meetings were held.
```
