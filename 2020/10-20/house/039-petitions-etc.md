# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, petitions and papers were laid on the 
clerk's desk and referred as follows:

       PT-143. The SPEAKER presented a petition of the Township of 
     Mount Olive, New Jersey, relative to urging the State of New 
     Jersey to provide direct stabilization funding to Morris Cony 
     from the Coronavirus Relief Fund in an amount consistent with 
     the allocation made to counties that have populations 
     slightly over 500,000; to the Committee on Oversight and 
     Reform.
       PT-144. Also, a petition of the Wyoming County, NY Board of 
     Supervisors, relative to Resolution No. 20-345, calling on 
     United States Congressional Representatives to demand direct 
     assistance to County and Local Governments, and to upgrade 
     rural Internet services; to the Committee on Oversight and 
     Reform.
       PT-145. Also, a petition of the Township of Saddle Brook, 
     NJ, relative to Resolution 820-146, strongly supporting and 
     requesting that federal aid be provided to all states, 
     counties and municipal governments, as this is a national 
     emergency being deeply felt by all Americans in all 50 states 
     and US territories; to the Committee on Oversight and Reform.
       PT-146. Also, a petition of Mr. Christopher Glenn Sawyer, a 
     citizen of Atlanta, GA, relative to Dealing with a Contested 
     Election: Impeachment as a Strategy; to the Committee on the 
     Judiciary.
```
