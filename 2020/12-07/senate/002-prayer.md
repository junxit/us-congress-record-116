# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Great Redeemer, guide our lawmakers along the best path to fulfill 
Your purposes on Earth. May they submit to Your wisdom and providential 
leading, believing that You will supply all of their needs. Remind them 
that Your unfailing love accompanies those who put their trust in You.
  Lord, give our Senators an attitude of reverential awe that will keep 
them from evil and inspire them to rejoice and be glad as they strive 
to live lives of purity and obedience.
  And, Lord, as we remember Pearl Harbor on this December 7, thank You 
for Your loving and prevailing providence. May we show our gratitude by 
facing the future without fear.
  We pray in Your majestic Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
