# ORDER OF BUSINESS

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** H.R. 6172

```
                           ORDER OF BUSINESS

  Mr. McCONNELL. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that 
following leader remarks on Wednesday, May 13, the Senate proceed to 
the consideration of H.R. 6172 under the order of March 16. I further

by-side amendment to the Daines amendment, if offered. I further ask
```
