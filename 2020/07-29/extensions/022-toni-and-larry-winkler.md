# TONI AND LARRY WINKLER

**Speaking:** Perlmutter, Ed (P000593) [D-CO]

```
                         TONI AND LARRY WINKLER

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ED PERLMUTTER

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 29, 2020

  Mr. PERLMUTTER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize Toni and 
Larry Winkler for receiving the Arvada Chamber's Image Award.
  Toni and Larry Winkler employ over 100 people across their three 
local businesses--WL Contractors, Steel Affairs and the Lamar Street 
Center. They continuously give back to the community by offering non-
profit, military and political discounts, and hosting and suppotiing 
events such as the 9Health Fair, which provides free and low-cost 
health services to the community.
  In addition, the Winklers generously donate goods and services to 
local non-profits such as Food for Thought, Community Table, Ralston 
House, Police K9 Association, Morgan Adams Foundation and the Jefferson 
County Business Education Alliance.
  I extend my deepest congratulations to Toni and Larry Winkler for 
this well-deserved award and appreciate their contribution to our 
community.

                          ____________________
```
