# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MARCH 18, 2020

**Speaking:** Rounds, Mike (R000605) [R-SD]

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MARCH 18, 2020

  Mr. ROUNDS. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m. 
Wednesday, March 18; further that following the prayer and pledge, the 
morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceeding be approved 
to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and morning business be closed; finally, following leader 
remarks, the Senate be in a period of morning business with Senators 
permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
