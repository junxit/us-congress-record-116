# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MAY 18, 2020

**Speaking:** Cornyn, John (C001056) [R-TX]

```
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MAY 18, 2020

  Mr. CORNYN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 3 p.m., Monday, 
May 18; further, that following the prayer and pledge, the morning hour 
be deemed expired and the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and morning business be closed; further, that following leader 
remarks, the Senate proceed to executive session to resume the 
consideration of the Rash nomination. Finally, notwithstanding rule 
XXII, the cloture vote on the Rash nomination occur at 5:30 p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
