# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 7579

**Measures:** H.R. 7579

```
           By Mr. BURGESS:
       H.R. 7579.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The attached legislation falls under Congress' enumerated 
     Constitutional authority to impose taxes pursuant to Article 
     I, Section 8, Clause 1 and regulate the properties of the 
     United States pursuant to Article IV, Section 3, Clause 2.
```
