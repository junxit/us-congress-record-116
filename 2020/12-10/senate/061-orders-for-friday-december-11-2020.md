# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, DECEMBER 11, 2020

**Speaking:** Murkowski, Lisa (M001153) [R-AK]

**Measures:** H.R. 6395

```
                  ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, DECEMBER 11, 2020

  Ms. MURKOWSKI. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m., 
Friday, December 11, 2020; further, that following the prayer and 
pledge, the morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings 
be approved to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their 
use later in the day, and morning business be closed; finally, that 
following leader remarks, the Senate resume consideration of the 
conference report to accompany H.R. 6395.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
