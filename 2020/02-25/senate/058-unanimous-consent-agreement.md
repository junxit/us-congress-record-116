# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                      UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT

  Mr. McCONNELL. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent to modify the 
order of January 31 to allow Senators to have until Thursday, February 
27, 2020, to have printed statements and opinions in the Congressional 
Record, if they choose, explaining their votes and include those in the 
documentation of the impeachment proceedings; finally, I ask that the 
two-page rule be waived.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
