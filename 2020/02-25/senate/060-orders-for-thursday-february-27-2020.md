# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, FEBRUARY 27, 2020

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, FEBRUARY 27, 2020

  Mr. McCONNELL. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m., 
Thursday, February 27; further, that following the prayer and pledge, 
the morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their 
use later in the day, and morning business be closed; finally, 
following leader remarks, the Senate proceed to executive session and 
resume consideration of the Greaves nomination, under the previous 
order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
