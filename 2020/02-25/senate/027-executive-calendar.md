consent to the nomination of Katharine MacGregor, of Pennsylvania, to 
be Deputy Secretary of the Interior?
  Mr. HAWLEY. Mr. President, I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
