# TRIBUTE TO MALACHI MILLER

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO MALACHI MILLER

● Mr. BARRASSO. Madam President, I would like to take the 
opportunity to express my appreciation to Malachi for his hard work as 
an intern in the Senate Republican Conference. I recognize his efforts 
and contributions to my office as well as to the State of Wyoming.
  Malachi is a native of Casper. He is a student at the University of 
Wyoming, where he is studying economics. He has demonstrated a strong 
work ethic, which has made him an invaluable asset to our office. The 
quality of his work is reflected in his great efforts over the last 
several months.
  I want to thank Malachi for the dedication he has shown while working 
for me and my staff. It is a pleasure to have him as part of our team. 
I know he will have continued success with all of his future endeavors. 
I wish him all my best on his journey.●

                          ____________________
```
