# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, thank You for the gift of this day. Open the minds of 
our lawmakers and give them a vision of the unlimited possibilities 
available to those who trust You as their guide. May our Senators 
embrace a humility that seeks first to understand rather than to be 
understood. Inspire them to seek Your wisdom as You sustain them with 
Your grace, mercy, and peace. Lord, remind them that You will judge 
their fruits, productivity, and faithfulness.
  We pray in Your strong Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
