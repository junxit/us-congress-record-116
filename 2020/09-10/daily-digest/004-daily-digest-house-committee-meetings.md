# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 3450, H.R. 3788, H.R. 3826, H.R. 6092, H.R. 7469, H.R. 7504, H.R. 7541, H.R. 7747, H.R. 7784, H.R. 7879, H.R. 7888, H.R. 7964, H.R. 8005, H.R. 8033, H.R. 8068, H.R. 8084, H.R. 8107, H.R. 8108, H.R. 8130, H.R. 8144, H.R. 8145, H.R. 8147, H.R. 8148, H.R. 8149

Committee Meetings
MACHINES, ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE, AND THE WORKFORCE: RECOVERING AND 
READYING OUR ECONOMY FOR THE FUTURE
Committee on the Budget: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``Machines, Artificial Intelligence, and the Workforce: Recovering and 
Readying Our Economy for the Future''. Testimony was heard from Jason 
Matheny, Commissioner, National Security Commission on Artificial 
Intelligence; and public witnesses.
BUSINESS MEETING
Committee on Education and Labor: Full Committee held a business 
meeting to approve new subcommittee assignments and a subcommittee 
Ranking Member. Subcommittee assignments and subcommittee Ranking 
Member were approved.
ON THE BASIS OF SEX: EXAMINING THE ADMINISTRATION'S ATTACKS ON GENDER-
BASED PROTECTIONS
Committee on Education and Labor: Subcommittee on Civil Rights and 
Human Services held a hearing entitled ``On the Basis of Sex: Examining 
the Administration's Attacks on Gender-Based Protections''. Testimony 
was heard from public witnesses.
THE NEED FOR FINANCIAL AID TO AMERICA'S STATES AND TERRITORIES DURING 
THE PANDEMIC: SUPPORTING FIRST RESPONDERS, ASSISTING SCHOOLS IN THEIR 
EFFORTS TO SAFELY EDUCATE, AND PREVENTING MASS LAYOFFS
Committee on Financial Services: Full Committee held a hearing entitled 
``The Need for Financial Aid to America's States and Territories During 
the Pandemic: Supporting First Responders, Assisting Schools in Their 
Efforts to Safely Educate, and Preventing Mass Layoffs''. Testimony was 
heard from Michelle Lujan Grisham, Governor, New Mexico; Tim Walz, 
Governor, Minnesota; Laura Kelly, Governor, Kansas; Lourdes Leon 
Guerrero, Governor, Territory of Guam; and a public witness.

[[Page D778]]


PROTECTING DEMOCRACY DURING COVID-19 IN EUROPE AND EURASIA AND THE 
DEMOCRATIC AWAKENING IN BELARUS
Committee on Foreign Affairs: Subcommittee on Europe, Eurasia, Energy, 
and the Environment held a hearing entitled ``Protecting Democracy 
During COVID-19 in Europe and Eurasia and the Democratic Awakening in 
Belarus''. Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
EXAMINING THE BUREAU OF INDIAN EDUCATION'S SCHOOL REOPENING GUIDANCE 
DURING THE COVID-19 PANDEMIC
Committee on Natural Resources: Subcommittee for Indigenous Peoples of 
the United States held a hearing entitled ``Examining the Bureau of 
Indian Education's School Reopening Guidance During the COVID-19 
Pandemic''. Testimony was heard from Lance Witte, Superintendent and 
Principal, Lower Brule Schools, South Dakota; and public witnesses.
PROVIDING THE CENSUS BUREAU WITH THE TIME TO PRODUCE A COMPLETE AND 
ACCURATE CENSUS
Committee on Oversight and Reform: Full Committee held a hearing 
entitled ``Providing the Census Bureau with the Time to Produce a 
Complete and Accurate Census''. Testimony was heard from J. Christopher 
Mihm, Managing Director, Strategic Issues Team, Government 
Accountability Office; and public witnesses.
KICK STARTING ENTREPRENEURSHIP AND MAIN STREET ECONOMIC RECOVERY
Committee on Small Business: Subcommittee on Rural Development, 
Agriculture, Trade, and Entrepreneurship held a hearing entitled ``Kick 
Starting Entrepreneurship and Main Street Economic Recovery''. 
Testimony was heard from public witnesses.
BUSINESS MEETING
Committee on Veterans' Affairs: Full Committee held a business meeting 
to Reauthorize the Women Veterans' Task Force. The Women Veterans' Task 
Force was reauthorized.
LEGISLATIVE MEASURES
