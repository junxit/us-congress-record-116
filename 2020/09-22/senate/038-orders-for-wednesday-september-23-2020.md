# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 23, 2020

**Speaking:** Hawley, Josh (H001089) [R-MO]

```
                ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, SEPTEMBER 23, 2020

  Mr. HAWLEY. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m., 
Wednesday, September 23; further, that following the prayer and pledge, 
the morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their 
use later in the day, and morning business be closed; finally, that 
following leader remarks, the Senate proceed to executive session to 
resume consideration of the Hinderaker nomination under the previous 
order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
