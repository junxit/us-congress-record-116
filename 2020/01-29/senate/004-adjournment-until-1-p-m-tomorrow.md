# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 1 P.M. TOMORROW

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                   ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 1 P.M. TOMORROW

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. Chief Justice, I ask unanimous consent that the 
trial adjourn until 1 p.m., Thursday, tomorrow, January 30, and this 
order also constitute the adjournment of the Senate.
  There being no objection, at 11:05 p.m., the Senate, sitting as a 
Court of Impeachment, adjourned until Thursday, January 30, 2020, at 1 
p.m.
```
