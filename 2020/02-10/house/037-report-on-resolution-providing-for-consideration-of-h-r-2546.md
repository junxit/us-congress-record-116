REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2546, COLORADO

 REMOVING DEADLINE FOR RATIFICATION OF EQUAL RIGHTS AMENDMENT; AND FOR

report (Rept. No. 116-395) on the resolution (H. Res. 844) providing

Preservation System, and for other purposes; providing for 
consideration of the joint resolution (H.J. Res. 79) removing the 
deadline for the ratification of the equal rights amendment; and for 
other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to 
be printed.
