# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 7259

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** H.R. 7259

```
               MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 7259

  Mr. McCONNELL. Madam President, I understand there is a bill at the 
desk due for a second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill by title for the 
second time.

       An act (H.R. 7259) to allow acceleration certificates
```
