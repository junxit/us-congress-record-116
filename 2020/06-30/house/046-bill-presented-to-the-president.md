# BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 7010

```
                    BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Cheryl L. Johnson, Clerk of the House, reported that on June 5, 2020, 
she presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, 
the following bill.

       H.R. 7010. To amend the Small Business Act and the CARES 
     Act to modify certain provisions related to the forgiveness 
     of loans under the paycheck protection program, to allow 
     recipients of loan forgiveness under the paycheck protection 
     program to defer payroll taxes, and for other purposes.
```
