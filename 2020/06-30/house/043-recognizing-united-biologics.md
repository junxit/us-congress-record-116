# RECOGNIZING UNITED BIOLOGICS

**Speaking:** Correa, J. Luis (C001110) [D-CA]

```
                      RECOGNIZING UNITED BIOLOGICS

  (Mr. CORREA asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. CORREA. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize United Biologics 
in Santa Ana, who stepped up during the pandemic to help our local 
healthcare workers.
  After I called on local manufacturers in my district to retool and 
manufacture PPE, United Biologics' President, John Barnhill, stepped up 
to the plate. The company designed a face shield, ordered supplies, and 
even added more employees to their workforce to produce thousands of 
face shields. Not only did this effort keep the company running and in 
business, but John Barnhill decided to donate the PPE to the local 
healthcare workers.
  United Biologics shows what happens when we call on American 
companies to do the right thing.

                          ____________________
```
