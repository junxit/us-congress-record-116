# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 7421

**Measures:** H.R. 7421

```
            By Mr. DUNCAN:
        H.R. 7421.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 17 grants Congress the 
     authority to pass laws related to ``needful buildings'' on 
     lands owned by the federal government.
```
