# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 9, S.Res. 107

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       170. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the Legislature of 
     the State of Alaska, relative to House Joint Resolution 9, 
     requesting the United States Secretary of the Treasury to 
     mint not less than 5,000,000 $1 coins honoring Elizabeth 
     Peratrovich under the Native American Coin Act; to the 
     Committee on Financial Services.
       171. Also, a memorial of the Senate of the State of 
     Michigan, relative to Senate Resolution No. 107, urging the 
     Federal Government to fund the medical costs associated with 
     testing and mandatory hospital stays for the novel 
     coronavirus; to the Committee on Energy and Commerce.

                          ____________________
```
