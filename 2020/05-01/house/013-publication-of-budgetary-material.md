                   PUBLICATION OF BUDGETARY MATERIAL


  AGGREGATES, ALLOCATIONS, AND OTHER BUDGETARY LEVELS FOR FISCAL YEAR 
                                  2021

                                         House of Representatives,


                                      Committee on the Budget,

                                      Washington, DC, May 1, 2020.
       Madam Speaker: Pursuant to the Bipartisan Budget Act of 
     2019 and Congressional Budget Act of 1974, I hereby submit 
     for printing in the Congressional Record: (1) aggregate 
     spending levels for fiscal year 2021 and aggregate revenue 
     levels for fiscal year 2021 and for the period of fiscal 
     years 2021 through 2030, (2) allocations for fiscal year 2021 
     for the House Committee on Appropriations, (3) committee 
     allocations for fiscal year 2021 and for the period of fiscal 
     years 2021 through 2030 for all committees other than the 
     Committee on Appropriations, and (4) a list of discretionary 
     accounts identified for advance appropriations in fiscal year 
     2021 appropriations bills. The authorizing committee 
     allocation is divided into current law amounts and 
     reauthorization. Reauthorization refers to amounts assumed in 
     CBO's baseline for legislation that would extend current law 
     provisions.
       This filing is made for technical purposes as required by 
     the Bipartisan Budget Act of 2019, and the budgetary material 
     published herein is for the purposes of enforcing titles III 
     and IV of the Congressional Budget Act of 1974 and other 
     budgetary enforcement provisions. If there are any questions, 
     please contact Jennifer Wheelock or Raquel Spencer of the 
     Budget Committee staff.
                                                     John Yarmuth.
