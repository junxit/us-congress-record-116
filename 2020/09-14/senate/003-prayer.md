# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Mighty God, teach us to trust You more fully, to wait quietly for the 
unfolding of Your providence, to pray fervently, and to worship more 
humbly. Reassure us with Your wisdom, power, and love that Your 
providence will prevail.
  Lord, You are our shelter for every storm. Remind our lawmakers to 
remember how Your purposes have prevailed in our Nation's history so 
they need not fear for the future.
  Lord, provide them with a firm faith that will enable them to meet 
the daunting challenges of these times. Lengthen their moments of 
prayer until their lives become a continual aspiration after You.
  We pray in Your matchless Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
