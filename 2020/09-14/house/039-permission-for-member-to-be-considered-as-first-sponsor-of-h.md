# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 3114

**Speaking:** Craig, Angie (C001119) [D-MN]

**Measures:** H.R. 3114

```
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 3114

  Ms. CRAIG. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may hereafter 
be considered to be the first sponsor of H.R. 3114, a bill originally 
introduced by Representative Lewis of Georgia, for the purposes of 
adding cosponsors and requesting reprintings pursuant to clause 7 of 
rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Rose of New York). Is there objection to 
the request of the gentlewoman from Minnesota?
  There was no objection.
```
