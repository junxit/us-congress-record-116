# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Patrick J. Conroy, offered the following 
prayer:
  Merciful God we give You thanks for giving us another day.
  As the Members of the people's House return to Washington, keep them 
safe in their travels. You know well the dangers confronting the 
citizens of our Nation: extreme weather events, ongoing pandemic 
effects, economic distress and impending loss of employment, and even 
housing.
  Fill the Members with wisdom, patience, and determination to address 
these needs as so many dangers continue to close in on our communities.
  May all that is done this day be for Your greater honor and glory.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
