# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal Lord God, we again thank You for the life and legacy of 
Representative John Robert Lewis.
  Lord, accept our gratitude for his ethical congruence that motivated 
him to find a moral way to get in the way. We are grateful that his 
passion for freedom summoned our Nation to embrace our best hopes and 
not our worst fears. Inspired by his exemplary pilgrimage, may our 
lawmakers strive to live with such integrity that they will stand on 
the right side of history.
  Lord, help our Senators to strive to see You more clearly, to love 
You more dearly, and to follow You more nearly day by day.
  We pray in Your sovereign Name.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
