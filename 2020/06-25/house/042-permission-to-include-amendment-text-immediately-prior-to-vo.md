# PERMISSION TO INCLUDE AMENDMENT TEXT IMMEDIATELY PRIOR TO VOTE ON PREVIOUS QUESTION ON HOUSE RESOLUTION 1017

**Speaking:** Woodall, Rob (W000810) [R-GA]

**Measures:** H.Res. 1017

```
   PERMISSION TO INCLUDE AMENDMENT TEXT IMMEDIATELY PRIOR TO VOTE ON 
               PREVIOUS QUESTION ON HOUSE RESOLUTION 1017

  Mr. WOODALL. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I be permitted 
to insert the text of the amendment I would have offered had the House 
rejected the previous question on House Resolution 1017, along with 
extraneous material, into the Congressional Record immediately prior to 
the vote on ordering the previous question on House Resolution 1017.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Cunningham). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from Georgia?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
