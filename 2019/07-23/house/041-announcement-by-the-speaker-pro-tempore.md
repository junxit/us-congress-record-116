# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.Res. 246, H.R. 549, H.R. 3304

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Ms. DelBene). Proceedings will resume on 
questions previously postponed. Votes will be taken in the following 
order:
  Motions to suspend the rules and
  Agree to H. Res. 246;
  Pass H.R. 549;
  Pass H.R. 3304; and
  Agreeing to the Speaker's approval of the Journal, if ordered.
  The first electronic vote will be conducted as a 15-minute vote. 
Pursuant to clause 9 of rule XX, remaining electronic votes will be 
conducted as 5-minute votes.

                          ____________________
```
