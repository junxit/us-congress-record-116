# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Lord of the harvest, we continue to seek You, for we desire to please 
You. You, O God, are our light and salvation, so we refuse to be 
afraid. As our lawmakers seek to walk with integrity, provide them with 
a harvest of truth, justice, and righteousness. May they cultivate such 
ethical consistency that their words will be undergirded by right 
actions.
  Lord, keep them aware of Your continued presence as they find in You 
fullness of joy. Show them the path to life, as You guide them to Your 
desired destination.
  We pray in Your merciful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
