# RECOGNIZING LE GAUB FOR THE 2019 MONTANA CONGRESSIONAL VETERAN COMMENDATION

**Speaking:** Gianforte, Greg (G000584) [R-MT]

```
    RECOGNIZING LE GAUB FOR THE 2019 MONTANA CONGRESSIONAL VETERAN 
                              COMMENDATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. GREG GIANFORTE

                               of montana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 14, 2019

  Mr. GIANFORTE. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize Le Gaub of 
Bozeman, a recipient of the 2019 Montana Congressional Veteran 
Commendation for his service to his country and leadership in his 
community.
   Mr. Gaub is a veteran of the U.S. Army. In his distinguished service 
from 1972 to 2003, He earned multiple commendations, including the 
Legion of Merit, Meritorious Service Medal, Reserve Component 
Achievement Medal, National Defense Service Medal, and Armed Forces 
Reserve Medal.
   Serving his community through Troops to Teachers, Mr. Gaub works as 
a program manager to secure funding for military veterans seeking to 
transition to careers in K-12 education. He is a friendly face around 
many schools in Montana and in veteran support groups and nonprofits 
where he invests much of his time. Since his start with the Troops to 
Teachers program, he has helped bring in a substantial amount of grants 
ensuring its success.
   I ask colleagues to join me today in commending Le Gaub for his 
dedication and service.
```
