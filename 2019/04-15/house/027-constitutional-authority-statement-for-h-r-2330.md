# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 2330

**Measures:** H.R. 2330

```
           Mr. CARBAJAL:
       H.R. 2330.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       ``The constitutional authority of Congress to enact this 
     legislation is provided by Article I, Section 8, Clause 14 
     which provides Congress with the power to make rules for the 
     government and regulation of the land and naval forces.''
```
