# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** S. 1379

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE


                          enrolled bill signed

  At 3:54 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Novotny, one of its reading clerks, announced that the Speaker 
has signed the following enrolled bill:

       S. 1379. An act to reauthorize certain programs under the 
     Public Health Service Act and the Federal Food, Drug, and 
     Cosmetic Act with respect to public health security and all-
     hazards preparedness and response, and for other purposes.

  The enrolled bill was subsequently signed by the President pro 
tempore (Mr. Grassley).

                          ____________________
```
