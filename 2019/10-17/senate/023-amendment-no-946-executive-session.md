# Amendment No. 946 (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                           Amendment No. 946

  Mr. McCONNELL. I have an amendment at the desk and ask the clerk to 
report.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the amendment.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       The Senator from Kentucky [Mr. McConnell] proposes an 
     amendment numbered 946 to Treaty Doc. No. 116-1.

  The amendment (No. 946) is as follows:

       At the end add the following.
       ``This Treaty shall be effective 1 day after the date of 
     ratification.''

  Mr. McCONNELL. I ask for the yeas and nays on my amendment.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The yeas and nays have been ordered.
```
