# TRIBUTE TO JACOB TARRELL

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO JACOB TARRELL

● Mr. THUNE. Madam President, today I recognize Jacob Tarrell, 
an intern in my Aberdeen, SD, office, for all of the hard work he has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
several months.
  Jacob is a graduate of Hot Springs High School in Hot Springs, SD. 
Currently, he is attending Presentation College in Aberdeen, where he 
is majoring in sports and event management. He is a hard worker who has 
been dedicated to getting the most out of his internship experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Jacob for all of the 
fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
