# APPOINTMENT

```
                              APPOINTMENT

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the Vice President, 
pursuant to 14 U.S.C. 194(a), as amended by Public Law 101-595, and 
further amended by Public Law 113-281, appoints the following Senator 
to the Board of Visitors of the U.S. Coast Guard Academy: The Honorable 
Roger Wicker of Mississippi.
```
