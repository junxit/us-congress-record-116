     3256. A bill to amend the Homeland Security Act of 2002 to

     Standards Program, and for other purposes; with an amendment

     research on the science of suicide, and to advance the 
     knowledge and understanding of issues that may be associated

     extrinsic factors related to areas such as wellbeing, 
     resilience, and vulnerability; with an amendment (Rept. 116-
     342). Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on the 
     state of the Union.
       Ms. VELAZQUEZ: Committee on Small Business. H.R. 5065. A

     entrepreneurship counseling and training services for 
     formerly incarcerated individuals, and for other purposes 
     (Rept. 116-343). Referred to the Committee of the Whole House 
     on the state of the Union.
       Ms. VELAZQUEZ: Committee on Small Business. H.R. 5078. A 
     bill to amend the Small Business Act to provide re-entry 
     entrepreneurship counseling and training services for 
     incarcerated individuals, and for other purposes (Rept. 116-
     344). Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on the
