# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 1, S.J.Res. 2

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       37. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the Legislature of 
     the State of Wyoming, relative to Senate Joint Resolution 2, 
     requesting Congress to take action relating to management of 
     federal lands to promote multiple uses of public lands and 
     state involvement in federal resource management; which was 
     referred to the Committee on Natural Resources.
       38. Also, a memorial of the Legislature of the State of 
     Wyoming, relative to House Joint Resolution 1, requesting the 
     swift delisting under the Endangered Species Act of the 
     Greater Yellowstone Ecosystem grizzly bear population; 
     requesting a return of the species management to local 
     control; and requesting full federal funding of species 
     management until delisting occurs; which was referred to the 
     Committee on Natural Resources.
```
