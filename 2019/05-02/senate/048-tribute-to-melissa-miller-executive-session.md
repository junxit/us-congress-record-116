# Tribute to Melissa Miller (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Blackburn, Marsha (B001243) [R-TN]

```
                       Tribute to Melissa Miller

  Mr. President, Senator Sullivan was mentioning his Teacher of the 
Year from Alaska. As I begin my remarks about some wonderful things 
that have happened with women and for women and by women in this 
country, I want to mention that I just left a visit with our Tennessee 
Teacher of the Year--Melissa Miller from Columbia--who teaches in 
nearby Franklin at the elementary school. We are thrilled for her and 
are honored to have her here in DC. I join in praising her for the 
great work that she does for children by encouraging them to learn how 
they best learn so that they can live their versions of the American 
dream.
```
