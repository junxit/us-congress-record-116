# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 3 P.M. MONDAY

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** S.Res. 31

```
                    ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 3 P.M. MONDAY

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I ask that it stand adjourned under the provisions 
of S. Res. 31 and do so as a further mark of respect for the late 
Harris Wofford, Jr., former Senator for the Commonwealth of 
Pennsylvania.

[[Page S685]]

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order and pursuant to S. 
Res. 31, the Senate stands adjourned until 3 p.m. on Monday, January 
28, and does so as a further mark of respect for the late Harris L. 
Wofford, Jr., former Senator from Pennsylvania.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 4:42 p.m., adjourned until 
Monday, January 28, 2019, at 3 p.m.
```
