# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** S. 863

```
           MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT


                          Enrolled Bill Signed

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 3, 2019, 
the Secretary of the Senate, on March 29, 2019, during the adjournment 
of the Senate, received a message from the House of Representatives 
announcing that the Speaker had signed the following enrolled bill:

       S. 863. An act to amend title 38, United States Code, to 
     clarify the grade and pay of podiatrists of the Department of 
     Veterans Affairs.

                          ____________________
```
