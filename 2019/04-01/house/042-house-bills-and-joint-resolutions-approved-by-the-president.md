# HOUSE BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS APPROVED BY THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 28, H.J.Res. 31, H.R. 251, H.R. 259, H.R. 430, H.R. 439

```
      HOUSE BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS APPROVED BY THE PRESIDENT

  The President notified the Clerk of the House that on the following 
dates he had approved and signed bills and joint resolutions of the 
following titles:

           January 18, 2019:
       H.R. 251. An Act to extend by 15 months the Chemical 
     Facility Anti-Terrorism Standards Program of the Department 
     of Homeland Security, and for other purposes.
           January 24, 2019:
       H.R. 259. An Act to extend the Medicaid Money Follows the 
     Person Rebalancing demonstration, to extend protection for 
     Medicaid recipients of home and community-based services 
     against spousal impoverishment, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 430. An Act to extend the program of block grants to 
     States for temporary assistance for needy families and 
     related programs through June 30, 2019.
           January 25, 2019:
       H.J. Res. 28. A joint resolution making further continuing 
     appropriations for fiscal year 2019, and for other purposes.

           February 15, 2019:
       H.J. Res. 31. A joint resolution making consolidated 
     appropriations for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2019, 
     and for other purposes.
           February 21, 2019:
       H.R. 439. An Act to amend the charter of the Future Farmers 
     of America, and for other purposes.
```
