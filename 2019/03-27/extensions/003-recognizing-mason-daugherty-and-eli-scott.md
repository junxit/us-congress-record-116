# RECOGNIZING MASON DAUGHERTY AND ELI SCOTT

**Speaking:** Taylor, Van (T000479) [R-TX]

```
               RECOGNIZING MASON DAUGHERTY AND ELI SCOTT

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. VAN TAYLOR

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, March 27, 2019

  Mr. TAYLOR. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize Mason Daugherty 
and Eli Scott from McKinney, Texas on winning the 2019 C-SPAN video 
documentary competition.
   Their documentary, ``What it Means to be American: Citizen 
Accountability in Government'' highlights how the United States 
Constitution intended to instill power within the American people and 
how corruption within our system of government diverts that power away 
from individuals.
   Like these impressive 11th graders from Imagine International 
Academy of North Texas, I too believe power must rest with we the 
people. Working together, we can bring about real change in Washington.
   Madam Speaker, I ask that my colleagues in the House of 
Representatives join me today in congratulating Mason Daugherty and Eli 
Scott on their achievement.

                          ____________________
```
