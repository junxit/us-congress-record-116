# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 1919

**Measures:** H.R. 1919

```
           By Mr. KELLY of Mississippi:
       H.R. 1919.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The Congress enacts this bill pursuant to Section 8 of 
     Article I of the United States Constitution, which provides 
     Congress with the ability to enact legislation necessary and 
     proper to effectuate its purposes in taxing and spending.
```
