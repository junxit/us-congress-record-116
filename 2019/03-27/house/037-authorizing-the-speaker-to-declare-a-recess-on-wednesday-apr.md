# AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE A RECESS ON WEDNESDAY, APRIL 3, 2019, FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING IN JOINT MEETING HIS EXCELLENCY JENS STOLTENBERG, SECRETARY GENERAL OF THE NORTH ATLANTIC...

**Speaking:** Payne, Donald M. (P000604) [D-NJ]

```
  AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE A RECESS ON WEDNESDAY, APRIL 3, 
2019, FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING IN JOINT MEETING HIS EXCELLENCY JENS 
      STOLTENBERG, SECRETARY GENERAL OF THE NORTH ATLANTIC TREATY 
                              ORGANIZATION

  Mr. PAYNE. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that it may be in 
order at any time on Wednesday, April 3, 2019, for the Speaker to 
declare a recess, subject to the call of the Chair, for the purpose of 
receiving in joint meeting His Excellency Jens Stoltenberg, Secretary 
General of the North Atlantic Treaty Organization.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mrs. Craig). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentleman from New Jersey?
  There was no objection.
```
