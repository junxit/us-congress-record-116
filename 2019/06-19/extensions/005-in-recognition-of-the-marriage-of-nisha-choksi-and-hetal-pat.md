# IN RECOGNITION OF THE MARRIAGE OF NISHA CHOKSI AND HETAL PATEL

**Speaking:** Stevens, Haley M. (S001215) [D-MI]

```
     IN RECOGNITION OF THE MARRIAGE OF NISHA CHOKSI AND HETAL PATEL

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. HALEY M. STEVENS

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 19, 2019

  Ms. STEVENS. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize Nisha Choksi 
and Hetal Patel on their remarkable relationship and to wish them well 
on a lifetime of marriage.
   Nisha and Hetal were wed in a beautiful ceremony on May 11, 2019 in 
Atlanta, Georgia. Surrounded by friends and family from across the 
globe, their three days of festivities symbolize their deep commitment 
to one another and their future life.
   Their wedding symbolizes the beginning of a journey that will carry 
them through the joys and hardships of life. Their ability to work 
together professionally and grow only closer is another testament to 
the powerful bond they share.
   Madam Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating 
Nisha Choksi and Hetal Patel for embarking on a new chapter in their 
lives and wishing them well on their journey together.

                          ____________________
```
