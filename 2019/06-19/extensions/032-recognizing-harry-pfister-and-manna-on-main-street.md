# RECOGNIZING HARRY PFISTER AND MANNA ON MAIN STREET

**Speaking:** Fitzpatrick, Brian K. (F000466) [R-PA]

```
           RECOGNIZING HARRY PFISTER AND MANNA ON MAIN STREET

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. BRIAN K. FITZPATRICK

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 19, 2019

  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize an 
outstanding citizen from Montgomery County, Pennsylvania, who is 
working tirelessly to make our community a better place.
  Master Harry Pfister, a 7th Degree Black Belt who serves as Vice 
President of Villari's Pennsylvania and Owner and Chief Instructor at 
Villari's of Chalfont, has been using his karate expertise as a way to 
assist a nonprofit organization in Lansdale. Since the 1990s, Master 
Pfister has hosted a ``Kick-A-Thon,'' which to date has raised over 
$100,000 for Manna on Main Street. This year, $10,500 was raised.
  I applaud Master Pfister for his work, and appreciate the commitment 
of Manna on Main Street to ending food insecurity in the North Penn 
region.
```
