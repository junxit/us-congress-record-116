# TRIBUTE TO CHILDREN'S HOSPITAL LOS ANGELES

**Speaking:** Schiff, Adam B. (S001150) [D-CA]

```
               TRIBUTE TO CHILDREN'S HOSPITAL LOS ANGELES

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. ADAM B. SCHIFF

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 19, 2019

  Mr. SCHIFF. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor Children's Hospital 
Los Angeles for being ranked first among children's hospitals in 
California and across the Western U.S., in addition to being named one 
of the top five children's hospitals in the nation, according to U.S. 
News and World Report's Honor Roll of Best Children's Hospitals. The 
talented doctors, nurses, volunteers, and staff at Children's Hospital 
Los Angeles provide every child with the best care possible, and have 
touched the lives of countless families across Los Angeles, including 
mine.
  Children's Hospital Los Angeles has been providing excellent 
pediatric care to the Los Angeles area since 1901, and today offers 
more than 350 specialty programs and services to meet the needs of some 
of the community's most vulnerable members. In addition to its patient 
services, Children's Hospital Los Angeles supports innovative research 
efforts which impact children across the nation and around the world. 
When it comes to children's health and well-being, Children's Hospital 
Los Angeles's quality of care is truly unparalleled.
  I am proud to recognize every individual at Children's Hospital Los 
Angeles for their unyielding commitment to caring for the children of 
Los Angeles. I ask all members to join me in commending Children's 
Hospital Los Angeles for its compassionate, exceptional pediatric care.

                          ____________________
```
