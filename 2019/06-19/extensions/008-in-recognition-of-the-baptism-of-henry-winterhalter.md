# IN RECOGNITION OF THE BAPTISM OF HENRY WINTERHALTER

**Speaking:** Stevens, Haley M. (S001215) [D-MI]

```
          IN RECOGNITION OF THE BAPTISM OF HENRY WINTERHALTER

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. HALEY M. STEVENS

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 19, 2019

  Ms. STEVENS. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize Henry Graham 
Winterhalter and his baptism on Sunday, May 12, 2019.
   I have the honor of being the Godmother to Henry and the lifelong 
friend of his parents Lindsay and Brian Winterhalter. On May 12th we 
gathered at St. Paul's Lutheran Church, a religious institution open to 
all children to witness Henry's baptism. Born on June 28, 2018, Henry 
is now freed from sin and baptized to Christ.
   Without shedding a tear, and symbolizing his gentle heart and 
growing mind, Henry was baptized by Pastor John Spangler.
   As his sponsor, I promised before friends and family to nurture 
Henry Graham in the Christian faith and to serve as a role model 
throughout his life. His life a blessing, we are inspired by all Henry 
will become, a true gift from God.
   Let us all reflect on this as we work to make this country a better 
country for Henry.
   Madam Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in reflecting on 
another milestone in the life of Henry Graham Winterhalter and his 
bright future.

                          ____________________
```
