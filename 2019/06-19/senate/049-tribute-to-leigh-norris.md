# TRIBUTE TO LEIGH NORRIS

**Speaking:** Rubio, Marco (R000595) [R-FL]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO LEIGH NORRIS

● Mr. RUBIO. Mr. President, today I am pleased to honor Leigh 
Norris, the Hamilton County Teacher of the Year from Hamilton County 
Elementary School in Jasper, FL.
  Peggy Sue Hasty, principal at Hamilton County Elementary School, 
described Leigh as a fine example of a dedicated teacher who works hard 
to ensure her students achieve academic success.
  A sixth grade math teacher who has worked in the district for 21 
years, Leigh considers teaching the profession she was born to do. She 
loves to inspire a passion for learning math, and shares the excitement 
with her students when they make gains or solve challenging problems on 
their own.
  I offer my best wishes to Leigh for her work and look forward to 
hearing of her continued success in the coming years.●

                          ____________________
```
