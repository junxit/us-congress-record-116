# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Ruler of history, giver of peace, guide our lawmakers. Remind them 
that no Earthly power can cancel Your promises. May they claim Your 
promise to supply their needs and to infuse them with Your spirit of 
peace. Lord, save them from the temptations of earthly security.
  When their spirits shrivel, overtake our Senators with Your grace, 
mercy, and love. Break through estrangements and shatter the structures 
that lead us away from You.
  We pray in Your strong Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
