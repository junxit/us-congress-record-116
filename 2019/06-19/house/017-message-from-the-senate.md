# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 60

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Lasky, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has agreed to without amendment a joint resolution of 
the House of the following title:

       H.J. Res. 60. Joint Resolution requesting the Secretary of 
     the Interior to authorize unique and one-time arrangements 
     for displays on the National Mall and the Washington Monument 
     during the period beginning on July 16, 2019 and ending on 
     July 20, 2019.
```
