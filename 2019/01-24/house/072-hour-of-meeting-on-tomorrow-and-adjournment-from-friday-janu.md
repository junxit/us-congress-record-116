# HOUR OF MEETING ON TOMORROW, AND ADJOURNMENT FROM FRIDAY, JANUARY 25, 2019, TO MONDAY, JANUARY 28, 2019

**Speaking:** Swalwell, Eric (S001193) [D-CA]

```
 HOUR OF MEETING ON TOMORROW, AND ADJOURNMENT FROM FRIDAY, JANUARY 25, 
                   2019, TO MONDAY, JANUARY 28, 2019

  Mr. SWALWELL of California. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent 
that when the House adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 2 p.m. 
tomorrow, and further, when the House adjourns on that day, it adjourn 
to meet on Monday next, when it shall convene at noon for morning-hour 
debate and 2 p.m. for legislative business.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from California?
  There was no objection.
```
