# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JANUARY 25, 2019

**Speaking:** Rubio, Marco (R000595) [R-FL]

```
                  ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JANUARY 25, 2019

  Mr. RUBIO. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it recess until 12 noon, Friday, 
January 25, 2019; and, further, that following the prayer and pledge, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
