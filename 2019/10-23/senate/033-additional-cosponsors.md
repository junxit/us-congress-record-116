                                 S. 299

(Mrs. Gillibrand) was added as a cosponsor of S. 299, a bill to amend

and diverse aging American population and their families, and for other 
purposes.

(Ms. Ernst) was added as a cosponsor of S. 518, a bill to amend title

certain lymphedema compression treatment items as items of durable

projects to expand the child care workforce and child care facilities
