# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 1579

**Measures:** H.R. 1579

```
           By Mr. PAPPAS:
       H.R. 1579.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Sec. 8, Clause 1, of the United States 
     Constitution states that ``Congress shall have power to lay 
     and collect taxes, duties, imposts and excises, to pay the 
     debts and provide for the common defense and general welfare 
     of the United States.'' [Page H473]
```
