# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal Father, strong to save, empower our lawmakers to serve You 
today, to solve problems, to remove impediments, and to glorify You. 
Give them Your higher wisdom as they seek You, the source of their 
strength.
  Lord, surround them with the shield of Your Divine favor so that no 
weapon formed against them will prosper.
  Almighty God, provide our Senators strength for the adventures of 
these hours, and may Your truth and love fill their hearts and find 
expression in their daily living.
  We pray in Your great Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
