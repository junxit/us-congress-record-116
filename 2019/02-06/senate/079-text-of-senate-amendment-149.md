# Text of Senate Amendment 149

**Speaking:** Braun, Mike (B001310) [R-IN]

```
  SA 149. Mr. BRAUN (for himself and Mr. Toomey) submitted an amendment 
intended to be proposed to amendment SA 111 submitted by Ms. Murkowski 
(for herself and Mr. Manchin) and intended to be proposed to the bill 
S. 47, to provide for the management of the natural resources of the 
United States, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the 
table; as follows:

       On page 695 of the amendment, after line 22, add the 
     following:

     SEC. 90__. REPORT ON MAINTAINING FEDERAL LAND HOLDINGS.

       Not later than 120 days after the date on which the 
     President submits to Congress the budget of the United States 
     for fiscal year 2020, the President shall submit to Congress 
     a report that describes--
       (1) all Federal land holdings; and
       (2) the total cost of maintaining the Federal land holdings 
     described under paragraph (1) for each of fiscal years 2017 
     through 2019, including an accounting of holdings and 
     expenditures by each Federal agency with respect to the land 
     holdings.
                                 ______
```
