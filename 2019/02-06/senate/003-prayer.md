# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, the source from which we come and the goal to which we 
travel, enter into our lives and make us more like You. Strengthen our 
Senators. Equip and empower them with Your heavenly grace, so that they 
may solve the problems of our times with Your wisdom and love. May 
their labors help make America a nation You can trust and bless. Lord, 
grant that the powers of justice, understanding, and cooperative 
endeavors will be used to unify this land we love.
  We pray in Your strong Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
