# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. HEINRICH (for himself, Mrs. Feinstein, Mr. 
             Leahy, Mr. Udall, Mr. Blumenthal, Mr. Carper, Ms. 
             Harris, Mr. Whitehouse, Mr. Murphy, Mrs. Gillibrand, 
             Ms. Warren, Mr. Merkley, Ms. Cortez Masto, Mr. Wyden, 
             Mr. Booker, Ms. Klobuchar, Mr. Cardin, Mr. Reed, Mr.

     information submitted in requests for deferred action under 
     the deferred action for childhood arrivals program, and for 
     other purposes; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
           By Mr. WARNER:
       S. 198. A bill to provide for continuing appropriations in 
     the event of a lapse in appropriations under the normal 
     appropriations process, other than for the legislative branch 
     and the Executive Office of the President; to the Committee 
     on Appropriations.
```
