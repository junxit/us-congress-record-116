**Measures:** H.J.Res. 31, H.Res. 61, H.R. 648

```
         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XIII, reports of committees were delivered to 
the Clerk for printing and reference to the proper calendar, as 
follows:

       Mr. MORELLE: Committee on Rules. H. Res. 61. A resolution 
     providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 648) making 
     appropriations for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2019,

     joint resolution (H.J. Res. 31) making further continuing 
     appropriations for the Department of Homeland Security for 
     fiscal year 2019, and for other purposes; providing for 
     consideration of motions to suspend the rules; and waiving a 
     requirement of clause 6(a) of rule XIII with respect to 
     consideration of certain resolutions reported from the 
     Committee on Rules (Rept. 116-4). Referred to the House 
     Calendar.
```
