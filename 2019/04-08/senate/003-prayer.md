# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal Father, You are the source and strength of our lives. Great 
is Your faithfulness. We thank You today for our lawmakers, who have 
embraced the calling to serve You by serving our Nation. Lord, provide 
them with the wisdom and courage they need for the living of these 
challenging times. Bless them with Divine insights as they grapple with 
complexity that requires difficult choices. May they make it their 
ultimate goal to serve You by striving to do what is best for our 
Nation and world.
  Guide us, Eternal Father, for we are pilgrims in this land.
  We pray in Your merciful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
