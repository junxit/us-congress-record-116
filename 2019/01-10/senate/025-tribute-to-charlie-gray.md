# TRIBUTE TO CHARLIE GRAY

**Speaking:** Rubio, Marco (R000595) [R-FL]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO CHARLIE GRAY

● Mr. RUBIO. Mr. President, today I recognize Charlie Gray, a 
2018 fall intern in my Orlando office, for all of the hard work he has 
done for me, my staff, and the people of the State of Florida.
  Charlie is a student at Rollins College, where he is majoring in 
environmental studies with a minor in political science. He is a 
dedicated and diligent worker who has been devoted to getting the most 
out of his internship experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Charlie 
for all the fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the 
years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
