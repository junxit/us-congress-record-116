# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JUNE 13, 2019

**Speaking:** Lankford, James (L000575) [R-OK]

```
                   ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JUNE 13, 2019

  Mr. LANKFORD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m., 
Thursday, June 13; further, that following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the time for the two 
leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate be 
in a period of morning business under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
