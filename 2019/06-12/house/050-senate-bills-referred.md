# SENATE BILLS REFERRED

**Measures:** S. 395, S. 504

```
                         SENATE BILLS REFERRED

  Bills of the Senate of the following titles were taken from the 
Speaker's table and, under the rule, referred as follows:

       S. 395. An act to require each agency in providing notice 
     of a rule making, to include a link to a 100 word plain 
     language summary of the proposed rule; to the Committee on 
     the Judiciary.
       S. 504. An act to amend title 36, United States Code, to 
     authorize The American Legion to determine the requirements 
     for membership in The American Legion, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on the Judiciary.

                          ____________________
```
