# TARGET PRACTICE AND MARKSMANSHIP TRAINING SUPPORT ACT

**Measures:** S. 94, H.R. 1222

```
         TARGET PRACTICE AND MARKSMANSHIP TRAINING SUPPORT ACT

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. For the information of the Senate, pursuant to 
the order of April 11, 2019, the Senate having received from the House 
H.R. 1222, and its being identical to S. 94, H.R. 1222 is considered 
read the third time and passed, and the motion to reconsider is 
considered made and laid upon the table.
  The passage of S. 94 is thus vitiated, and the bill is indefinitely 
postponed.
  The bill (H.R. 1222) was ordered to a third reading, was read the 
third time, and passed.

                          ____________________
```
