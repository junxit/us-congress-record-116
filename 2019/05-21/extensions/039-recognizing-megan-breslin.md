# RECOGNIZING MEGAN BRESLIN

**Speaking:** Bost, Mike (B001295) [R-IL]

```
                       RECOGNIZING MEGAN BRESLIN

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. MIKE BOST

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, May 21, 2019

  Mr. BOST. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize Megan Breslin in 
honor of her presentation of the Golden Apple Award. Out of 150,000 
high schoolers, this distinction is given to 26 students across the 
state of Illinois who not only exhibit an exceptional work ethic within 
the classroom, but in athletics as well.
  As a senior at Pinckneyville High School, Megan has shown her 
excellence on the basketball court and golf links while maintaining a 
perfect 4.0 Grade Point Average. This balance of academics and 
athletics is a difficult task, and I can't think of a more fitting 
individual to be merited this tremendous award.
  Madam Speaker, please join me in honoring Megan Breslin and commend 
her for her hard work in and out of the classroom.

                          ____________________
```
