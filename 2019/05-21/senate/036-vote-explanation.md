# VOTE EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Baldwin, Tammy (B001230) [D-WI]

```
                            VOTE EXPLANATION

the motion to invoke cloture on Executive Calendar No. 201, Daniel P. 
Collins, of California, to be United States Circuit Judge for the Ninth

confirming this nominee, and I would have voted against cloture if I
```
