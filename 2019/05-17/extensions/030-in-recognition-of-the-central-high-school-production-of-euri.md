# IN RECOGNITION OF THE CENTRAL HIGH SCHOOL PRODUCTION OF EURIPIDES' MEDEA

**Speaking:** Burgess, Michael C. (B001248) [R-TX]

```
  IN RECOGNITION OF THE CENTRAL HIGH SCHOOL PRODUCTION OF EURIPIDES' 
                                 MEDEA

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. MICHAEL C. BURGESS

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 17, 2019

  Mr. BURGESS. Madam Speaker, today I rise to congratulate the cast and 
crew of Central High School's production of Euripides' Medea, which won 
the 2019 Texas UIL Class 6A State One-Act Play Championship.
  This is the first time that Central High School has won the State 
One-Act Play Championship, a remarkable artistic accomplishment, in 
school history. This victory was a team effort, and all members of the 
cast and crew have rightfully earned this honor.
  I also congratulate Ms. Renee Powell for earning a spot on the State 
All-Star Cast and Mr. Jose Gonzalez for earning an Honorable Mention. 
This recognition of their talent and dedication is well-deserved.
  I am glad to join the entire Keller ISD community in celebrating the 
success of these students, and I wish them every continued success in 
their academic and professional endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
