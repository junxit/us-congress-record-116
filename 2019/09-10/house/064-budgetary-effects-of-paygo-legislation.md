# BUDGETARY EFFECTS OF PAYGO LEGISLATION

**Measures:** H.R. 241

```
                 BUDGETARY EFFECTS OF PAYGO LEGISLATION

Yarmuth hereby submits, prior to the vote on passage, for printing in
```
