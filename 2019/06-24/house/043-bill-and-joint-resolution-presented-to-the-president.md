# BILL AND JOINT RESOLUTION PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 60, H.R. 3151

```
          BILL AND JOINT RESOLUTION PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Cheryl L. Johnson, Clerk of the House, reported that on June 24, 
2019, she presented to the President of the United States, for his 
approval, the following bill and joint resolution:

       H.J. Res. 60. Requesting the Secretary of the Interior to 
     authorize unique and one-time arrangements for displays on 
     the National Mall and the Washington Monument during the 
     period beginning on July 16, 2019 and ending on July 20, 
     2019.
       H.R. 3151. To amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to 
     modernize and improve the Internal Revenue Service, and for 
     other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
