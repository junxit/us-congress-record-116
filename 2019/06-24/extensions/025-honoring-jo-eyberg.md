# HONORING JO EYBERG

**Speaking:** Graves, Sam (G000546) [R-MO]

```
                           HONORING JO EYBERG

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. SAM GRAVES

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, June 24, 2019

  Mr. GRAVES of Missouri. Madam Speaker, I proudly pause to recognize 
Jo Eyberg. Jo is a very special woman and leader who is being honored 
with the Lifetime Achievement in the Workplace Award as a part of the 
St. Joseph YWCA's prestigious Women of Excellence.
  When you look at the number of organizations that Jo has volunteered, 
chaired or donated to, it's far easier to name the organizations she 
hasn't worked with in St. Joseph. It's humbling when you consider the 
multitude of lives she has touched over years and years of dedicated 
volunteerism. To say that Jo is a sterling example of unwavering 
service to her community and an example of what we should all hope to 
be is a great understatement.
  Madam Speaker, I proudly ask you to join me in recognizing Jo Eyberg 
for her lifetime of accomplishments serving St. Joseph, the state of 
Missouri and the Sixth Congressional District. I am honored to 
represent her in the United States Congress.

                          ____________________
```
