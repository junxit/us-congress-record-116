# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JULY 10, 2019

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JULY 10, 2019

  Mr. THUNE. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m., 
Wednesday, July 10; further, that following the prayer and pledge, the 
morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved 
to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, morning business be closed, and the Senate proceed to 
executive session and resume consideration of the Wetherell nomination.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
