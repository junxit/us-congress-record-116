# MICHAEL WYNN RECEIVES THE COLLIER COUNTY CITIZEN OF THE YEAR FOR 2019

**Speaking:** Rooney, Francis (R000607) [R-FL]

```
 MICHAEL WYNN RECEIVES THE COLLIER COUNTY CITIZEN OF THE YEAR FOR 2019

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. FRANCIS ROONEY

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 9, 2019

  Mr. ROONEY of Florida. Madam Speaker, I rise to congratulate Michael 
Wynn who was recently voted the Collier County Citizen of the Year for 
2019.
  Being chairman and president of Sunshine Ace Hardware in Naples, Mr. 
Wynn provides leadership to stores delivering friendly and helpful 
service with ``Big City'' quality in stores in Estero, Bonita Springs, 
Golden Gate, Marco Island, Naples, Port Charlotte, Largo and Port 
Charlotte. Since 1992, Mr. Wynn has held various positions increasing 
in responsibility with Sunshine Ace Hardware.
  Michael is especially grateful for the opportunity to provide several 
basic hardware lines, such as tools, builders' hardware, paint and 
glass, housewares and household appliances, outdoor living and fishing 
products to the citizens of Florida. His good-natured service to the 
community has led to Michael being voted as the Collier County Citizen 
of the Year for 2019.
  I am grateful for Michael and his commitment to his community. I look 
forward to seeing what the future holds from the positive effects of 
Mr. Wynn and Sunshine Ace Hardware.

                          ____________________
```
