# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2500, NATIONAL DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 2020, AND PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF MOTIONS TO SUSPEND THE RULES

**Measures:** H.Res. 476, H.R. 2500

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2500, NATIONAL 
   DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 2020, AND PROVIDING FOR 
             CONSIDERATION OF MOTIONS TO SUSPEND THE RULES

  Mr. McGOVERN, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 116-143) on the resolution (H. Res. 476) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 2500) to authorize appropriations 
for fiscal year 2020 for military activities of the Department of 
Defense and for military construction, to prescribe military personnel 
strengths for such fiscal year, and for other purposes, and providing 
for consideration of motions to suspend the rules, which was referred 
to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
