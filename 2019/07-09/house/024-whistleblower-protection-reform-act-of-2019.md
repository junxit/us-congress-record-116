  Messrs. MAST, ABRAHAM, DUNCAN, RICE of South Carolina, and KEVIN HERN

  The title of the bill was amended so as to read: ``A bill to amend 
the Securities and Exchange Act of 1934 to amend the definition of 
whistleblower, to extend the anti-retaliation protections provided to 
whistleblowers, and for other purposes.''.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
