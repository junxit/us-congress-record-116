# TAX TREATIES AND PROTOCOLS

**Speaking:** Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA]

```
                       TAX TREATIES AND PROTOCOLS

  Mr. GRASSLEY. Madam President, later today, the Senate Foreign 
Relations Committee is scheduled to consider four protocols to the 
United States' tax treaties with Spain, Switzerland, Japan, and 
Luxembourg. I support swift action on these protocols both in committee 
and in the Senate, and I urge my colleagues to vote yes on them.
  I encourage the committee to also take up the new tax treaties with 
Chile, Hungary, and Poland as soon as possible. These new treaties will 
provide important benefits to U.S. taxpayers and the U.S. Government.
  After years of discussion and debate, the time has come to move 
forward on all of these bilateral agreements.
  I yield the floor.

                          ____________________
```
