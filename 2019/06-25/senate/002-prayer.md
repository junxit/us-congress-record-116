# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Gracious God, You have given us eyes to see, ears to hear, and minds 
to understand. Reveal Yourself to our lawmakers so that what they see, 
hear, and think will glorify You. Today, may they desire and do that 
which is most acceptable to You. Lord, use them so that Your will may 
be done in our Nation and world as they trust the unfolding of Your 
powerful providence. As they wait for You, O God, renew their strength, 
enabling them to mount up with wings as eagles, running without 
weariness and walking without fainting.
  We pray in Your merciful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
