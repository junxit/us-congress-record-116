# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 2954

**Measures:** H.R. 2954

```
           By Mr. PALAZZO:
       H.R. 2954.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8 of the Constitution provides Congress 
     authority to raise and support Armies and to provide and 
     maintain a Navy.
```
