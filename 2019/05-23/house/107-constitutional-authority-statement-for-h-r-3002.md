# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 3002

**Measures:** H.R. 3002

```
           By Mr. NEAL:
       H.R. 3002.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority on which this bill rests is in 
     the power of Congress to regulate commerce as enumerated in 
     Article I, Section 8.
```
