# ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

**Measures:** H.R. 1569, H.R. 2196

```
                         ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

  Cheryl L. Johnson, Clerk of the House, reported and found truly 
enrolled bills of the House of the following titles, which were 
thereupon signed by the Speaker on Friday, July 26, 2019:

       H.R. 1569. An act to amend title 28, United States Code, to 
     add Flagstaff and Yuma to the list of locations in which 
     court shall be held in the judicial district for the State of 
     Arizona.
       H.R. 2196. An act to amend title 38, United States Code, to 
     reduce the credit hour requirement for the Edith Nourse 
     Rogers STEM Scholarship program of the Department of Veterans 
     Affairs.

                          ____________________
```
