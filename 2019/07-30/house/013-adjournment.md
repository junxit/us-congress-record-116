# ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** H.Res. 509

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to section 5(b) of House Resolution 
509, consistent with the fourth clause in section 5 of Article I of the 
Constitution, and notwithstanding section 132 of the Legislative 
Reorganization Act of 1946, the House stands adjourned until 11 a.m. on 
Friday, August 2, 2019.
  Thereupon (at 11 o'clock and 34 minutes a.m.), consistent with the 
fourth clause in section 5 of Article I of the Constitution, and 
notwithstanding section 132 of the Legislative Reorganization Act of 
1946, under its previous order, the House adjourned until Friday, 
August 2, 2019, at 11 a.m.
```
