# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Monsignor Stephen J. Rossetti, Catholic University of America, 
Washington, D.C., offered the following prayer:
  Good and gracious God, you are light, and wherever You shine, You 
cast out the darkness. We have become so very aware of the darkness 
which invades this world and causes distrust and discord.
  Come now into this place and into our hearts, dispel the darkness, 
and bring on Your kingdom of light. Let peace reign in this place today 
and always.
  We make this prayer in the power of Your spirit and Your holy name.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
