# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** S. 6

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       125. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the General 
     Assembly of the State of Missouri, relative to Senate 
     Concurrent Resolution No. 6, calling upon the People's 
     Republic of China to immediately end the practice of organ 
     harvesting from all prisoners and prisoners of conscience, 
     and explicitly from Falun Gong prisoners of conscience and 
     members of other religious and ethnic minority groups; which 
     was referred to the Committee on Foreign Affairs.
```
