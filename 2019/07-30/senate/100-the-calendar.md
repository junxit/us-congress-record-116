# THE CALENDAR

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                              THE CALENDAR

  Mr. McCONNELL. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Senate proceed to the immediate consideration of the following Calendar 
bills, en bloc: Calendar Nos. 85 through 89, 121 through 124, and 
Calendar No. 162.
  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the bills, 
en bloc.
  Mr. McCONNELL. I ask unanimous consent that the bills be considered 
read a third time and passed, and the motions to reconsider be 
considered made and laid upon the table, all en bloc.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
