# HENDERSON VETERANS MEMORIAL POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 1198

```
            HENDERSON VETERANS MEMORIAL POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 1198) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 404 South Boulder Highway in Henderson, 
Nevada, as the ``Henderson Veterans Memorial Post Office Building'',
```
