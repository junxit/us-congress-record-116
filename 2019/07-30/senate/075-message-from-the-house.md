# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** S. 2249

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE


                          enrolled bill signed

  At 3:44 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks announced that the Speaker pro 
tempore (Mr. Raskin) has signed the following enrolled bill:

       S. 2249. An act to allow the Deputy Administrator of the 
     Federal Aviation Administration on the date of enactment of 
     this Act to continue to serve as such Deputy Administrator.

  The enrolled bill was subsequently signed by the President pro 
tempore (Mr. Grassley).

                          ____________________
```
