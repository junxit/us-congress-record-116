# IN RECOGNITION OF AUBURN UNIVERSITY BASS FISHING CLUB WINNING NATIONAL CHAMPIONSHIP

**Speaking:** Rogers, Mike D. (R000575) [R-AL]

```
IN RECOGNITION OF AUBURN UNIVERSITY BASS FISHING CLUB WINNING NATIONAL 
                              CHAMPIONSHIP

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE ROGERS

                               of alabama

                    in the house of representatives

                      Tuesday, September 17, 2019

  Mr. ROGERS of Alabama. Madam Speaker, I ask for the House's attention 
to recognize the Auburn University Bass Fishing Club for winning the 
first ever Major League Fishing College Redcrest National Championship.
  The championship took place in August in LaCrosse, Wisconsin on the 
Mississippi River.
  The Auburn University Bass Fishing Club was founded in 2007. This 
season, the club will compete in up to 20 events.
  Club members include Logan Parks, Lucas Lindsay, Will Phillips, Chase 
Mundhenke, Patch Pelt and Anthony Vinston.
  Madam Speaker, please join me in recognizing the Auburn University 
Bass Fishing Club.

                          ____________________
```
