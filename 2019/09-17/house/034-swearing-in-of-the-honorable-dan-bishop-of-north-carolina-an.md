# SWEARING IN OF THE HONORABLE DAN BISHOP, OF NORTH CAROLINA, AND THE HONORABLE GREG MURPHY, OF NORTH CAROLINA, AS MEMBERS OF THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

**Speaking:** Price, David E. (P000523) [D-NC]

```
  SWEARING IN OF THE HONORABLE DAN BISHOP, OF NORTH CAROLINA, AND THE 
 HONORABLE GREG MURPHY, OF NORTH CAROLINA, AS MEMBERS OF THE HOUSE OF 
                            REPRESENTATIVES

  Mr. PRICE of North Carolina. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent 
that the gentlemen from North Carolina, the Honorable Dan Bishop and 
the Honorable Greg Murphy, be permitted to take the oath of office 
today.
  Their certificates of election have not arrived, but there is no 
contest and no question has been raised with regard to their elections.
  The SPEAKER. Is there objection to the request of the gentleman from 
North Carolina?
  There was no objection.
```
