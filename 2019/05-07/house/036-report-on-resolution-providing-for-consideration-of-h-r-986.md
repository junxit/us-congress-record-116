and Affordable Care Act shall have no force or effect, and providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 2157) making supplemental 
appropriations for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2019, and for 
other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to 
be printed.
