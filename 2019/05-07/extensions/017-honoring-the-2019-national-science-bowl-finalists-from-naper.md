# HONORING THE 2019 NATIONAL SCIENCE BOWL FINALISTS FROM NAPERVILLE NORTH HIGH SCHOOL

**Speaking:** Foster, Bill (F000454) [D-IL]

```
HONORING THE 2019 NATIONAL SCIENCE BOWL FINALISTS FROM NAPERVILLE NORTH 
                              HIGH SCHOOL

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. BILL FOSTER

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                          Tuesday, May 7, 2019

  Mr. FOSTER. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize the National 
Science Bowl team of Naperville North High School and to commend them 
on reaching the national finals of the high school academic 
competition.
  Since 1991, the Office of Science's National Science Bowl has hosted 
some of the nation's

[[Page E549]]

most intelligent students for one of the largest and most prestigious 
academic competitions. Every year, teams from 64 schools around the 
country compete to demonstrate their understanding of the great issues 
in mathematics and science.
  I want to congratulate the Naperville North High School team on 
reaching the national finals and on placing first in SLAC Division Team 
Challenge. The talent and knowledge present in these students are truly 
impressive, and as Congress' resident PhD-trained physicist, I wish 
them the best of luck in their future endeavors.

                          ____________________
```
