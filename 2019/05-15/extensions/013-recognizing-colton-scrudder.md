# RECOGNIZING COLTON SCRUDDER

**Speaking:** Steil, Bryan (S001213) [R-WI]

```
                      RECOGNIZING COLTON SCRUDDER

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. BRYAN STEIL

                              of wisconsin

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, May 15, 2019

  Mr. STEIL. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize one of our 
hardworking D.C. office interns, Colton Scrudder. Colton is a native of 
Coppell, Texas and studies finance at Georgetown University. He has 
been with our office since January. Colton was a great help getting our 
office up and running.
   From drafting letters and memos, to giving Capitol tours, Colton was 
always eager to assist. Colton is an intelligent, diligent young man 
who aspires to continue serving his community.
   We'll miss having Colton in the office and wish him all the best in 
the future. On behalf of Wisconsin's First Congressional District, we 
thank Colton for his help.

                          ____________________
```
