# RECOGNIZING KYLER REESE

**Speaking:** Steil, Bryan (S001213) [R-WI]

```
                        RECOGNIZING KYLER REESE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. BRYAN STEIL

                              of wisconsin

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, May 15, 2019

  Mr. STEIL. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize one of our 
hardworking D.C. office interns, Kyler Reese. Kyler was born and raised 
in Virginia and is working towards a concentration in Sports Management 
from George Mason University.
   Our office was fortunate to have Kyler as part of the Gregg and 
Livingston Harper Congressional Internship Program for Individuals with 
Intellectual Disabilities. Kyler lit up the office with his smile and 
energy, and enjoyed learning from staff and helping with our mail 
program.
   We'll miss having Kyler in the office and wish him all the best in 
the future. On behalf of Wisconsin's First Congressional District, we 
thank Kyler for his help.

                          ____________________
```
