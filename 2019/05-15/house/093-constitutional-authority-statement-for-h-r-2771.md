# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 2771

**Measures:** H.R. 2771

```
           By Mrs. RODGERS of Washington:
       H.R. 2771.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Consistent with the understanding and interpretation of the 
     Commerce Clause, Congress has authority to enact this 
     legislation in accordance with Clause 3 of Section 8, Article 
     1 of the U.S. Constitution.
```
