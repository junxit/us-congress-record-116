# REPORT ON H.R. 2745, MILITARY CONSTRUCTION AND VETERANS AFFAIRS AND RELATED AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS BILL, 2020

**Measures:** H.R. 2745

```
  REPORT ON H.R. 2745, MILITARY CONSTRUCTION AND VETERANS AFFAIRS AND 
               RELATED AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS BILL, 2020

  Ms. WASSERMAN SCHULTZ, from the Committee on Appropriations, 
submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 116-63) on the bill (H.R. 
2745) making appropriations for military construction, the Department 
of Veterans Affairs, and related agencies for the fiscal year ending 
September 30, 2020, and for other purposes, which was referred to the 
Union Calendar and ordered to be printed.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 1, rule XXI, all points 
of order are reserved on the bill.
```
