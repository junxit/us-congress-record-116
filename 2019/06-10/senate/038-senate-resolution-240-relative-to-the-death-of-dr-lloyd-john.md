# SENATE RESOLUTION 240--RELATIVE TO THE DEATH OF DR. LLOYD JOHN OGILVIE, FORMER CHAPLAIN OF THE UNITED STATES SENATE

**Measures:** S.Res. 240

```
                         SUBMITTED RESOLUTIONS

                                 ______
                                 

SENATE RESOLUTION 240--RELATIVE TO THE DEATH OF DR. LLOYD JOHN OGILVIE, 
              FORMER CHAPLAIN OF THE UNITED STATES SENATE

  Mr. McCONNELL submitted the following resolution; which was 
considered and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 240

       Whereas Dr. Lloyd John Ogilvie, a native of Kenosha, 
     Wisconsin, earned degrees from Lake Forest College and 
     Garrett Theological Seminary and pursued postgraduate studies 
     at New College of the University of Edinburgh in Scotland;
       Whereas Dr. Lloyd John Ogilvie served as a Presbyterian 
     minister throughout his life in Illinois, Pennsylvania, and 
     California;
       Whereas Dr. Lloyd John Ogilvie authored many books and 
     hosted nationally syndicated radio and television ministry;
       Whereas Dr. Lloyd John Ogilvie became the 61st Senate 
     Chaplain on March 11, 1995, and faithfully served the Senate 
     for eight years as Senate Chaplain: Now, therefore, be it
       Resolved, That the Senate has heard with profound sorrow 
     and deep regret the announcement of the death of Dr. Lloyd 
     John Ogilvie, former Chaplain of the Senate.
       Resolved, That the Secretary of the Senate communicate 
     these resolutions to the House of Representatives and 
     transmit an enrolled copy thereof to the family of the 
     deceased.
       Resolved, That when the Senate adjourns today, it stand 
     adjourned as a further mark of respect to the memory of Dr. 
     Lloyd John Ogilvie.

                          ____________________
```
