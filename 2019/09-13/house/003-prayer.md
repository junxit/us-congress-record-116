# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Monsignor Stephen J. Rossetti, Catholic University of America, 
Washington, D.C., offered the following prayer:
  Good and gracious God, You have entered our world and defeated the 
powers of evil. Come now to this place, continue Your saving work, and 
cast out any remnants of discord and darkness. May this be a holy 
place, and may Your holy angels protect each and every person who 
enters. We make this prayer in the power of Your spirit and in Your 
holy name.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
