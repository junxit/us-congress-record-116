# EXPRESSING SUPPORT FOR THE DESIGNATION OF MARCH 21, 2019, AS "NATIONAL ROSIE THE RIVETER DAY"

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** S.Res. 114

```
EXPRESSING SUPPORT FOR THE DESIGNATION OF MARCH 21, 2019, AS ``NATIONAL 
                        ROSIE THE RIVETER DAY''

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the immediate consideration of S. Res. 114, submitted 
earlier today.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the resolution by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A resolution (S. Res. 114) expressing support for the 
     designation of March 21, 2019, as ``National Rosie the 
     Riveter Day.''

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
resolution.
  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I further ask unanimous consent that 
the resolution be agreed to, the preamble be agreed to, and the motions 
to reconsider be considered made and laid upon the table with no 
intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The resolution (S. Res. 114) was agreed to.
  The preamble was agreed to.
  (The resolution, with its preamble, is printed in today's Record 
under ``Submitted Resolutions.'')

                          ____________________
```
