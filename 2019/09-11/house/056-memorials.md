# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** H.Res. 247, S. 596

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       131. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the Senate of the 
     State of Mississippi, relative to Senate Concurrent 
     Resolution No. 596, requesting the Congress of the United 
     States call a convention of the states to propose amendments 
     to the Constitution of the United States; to the Committee on 
     the Judiciary.
       132. Also, a memorial of the General Assembly of the 
     Commonwealth of Pennsylvania, relative to House Resolution 
     No. 247, memorializing the Congress of the United States to 
     facilitate and ensure implementation of the VA Maintaining 
     Internal Systems and Strengthening Integrated Outside 
     Networks Act of 2018 by the United States Department of 
     Veterans Affairs; to the Committee on Veterans' Affairs.

                          ____________________
```
