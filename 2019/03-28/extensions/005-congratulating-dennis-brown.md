# CONGRATULATING DENNIS BROWN

**Speaking:** Rutherford, John H. (R000609) [R-FL]

```
                      CONGRATULATING DENNIS BROWN

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JOHN H. RUTHERFORD

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, March 28, 2019

  Mr. RUTHERFORD. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
congratulate Dennis Brown on 50 years of employment at the Anheuser-
Busch Jacksonville Brewery.
  Mr. Brown began at the brewery in April of 1969, working onsite even 
before it packaged its first barrel of beer.
  His attention to detail, unmatched work ethic, and commitment to 
quality has allowed him to excel as a leader in the brewery.
  I have visited the Jacksonville brewery and can personally attest to 
its productivity and efficiency.
  There is great honor in hard work at a job worth doing, and the 
admirable record of Mr. Brown's longevity speaks to his successful 
career.
  I thank Mr. Brown for his part in making Budweiser the King of Beers 
and wish him the best in all future endeavors.
  Cheers.

                          ____________________
```
