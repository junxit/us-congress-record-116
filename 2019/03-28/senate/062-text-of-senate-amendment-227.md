# Text of Senate Amendment 227

**Speaking:** Cotton, Tom (C001095) [R-AR]

```
  SA 227. Mr. COTTON submitted an amendment intended to be proposed to 
amendment SA 201 proposed by Mr. Shelby to the bill H.R. 268, making 
supplemental appropriations for the fiscal year ending September 30, 
2019, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as 
follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. __. DECLARATION OF EMERGENCY.

       (a) Declaration.--The Trafalgar Road Fire in Bella Vista, 
     Arkansas shall be deemed to be an emergency under section 
     title V of the Robert T. Stafford Disaster Relief and 
     Emergency Assistance Act (42 U.S.C. 5191 et seq.).
       (b) Effective Date.--Subsection (a) shall take effect as if 
     enacted on August 1, 2018.
                                 ______
```
