# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  King of our lives, we acknowledge Your sovereignty, believing because 
of You we live and move and have our being.
  Show our lawmakers how to use this day's fleeting minutes to 
accomplish Your purposes on Earth. Make our Senators Your instruments 
of deliverance, causing justice to roll down like waters and 
righteousness like a mighty stream. Sanctify their thoughts, words, and 
deeds throughout this day and all of their tomorrows. Lord, provide 
them with goodness, grace, and wisdom for the living of these days.
  We pray in Your Holy Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
