# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 297

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** H.R. 297

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 297

  Mr. McCONNELL. Madam President, I understand there is a bill at the 
desk due for a second reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the title of the bill 
for the second time.
  The senior assistant bill clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 297) to extend the Federal recognition to the 
     Little Shell Tribe of Chippewa Indians of Montana, and for 
     other purposes.

  Mr. McCONNELL. In order to place the bill on the calendar under the 
provisions of rule XIV, I object to further proceedings.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection having been heard, the bill will be 
placed on the calendar.

                          ____________________
```
