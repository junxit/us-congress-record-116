# Text of Senate Amendment 220

**Speaking:** Rubio, Marco (R000595) [R-FL]

```
  SA 220. Mr. RUBIO (for himself and Mr. Perdue) submitted an amendment 
intended to be proposed to amendment SA 201 proposed by Mr. Shelby to 
the bill H.R. 268, making supplemental appropriations for the fiscal 
year ending September 30, 2019, and for other purposes; which was 
ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       On page 3, line 20, strike ``occurring in calendar years 
     2018 and 2019'' and insert ``occurring during the period 
     beginning on January 1, 2018, and ending on the date of 
     enactment of this Act''.
                                 ______
```
