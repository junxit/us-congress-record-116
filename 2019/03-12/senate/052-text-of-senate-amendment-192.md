# Text of Senate Amendment 192

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
  SA 192. Mr. McCONNELL (for Mr. Coons) proposed an amendment to the 
resolution S. Res. 91, designating March 3, 2019, as ``World Wildlife 
Day''; as follows:

       In the 25th whereas clause of the preamble, in paragraph 
     (3), strike ``poses'' and insert ``could potentially pose''.
       In the 27th whereas clause of the preamble, strike 
     ``approximately 100,000,000 sharks are killed annually'' and 
     insert ``millions of sharks are killed every year in illegal, 
     unreported, and unregulated fisheries''.

                          ____________________
```
