# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Mighty God, hear our prayer. Because of Your mercies, give us the 
wisdom to strive to please You.
  Continue to effectually work through our lawmakers to accomplish Your 
purposes. Lord, strengthen them to catapult the hurdles that threaten 
true freedom.
  Grant that in all our perplexities, we will seek Your truth. Lord, 
may we hear the thunder of Your justice mingled with the showers of 
Your grace. Preserve our lawmakers even during turbulent seasons.
  We pray in Your Holy Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
