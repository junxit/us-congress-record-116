# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 583

**Measures:** H.R. 583

```
            By Mr. TONKO:
        H.R. 583.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:

                     Article I, Section 8, Clause I

       Provides Congress with the power to ``lay and collect 
     Taxes, Duties, Imposts and Excises'' in order to ``provide 
     for the . . . general Welfare of the United States.''
```
