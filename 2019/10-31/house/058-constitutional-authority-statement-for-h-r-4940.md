# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4940

**Measures:** H.R. 4940

```
            By Mr. FLORES:
        H.R. 4940.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
        Article I, Section 8, Clause 5 of the Constitution of the 
     United States:
        The Congress shall have Power to coin Money, regulate the 
     Value thereof, and of foreign Coin, and fix the Standard of 
     Weights and Measures;
```
