# PERMISSION TO INCLUDE AMENDMENT TEXT IMMEDIATELY PRIOR TO VOTE ON PREVIOUS QUESTION ON H. RES. 660

**Speaking:** Scanlon, Mary Gay (S001205) [D-PA]

**Measures:** H.Res. 660

```
   PERMISSION TO INCLUDE AMENDMENT TEXT IMMEDIATELY PRIOR TO VOTE ON 
                    PREVIOUS QUESTION ON H. RES. 660

  Ms. SCANLON. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that my colleague 
from the Rules Committee, Ranking Member Cole from Oklahoma, be 
permitted to insert the text of the amendment he would have offered had 
the House rejected the previous question on H. Res. 660, along with 
extraneous material, into the Record immediately prior to the vote on 
ordering the previous question on H. Res. 660.

the request of the gentlewoman from Pennsylvania?
  There was no objection.
```
