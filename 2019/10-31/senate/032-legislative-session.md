# LEGISLATIVE SESSION

**Speaking:** Cotton, Tom (C001095) [R-AR], Harris, Kamala D. (H001075) [D-CA]

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 52

```
                          LEGISLATIVE SESSION

                                 ______
                                 

                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. COTTON. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to legislative session and be in a period of morning business, 
with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  (At the request of Mr. Schumer, the following statement was ordered 
to be printed in the Record.)
● Ms. HARRIS. Mr. President, on October 30, 2019, I was not 
present for the vote on S.J. Res. 52, providing for congressional 
disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule 
submitted by the Secretary of the Treasury and the Secretary of Health 
and Human Services relating to ``State Relief and Empowerment 
Waivers.'' If I were present, I would have voted yes.●
```
