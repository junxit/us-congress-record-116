                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to executive session for consideration of Calendar Nos. 468 
through 477 and all nominations on the Secretary's desk in the Air 
Force, Army, Marine Corps, and Navy; that the nominations be confirmed; 
that the motions to reconsider be considered made and laid upon the 
table with no intervening action or debate; that no further motions be 
in order; that any statements related to the nominations be printed in 
the Record; that the President be immediately notified of the Senate's 
action, and the Senate then resume legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
