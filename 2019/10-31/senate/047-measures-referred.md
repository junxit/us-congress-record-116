# MEASURES REFERRED

**Measures:** H.R. 823, H.R. 1373, H.R. 2181

```
                           MEASURES REFERRED

  The following bills were read the first and the second times by 
unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 823. An act to provide for the designation of certain 
     wilderness areas, recreation management areas, and 
     conservation areas in the State of Colorado, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on Energy and Natural Resources.
       H.R. 1373. An act to protect, for current and future 
     generations, the watershed, ecosystem, and cultural heritage 
     of the Grand Canyon region in the State of Arizona, and for 
     other purposes; to the Committee on Energy and Natural 
     Resources.
       H.R. 2181. An act to provide for the withdrawal and 
     protection of certain Federal land in the State of New 
     Mexico; to the Committee on Energy and Natural Resources.

                          ____________________
```
