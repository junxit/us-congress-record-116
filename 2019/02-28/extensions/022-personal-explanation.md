**Measures:** H.R. 1112

```

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, February 28, 2019

the Van Drew Cunningham Amendment, NO on the Motion to Recommit H.R.
```
