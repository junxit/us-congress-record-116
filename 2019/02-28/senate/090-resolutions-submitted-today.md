# RESOLUTIONS SUBMITTED TODAY

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

**Measures:** S.Res. 86, S.Res. 87, S.Res. 88, S.Res. 89, S.Res. 90

```
                      RESOLUTIONS SUBMITTED TODAY

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
now proceed to the en bloc consideration of the following Senate 
resolutions which were submitted earlier today: S. Res. 86, S. Res. 87, 
S. Res. 88, S. Res. 89, and S. Res. 90.
  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
resolutions en bloc.
  Mr. McCONNELL. I ask unanimous consent that the resolutions be agreed 
to, the preambles, where applicable, be agreed to, and that the motions 
to reconsider be considered made and laid upon the table, all en bloc.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The resolutions (S. Res. 86 and S. Res. 87) were agreed to.
  (The resolutions are printed in today's Record under ``Submitted 
Resolutions.'')
  The resolutions (S. Res. 88, S. Res. 89, and S. Res. 90) were agreed 
to.
  The preambles were agreed to.
  (The resolutions, with their preambles, are printed in today's Record 
under ``Submitted Resolutions.'')

                          ____________________
```
