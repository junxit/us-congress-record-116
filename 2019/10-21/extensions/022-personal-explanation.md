                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                        HON. A. DONALD McEACHIN

                              of virginia

  Mr. McEACHIN. Madam Speaker, I was unavoidably detained on September 
26, 2019 during roll call no. 549, On Ordering the Previous Question,

national emergency declared by the President on February 15, 2019. Had

detained during roll call no. 550, On Agreeing to the Resolution, H. 
Res. 591, Providing for consideration of S.J. Res. 54, relating to a 
national emergency declared by the President on February 15, 2019. Had 
I been present, I would have voted ``yea.'' I was also unavoidably 
detained during roll call no. 551, On Motion to Recommit with 
Instructions, H.R. 3525, U.S. Border Patrol Medical Screening Standards 
Act. Had I been present, I would have voted ``nay.'' I was also 
unavoidably detained during roll call no. 552, On Passage, H.R. 3525, 
U.S. Border Patrol Medical Screening Standards Act. Had I been present, 
I would have voted ``yea.''
