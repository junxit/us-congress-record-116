# HONORING GLENN GILLESPIE FOR HIS 20 YEARS OF SERVICE ORGANIZING THE CLINTON COUNTY FAIR ON THE OCCASION OF HIS RETIREMENT

**Speaking:** Stefanik, Elise M. (S001196) [R-NY]

```
  HONORING GLENN GILLESPIE FOR HIS 20 YEARS OF SERVICE ORGANIZING THE 
         CLINTON COUNTY FAIR ON THE OCCASION OF HIS RETIREMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ELISE M. STEFANIK

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, July 19, 2019

  Ms. STEFANIK. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor Glenn Gillespie 
for his 20 years of service organizing the Clinton County Fair. After 
20 years and on the 71st anniversary, this year marks the last time 
Glenn will be heading the event after recently announcing his 
retirement.
  The Clinton County Fair has been a mainstay of the community since 
1948 with generations of North Country residents attending the fair 
each year. Whether it be the rides, the music, or the animals, the fair 
has something for everybody and has been a celebration that has bonded 
our communities for generations.
  Thanks to Glenn's vision and passion, the fair has grown in 
attendance every year and expanded the list of attractions. During his 
tenure, Glenn introduced the Plattsburgh Bluegrass Festival to the 
fairgrounds and brought in booster clubs from local schools. Glenn's 
accomplishments extend statewide having served as a representative to 
the state convention of fairs during his tenure and serving as-
president of all state fairs in 2008. In 2016, Glenn's contributions 
were recognized when the New York State Agricultural Fair Association 
named a stage on the fairground in his honor.
  Glenn Gillespie has left a permanent mark on the Clinton County Fair 
and the North Country at large. His accomplishments have strengthened 
our community and brought joy to countless North Country residents. On 
behalf of New York's 21st Congressional District, I want to thank Glenn 
and wish him and his family the best as they move into this new chapter 
in their lives.

                          ____________________
```
