**Measures:** H.J.Res. 37

```
            By Mr. KHANNA:
        H.J. Res. 37.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 11 of the U.S. Constitution 
     grants Congress the power to declare war.
```
