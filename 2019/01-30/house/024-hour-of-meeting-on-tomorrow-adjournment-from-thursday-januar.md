# HOUR OF MEETING ON TOMORROW; ADJOURNMENT FROM THURSDAY, JANUARY 31, 2019, TO MONDAY, FEBRUARY 4, 2019; HOUR OF MEETING ON TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 5, 2019

**Speaking:** Hoyer, Steny H. (H000874) [D-MD]

```
  HOUR OF MEETING ON TOMORROW; ADJOURNMENT FROM THURSDAY, JANUARY 31, 
2019, TO MONDAY, FEBRUARY 4, 2019; HOUR OF MEETING ON TUESDAY, FEBRUARY

and 2 p.m. for legislative business.

gentleman from Maryland?
```
