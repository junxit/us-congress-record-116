# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Patrick J. Conroy, offered the following 
prayer:
  Loving God, we give You thanks for giving us another day.
  Bless the Members of this assembly as they consider all the options 
and compromises they might face in the weeks to come.
  May the desire to act speedily to implement promises made while 
campaigning not prevent the careful consideration of all possible 
outcomes in the governing process. Send Your Spirit of wisdom and 
discernment upon them in their work.
  May Your blessing, O God, be with all our leaders this day and every 
day to come, and may all we do be done for Your greater honor and 
glory.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
