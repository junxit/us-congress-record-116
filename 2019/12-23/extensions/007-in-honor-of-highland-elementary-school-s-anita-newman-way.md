# IN HONOR OF HIGHLAND ELEMENTARY SCHOOL'S ``ANITA NEWMAN WAY''

**Speaking:** Guthrie, Brett (G000558) [R-KY]

```
     IN HONOR OF HIGHLAND ELEMENTARY SCHOOL'S ``ANITA NEWMAN WAY''

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. BRETT GUTHRIE

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, December 23, 2019

  Mr. GUTHRIE. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize former Highland 
Elementary School principal Anita Newman. Principal Newman served as an 
educator in the Daviess County Public Schools district for 36 years 
before she retired in 2012. She began her career as a teacher at 
Masonville Elementary School and joined Highland Elementary School as a 
teacher in 1978. After a stint as principal of Utica Elementary School, 
she returned to Highland Elementary School as principal in 1989. 
Principal Newman is a distinguished graduate of both Kentucky Wesley 
College and Western Kentucky University. I know that the new ``Anita 
Newman Way'' at Highland Elementary School, dedicated this month, will 
be a reminder of her lasting legacy on the hearts and minds of young 
Daviess County students. I thank Principal Newman for her service.

                          ____________________
```
