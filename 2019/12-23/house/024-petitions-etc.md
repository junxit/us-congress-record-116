# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       73. The SPEAKER presented a petition of Mr. Gregory D. 
     Watson, a citizen of Austin, TX, relative to requesting that 
     Congress enact legislation which would require that persons 
     seeing to immigrate to the United States -- including those 
     seeking asylum -- be proficient in the spoken and written use 
     of the English language; which was referred to the Committee 
     on the Judiciary.
```
