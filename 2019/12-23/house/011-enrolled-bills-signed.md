# ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

**Measures:** H.R. 777, H.R. 1158, H.R. 1865, H.R. 3196

```
                         ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

  Cheryl L. Johnson, Clerk of the House, reported and found truly 
enrolled bills of the House of the following titles, which were 
thereupon signed by the Speaker:

       H.R. 777. An act to reauthorize programs authorized under 
     the Debbie Smith Act of 2004.
       H.R. 1158. An act making consolidated appropriations for 
     the fiscal year ending September 30, 2020, and for other 
     purposes.
       H.R. 1865. An act making further consolidated 
     appropriations for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2020, 
     and for other purposes.
       H.R. 3196. An act to designate the Large Synoptic Survey 
     Telescope as the ``Vera C. Rubin Observatory''.

                          ____________________
```
