# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal King, unto whom all hearts are open, come to us in the purity 
of Your presence, and make us what we ought to be.
  Guide our lawmakers. Show them what needs to be changed, and give 
them the courage and wisdom to make the appropriate adjustments. Lord, 
in all their labors, help them to yield themselves to Your will so that 
this legislative branch may fulfill Your purposes for humanity. Quicken 
their thinking and reinforce their judgment until their decisions bring 
glory to Your Name. May Your grace be sufficient for all their needs.
  We pray in Your wonderful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
