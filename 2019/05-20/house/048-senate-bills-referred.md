# SENATE BILLS REFERRED

**Measures:** S. 744, S. 820, S. 998

```
                         SENATE BILLS REFERRED

  Bills of the Senate of the following titles were taken from the 
Speaker's table and, under the rule, referred as follows:

       S. 744. An act to amend section 175b of title 18, United 
     States Code, to correct a scrivener's error; to the Committee 
     on the Judiciary.

       S. 998. An act to amend the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe

     family services, stress reduction, and suicide prevention,
```
