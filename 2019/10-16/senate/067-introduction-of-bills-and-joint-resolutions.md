              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. BURR (for himself, Mr. Manchin, Mr. Tillis, Mr.

     for other purposes; to the Committee on Environment and 
     Public Works.

       S. 2603. A bill to amend the Immigration and Nationality 
     Act to end the immigrant visa backlog, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on the Judiciary.

     Transportation.

       S. 2605. A bill to amend title 49, United States Code, to

     purposes; to the Committee on Commerce, Science, and 
     Transportation.
           By Ms. HASSAN:
       S. 2608. A bill to amend the Higher Education Act of 1965

     projects; to the Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and 
     Pensions.
           By Mr. SCHUMER (for Mr. Sanders):
       S. 2609. A bill to amend the Child Nutrition Act of 1966

           By Ms. MURKOWSKI (for herself and Ms. Smith):
       S. 2610. A bill to reauthorize certain programs under the

     of Energy, and for other purposes; to the Committee on Indian

       S. 2611. A bill to amend title 49, United States Code, to

     Transportation.

           By Mr. SCHUMER (for Ms. Harris (for herself, Ms.

       S. 2613. A bill to provide a path to end homelessness in 
     the United States, and for other purposes; to the Committee

       S. 2614. A bill to prohibit certain noncompete agreements,
