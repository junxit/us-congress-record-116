# AMENDMENTS SUBMITTED AND PROPOSED

```
                    AMENDMENTS SUBMITTED AND PROPOSED

     proposed by him to the bill S. 2511, to amend title 40, 
     United States Code, to provide the Marshal of the Supreme 
     Court of the United States and Supreme Court Police with the

     individuals in any location, and for other purposes; which
```
