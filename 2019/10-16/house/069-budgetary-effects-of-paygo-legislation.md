# BUDGETARY EFFECTS OF PAYGO LEGISLATION

**Measures:** H.R. 1496

```
                 BUDGETARY EFFECTS OF PAYGO LEGISLATION

  Pursuant to the Statutory Pay-As-You-Go Act of 2010 (PAYGO), Mr. 
Yarmuth hereby submits, prior to the vote on passage, for printing in 
the Congressional Record, that H.R. 1496, the Presidential Allowance 
Modernization Act of 2019, as amended, would have no significant effect 
on the deficit, and therefore, the budgetary effects of such bill are 
estimated as zero.
```
