  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on ordering the previous question on 
the resolution (H. Res. 629) providing for consideration of the bill 
(H.R. 1815) to require the Securities and Exchange Commission, when 
developing rules and regulations about disclosures to retail investors, 
to conduct investor testing, including a survey and interviews of 
retail investors, and for other purposes, and providing for 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 3624) to amend the Securities Exchange 
Act of 1934 to require the disclosure of the total number of domestic 
and foreign employees of certain public companies, and for other 
purposes, on which the yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the resolution.
