# CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF BENEFITS

**Measures:** H.R. 1815, H.R. 3624

```
    CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF 
                                BENEFITS

   Under clause 9 of rule XXI, lists or statements on congressional 
earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits were 
submitted as follows:

        The amendment to be offered by Representative Huizenga, or 
     a designee, to H.R. 1815, the SEC Disclosure Effectiveness 
     Testing Act does not contain any congressional earmarks, 
     limited tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits as defined 
     in clause 9 of rule XXI.
        The amendment to be offered by Representative Huizenga, or 
     a designee, to H.R. 3624, the Outsourcing Accountability Act 
     of 2019 does not contain any congressional earmarks, limited 
     tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits as defined in clause 
     9 of rule XXI.
```
