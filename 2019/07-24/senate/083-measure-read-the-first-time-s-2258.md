**Measures:** S. 2258

                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 2258

  Mrs. HYDE-SMITH. Mr. President, I understand that there is a bill at 
the desk, and I ask for its first reading.

       A bill (S. 2258) to provide anti-retaliation protections
