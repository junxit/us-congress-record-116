# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

**Speaking:** Hyde-Smith, Cindy (H001079) [R-MS]

```
            UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mrs. HYDE-SMITH. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that at 1:45 
p.m. tomorrow, the Senate proceed to executive session for the 
consideration of Calendar No. 375; that the Senate vote on the 
nomination with no intervening action or debate; that if confirmed, the 
motion to reconsider be considered made and laid upon the table with no 
intervening action or debate; that no further motions be in order; that 
any statements related to the nomination be printed in the Record, the 
President be immediately notified of the Senate's action, and the 
Senate then resume legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
