# TRIBUTE TO JACK CHIESA

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO JACK CHIESA

● Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I would like to take the 
opportunity to express my appreciation to Al for his hard work as an 
intern in the Environment and Public Works Committee. I recognize his 
efforts and contributions to my office as well as to the State of 
Wyoming.
  Al is a native of New Jersey. He is a student at the College of 
William and Mary. He has demonstrated a strong work ethic, which has 
made him an invaluable asset to our office. The quality of his work is 
reflected in his great efforts over the last several months.
  I want to thank Al for the dedication he has shown while working for 
me and my staff. It is a pleasure to have him as part of our team. I 
know he will have continued success with all of his future endeavors. I 
wish him all my best on his journey.●

                          ____________________
```
