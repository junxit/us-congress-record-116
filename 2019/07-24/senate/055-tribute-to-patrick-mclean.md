# TRIBUTE TO PATRICK McLEAN

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO PATRICK McLEAN

● Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I would like to take the 
opportunity to express my appreciation to Patrick for his hard work as 
an intern in the Environment and Public Works Committee. I recognize 
his efforts and contributions to my office as well as to the State of 
Wyoming.
  Patrick is a native of South Carolina. He is a graduate of Wofford 
College, where he studied history. He has demonstrated a strong work 
ethic, which has made him an invaluable asset to our office. The 
quality of his work is reflected in his great efforts over the last 
several months.
  I want to thank Patrick for the dedication he has shown while working 
for me and my staff. It is a pleasure to have him as part of our team. 
I know he will have continued success with all of his future endeavors. 
I wish him all my best on his journey.●

                          ____________________
```
