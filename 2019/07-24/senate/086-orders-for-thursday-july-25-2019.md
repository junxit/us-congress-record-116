# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JULY 25, 2019.

**Speaking:** Hyde-Smith, Cindy (H001079) [R-MS]

```
                  ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JULY 25, 2019.

  Mrs. HYDE-SMITH. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m., 
Thursday, July 25; further, that following the prayer and pledge, the 
morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved 
to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and the Senate be in a period of morning business, with 
Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
