                    EXECUTIVE REPORTS OF COMMITTEES

     Columbia for the term of fifteen years.

     Columbia for the term of fifteen years.

       * Ann C. Fisher, of the District of Columbia, to be a

     expiring October 14, 2024.
       * Ashley Jay Elizabeth Poling, of North Carolina, to be a 
     Commissioner of the Postal Regulatory Commission for a term 
     expiring November 22, 2024.
