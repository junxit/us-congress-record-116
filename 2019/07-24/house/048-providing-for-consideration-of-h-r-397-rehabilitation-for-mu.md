unfinished business is the vote on ordering the previous question on 
the resolution (H. Res. 509) providing for consideration of the bill

Pension Rehabilitation Trust Fund, to establish a Pension 
Rehabilitation Administration within the Department of the Treasury to 
make loans to multiemployer defined benefit plans, and for other 
purposes; providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 3239) to 
require U.S. Customs and Border Protection to perform an initial health 
screening on detainees, and for other purposes; providing for 
proceedings during the period from July 29, 2019, through September 6, 
2019; and for other purposes, on which the yeas and nays were ordered.
