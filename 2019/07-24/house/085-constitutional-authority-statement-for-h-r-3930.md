# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 3930

**Measures:** H.R. 3930

```
            By Mr. BRADY:
        H.R. 3930.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution, which gives 
     Congress the authority to borrow money on the credit of the 
     United States.
```
