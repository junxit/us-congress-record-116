# CLOTURE MOTION

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                             CLOTURE MOTION

  Mr. McCONNELL. I ask unanimous consent that the mandatory quorum call 
be waived.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

[[Page S2366]]

  Pursuant to rule XXII, the Chair lays before the Senate the pending 
cloture motion, which the clerk will state.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of David Steven Morales, of Texas, to be United States 
     District Judge for the Southern District of Texas.
         Mitch McConnell, Johnny Isakson, Roger F. Wicker, John 
           Boozman, John Cornyn, Mike Crapo, Shelley Moore Capito, 
           Steve Daines, Roy Blunt, Deb Fischer, David Perdue, 
           Todd Young, John Thune, Mike Rounds, John Hoeven, Thom 
           Tillis, Lindsey Graham.

  The question is, Is it the sense of the Senate that debate on the 
nomination of David Steven Morales, of Texas, to be United States 
District Judge for the Southern District of Texas, shall be brought to 
a close?
  The yeas and nays are mandatory.
  The clerk will call the roll.
```
