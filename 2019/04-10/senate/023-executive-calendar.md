  The senior assistant legislative clerk read the nomination of John P. 
Abizaid, of Nevada, to be Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary 
of the United States of America to the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The question is, Will the Senate advise and 
consent to the Abizaid nomination?

  There appears to be a sufficient second.

  Mr. DURBIN. I announce that the Senator from New Jersey (Mr. Booker) 
is necessarily absent.

desiring to vote?
