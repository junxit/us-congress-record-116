# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.Res. 476, H.R. 1044

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Proceedings will resume on questions 
previously postponed.
  Votes will be taken in the following order:
  Ordering the previous question on House Resolution 476 and the 
amendment thereto;
  Adopting the amendment to House Resolution 476, if ordered;
  Adopting House Resolution 476, if ordered; and
  Motion to suspend the rules and pass H.R. 1044.
  The first electronic vote will be conducted as a 15-minute vote. 
Pursuant to clause 9 of rule XX, remaining electronic votes will be 
conducted as 5-minute votes.

                          ____________________
```
