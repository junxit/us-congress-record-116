# TRIBUTE TO RYDER FUHRMAN

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO RYDER FUHRMAN

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Ryder Fuhrman, an 
intern in my Aberdeen, SD, office, for all of the hard work he has done 
for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past several 
weeks.
  Ryder is a graduate of Warner High School in Warner, SD. Currently, 
he is attending Northern State University in Aberdeen, where he is 
majoring in political science and minoring in economics and legal 
studies. He is a hard worker who has been dedicated to getting the most 
out of his internship experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Ryder for all of the 
fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
