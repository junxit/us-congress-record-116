# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 3, S.J.Res. 8, S. 866

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       133. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the Senate of the 
     State of California, relative to Senate Joint Resolution No. 
     8, memorializing the Congress and the President of the United 
     States to enact legislation, S. 866, known as the IDEA Full 
     Funding Act, during the current session of Congress to fully 
     fund the federal Individuals with Disabilities Education Act; 
     to the Committee on Education and Labor.
       134. Also, a memorial of the Senate of the State of 
     California, relative to Senate Joint Resolution No. 3, 
     requesting the Congress of the United States to enact, and 
     the President to sign, legislation that would repeal the 
     Government Pension Offset and the Windfall Elimination 
     Provision from the Social Security Act; to the Committee on 
     Ways and Means.

                          ____________________
```
