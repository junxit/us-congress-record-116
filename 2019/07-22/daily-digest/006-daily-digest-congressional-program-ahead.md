                             Senate Chamber

  On Tuesday, Senate will begin consideration of H.R. 1327, Never 
Forget the Heroes: James Zadroga, Ray Pfeifer, and Luis Alvarez

Act.

nomination of Stephen M. Dickson, of Georgia, to be Administrator of
