# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Eternal God, rescue us. Come quickly and bring the stability and 
unity we need.
  May our lawmakers who seek You find You, receiving from Your divine 
presence wisdom, mercy, and power. Cleanse the inner fountains of our 
hearts from anything that will hinder Your will from being done.
  Lord, You are our helper and redeemer. Do not delay.
  We pray in Your powerful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
