# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Monsignor Stephen J. Rossetti, Ph.D., D.Min., Catholic University of 
America, Washington, D.C., offered the following prayer:
  Good and gracious God, You are the creator of all. May we see Your 
face in all of creation and protect it as Your sacred gift. Most of 
all, may we see Your face in our brothers and sisters across the globe 
and across the aisle. May we love them as You do, and thus, come to 
know Your divine and everlasting peace. We pray this in Your holy name.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
