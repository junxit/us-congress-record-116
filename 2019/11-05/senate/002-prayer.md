# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, You look down from Heaven upon humanity's children, 
seeking for those to use for Your glory. May our lawmakers live with 
such faithfulness that they will be Your redemptive instruments at a 
time when truth seems a rare commodity.
  May You find delight in those who by faith trust You to make the 
crooked places straight and the rough places smooth. Lead our Senators 
to a fresh dedication in their service to You and humanity. Grant that 
justice will roll down like waters and righteousness like a mighty 
stream.
  We pray in Your great Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
