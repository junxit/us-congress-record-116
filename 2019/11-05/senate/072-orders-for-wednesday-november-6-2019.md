# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 6, 2019

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                 ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, NOVEMBER 6, 2019

  Mr. McCONNELL. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m., 
Wednesday, November 6th; further, that following the prayer and pledge, 
the morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their 
use later in the day, morning business be closed, and the Senate 
proceed to executive session and resume consideration of the Rudofsky 
nomination under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
