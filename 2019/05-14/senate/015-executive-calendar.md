# EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

**Speaking:** Cornyn, John (C001056) [R-TX]

```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the nomination.
  The legislative clerk read the nomination of Kenneth Kiyul Lee, of 
California, to be United States Circuit Judge for the Ninth Circuit.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Texas.
  Mr. CORNYN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that all time 
during recess, adjournment, morning business, and leader remarks count 
postcloture on the Lee nomination.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
