# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 986, H.R. 2157

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 10:03 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bills, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 986. An act to provide that certain guidance related 
     to waivers for State innovation under the Patient Protection 
     and Affordable Care Act shall have no force of effect.
       H.R. 2157. An act making supplemental appropriations for 
     the fiscal year ending September 30, 2019, and for other 
     purposes.
```
