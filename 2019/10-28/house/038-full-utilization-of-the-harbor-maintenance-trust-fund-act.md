# FULL UTILIZATION OF THE HARBOR MAINTENANCE TRUST FUND ACT

**Measures:** H.R. 2440

       FULL UTILIZATION OF THE HARBOR MAINTENANCE TRUST FUND ACT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on the motion to suspend the rules and 
pass the bill (H.R. 2440) to provide for the use of funds in the Harbor 
Maintenance Trust Fund for the purposes for which the funds were 
collected and to ensure that funds credited to the Harbor Maintenance 
Trust Fund are used to support navigation, and for other purposes, as 
amended, on which the yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Oregon (Mr. DeFazio) that the House suspend the rules 
and pass the bill, as amended.
  The vote was taken by electronic device, and there were--yeas 296, 
nays 109, not voting 26, as follows:
