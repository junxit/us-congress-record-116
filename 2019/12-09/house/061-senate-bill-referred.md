# SENATE BILL REFERRED

**Measures:** S. 743

```
                          SENATE BILL REFERRED

  A bill of the Senate of the following title was taken from the 
Speaker's table and, under the rule, referred as follows:

       S. 743. An act to award a Congressional Gold Medal to the 
     soldiers of the 5307th Composite Unit (Provisional), commonly 
     known as ``Merrill's Marauders'', in recognition of their 
     bravery and outstanding service in the jungles of Burma 
     during World War II; to the Committee on Financial Services; 
     in addition, to the Committee on House Administration for a 
     period to be subsequently determined by the Speaker, in each 
     case for consideration of such provisions as fall within the 
     jurisdiction of the committee concerned.

                          ____________________
```
