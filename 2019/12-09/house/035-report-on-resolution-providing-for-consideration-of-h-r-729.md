# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 729, TRIBAL COASTAL RESILIENCY ACT

**Measures:** H.R. 729, H.Res. 748

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 729, TRIBAL 
                         COASTAL RESILIENCY ACT

  Mr. MORELLE, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 116-330) on the resolution (H. Res. 748) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 729) to amend the Coastal Zone 
Management Act of 1972 to authorize grants to Indian Tribes to further 
achievement of Tribal coastal zone objectives, and for other purposes, 
which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
