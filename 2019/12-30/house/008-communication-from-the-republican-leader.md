# COMMUNICATION FROM THE REPUBLICAN LEADER

```
                COMMUNICATION FROM THE REPUBLICAN LEADER

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Kevin McCarthy, Republican Leader:

                                    Congress of the United States,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                Washington, DC, December 19, 2019.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
     Speaker of the House, Washington, DC.
       Dear Madam Speaker: Pursuant to 22 USC 7002, I am pleased 
     to appoint the following member to the United States-China 
     Economic and Security Review Commission on behalf of the 
     Speaker:
       Mr. Michael Wessel, Falls Church, Virginia.
       As previously agreed, because of the change in Congress and 
     the presumed statutory intent of the Commission, I am 
     appointing Mr. Wessel on behalf of the Speaker. As such, I am 
     pleased to make this appointment.
       Thank you for your attention to this matter.
           Sincerely,
                                                   Kevin McCarthy,
     Republican Leader.

                          ____________________
```
