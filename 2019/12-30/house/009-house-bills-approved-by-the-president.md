                 HOUSE BILLS APPROVED BY THE PRESIDENT

  The President notified the Clerk of the House that on the following 
dates he had approved and signed bills of the following titles:

           September 26, 2019:
       H.R. 1200. An Act to increase, effective as December 1, 
     2019, the rates of compensation for veterans with service-
     connected disabilities and the rates of dependency and 
     indemnity compensation for the survivors of certain disabled 
     veterans, and for other purposes.

     fiscal year 2020, and for other purposes.

     respect to research on autism spectrum disorder and enhance 
     programs relating to autism, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 4285. An Act to amend title 38, United States Code, to 
     extend and modify certain authorities and requirements 
     relating to the Department of Veterans Affairs, and for other 
     purposes.
