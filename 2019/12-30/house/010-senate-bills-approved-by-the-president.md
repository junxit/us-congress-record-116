# SENATE BILLS APPROVED BY THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** S. 50, S. 163, S. 216, S. 239, S. 256, S. 693, S. 862, S. 1196, S. 1689, S. 1790, S. 1838, S. 2710

```
                 SENATE BILLS APPROVED BY THE PRESIDENT

  The President notified the Clerk of the House that on the following 
dates he had approved and signed bills of the Senate of the following 
titles:

           October 4, 2019:
       S. 163. An Act to prevent catastrophic failure or shutdown 
     of remote diesel power engines due to emission control 
     devices, and for other purposes.
       S. 1689. An Act to permit States to transfer certain funds 
     from the clean water revolving fund of a State to the 
     drinking water revolving fund of the State in certain 
     circumstances, and for other purposes.
           October 9, 2019:
       S. 239. An Act to require the Secretary of the Treasury to 
     mint coins in recognition of Christa McAuliffe.
           October 31, 2019:
       S. 1196. An Act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 1715 Linnerud Drive in Sun 
     Prairie, Wisconsin, as the ``Fire Captain Cory Carr Post 
     Office Building''.
           November 7, 2019:
       S. 693. An Act to amend title 36, United States Code, to 
     require that the POW/MIA flag be displayed on all days that 
     the flag of the United States is displayed on certain Federal 
     property.
           November 22, 2019:
       S. 862. An Act to extend the sunset for collateral 
     requirements for Small Business Administration disaster 
     loans.
           November 27, 2019:
       S. 1838. An Act to amend the Hong Kong Policy Act of 1992, 
     and for other purposes.
       S. 2710. An Act to prohibit the commercial export of 
     covered munitions items to the Hong Kong Police Force.
           December 20, 2019:
       S. 50. An Act to authorize the Secretary of the Interior to 
     assess sanitation and safety conditions at Bureau of Indian 
     Affairs facilities that were constructed to provide affected 
     Columbia River Treaty tribes access to traditional fishing 
     grounds and expend funds on construction of facilities and 
     structures to improve those conditions, and for other 
     purposes.
       S. 216. An Act to provide for equitable compensation to the 
     Spokane Tribe of Indians of the Spokane Reservation for the 
     use of tribal land for the production of hydropower by the 
     Grand Coulee Dam, and for other purposes.
       S. 256. An Act to amend the Native American Programs Act of 
     1974 to provide flexibility and reauthorization to ensure the 
     survival and continuing vitality of Native American 
     languages.
       S. 1790. An Act to authorize appropriations for fiscal year 
     2020 for military activities of the Department of Defense, 
     for military construction, and for defense activities of the 
     Department of Energy, to prescribe military personnel 
     strengths for such fiscal year, and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
