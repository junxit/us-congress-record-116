# HONORING JORDAN HALL

**Speaking:** Pence, Greg (P000615) [R-IN]

```
                          HONORING JORDAN HALL

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. GREG PENCE

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, August 16, 2019

  Mr. PENCE. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize a member of my 
staff that has been with me since the beginning of my campaign.
  Jordan Hall, 23, has served in my District office since January. 
Before that, he traveled with me across the District during our 
campaign.
  Folks across the 6th District know Jordan for his work ethic, warm 
smile, and friendly demeanor.
  We are losing a strong member of the team, but Jordan will remain a 
member of our Hoosier, and 6th District, family.
  Jordan will be studying at the Indiana University Robert H. McKinney 
School of Law on the campus of IUPUI in Indianapolis.
  I wish Jordan luck, though I know it will be his work ethic and 
determination that gets him though this next chapter of life.
  I wish Jordan, and his wife Chelsea, all the best as they continue to 
pursue their dreams and ambitions.

                          ____________________
```
