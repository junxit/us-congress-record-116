# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 3825

**Measures:** H.R. 3825

```
            By Mr. VARGAS:
        H.R. 3825.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       (1) To regulate commerce with foreign nations, and among 
     the several states, and with the Indian tribes, as enumerated 
     in Article 1, Section 8, Clause 3 of the U.S. Constitution;
       (2) To establish post offices and post roads as enumerated 
     in Article 1, Section 8, Clause 7 of the U.S. Constitution; 
     and
       (3) To make all laws necessary and proper for executing 
     powers vested by the Constitution in the Government of the 
     United States, as enumerated in Article I, Section 8, Clause 
     18 of the United States Constitution.
```
