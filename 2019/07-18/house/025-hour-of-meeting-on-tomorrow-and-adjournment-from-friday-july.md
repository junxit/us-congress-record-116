# HOUR OF MEETING ON TOMORROW, AND ADJOURNMENT FROM FRIDAY, JULY 19, 2019, TO TUESDAY, JULY 23, 2019

**Speaking:** Hoyer, Steny H. (H000874) [D-MD]

```
  HOUR OF MEETING ON TOMORROW, AND ADJOURNMENT FROM FRIDAY, JULY 19, 
                    2019, TO TUESDAY, JULY 23, 2019

  Mr. HOYER. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 9:30 a.m. tomorrow; and, further,

July 23, 2019, when it shall convene at noon for morning-hour debate 
and 2 p.m. for legislative business.

request of the gentleman from Maryland?
```
