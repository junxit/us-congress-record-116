# COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 3401

```

                                              Office of the Clerk,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                    Washington, DC, June 26, 2019.
     Hon. Nancy Pelosi,
```
