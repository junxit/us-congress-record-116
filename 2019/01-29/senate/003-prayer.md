# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, who rules the raging of the sea, we come to You today in 
the assurance not of our feeble hold on You but of Your mighty grasp on 
us.
  Bring peace to our lawmakers that will tune their hearts to the music 
of Your will. Lead our Senators along the paths of righteousness to 
still waters and green pastures by Your redeeming grace. May our 
legislators so serve You that they will contribute to the coming of the 
day when justice will roll down like waters and righteousness like a 
mighty stream.
  We pray in Your great Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
