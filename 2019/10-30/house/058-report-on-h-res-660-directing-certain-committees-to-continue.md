```
REPORT ON H. RES. 660, DIRECTING CERTAIN COMMITTEES TO CONTINUE ONGOING 
     INVESTIGATIONS INTO WHETHER SUFFICIENT GROUNDS EXIST FOR THE 
    IMPEACHMENT OF DONALD JOHN TRUMP, PRESIDENT OF THE UNITED STATES
```
