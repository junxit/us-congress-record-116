# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MARCH 26, 2019

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                   ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MARCH 26, 2019

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m., Tuesday, 
March 26, 2019; further, that following the prayer and pledge, the 
morning hour be deemed expired, the Journal of proceedings be approved 
to date, the time for the two leaders be reserved for use later in the 
day, and morning business be closed; that the Senate proceed to 
executive session and resume consideration of the Bade nomination under 
the previous order; finally, that the Senate recess from 12:30 until 
2:15 to allow for the weekly conference meetings.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
