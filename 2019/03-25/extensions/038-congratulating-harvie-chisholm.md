# CONGRATULATING HARVIE CHISHOLM

**Speaking:** Cloud, Michael (C001115) [R-TX]

```
                     CONGRATULATING HARVIE CHISHOLM

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MICHAEL CLOUD

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, March 25, 2019

  Mr. CLOUD. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize and congratulate 
Ms. Harvie Chisholm, who resides on Padre Island in Corpus Christi, 
Texas.
  Ms. Chisholm recently celebrated her 104th birthday, which makes her 
the oldest Islander. She accredits her spry longevity to a diet 
consisting of chocolate and bananas.
  I would like to extend my heartfelt congratulations to Harvie 
Chisholm and look forward to her soon celebrating her 105th.

                          ____________________
```
