# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, You continue to hear and answer prayers. You have 
empowered us to catapult daunting hurdles, and we continue to depend on 
You to strengthen us for the difficulties ahead.
  Be near to us during this challenging season, as our lawmakers work 
in an effort to accomplish Your purposes.
  Supply the needs of our Senators, providing them with wisdom to 
navigate to Your desired destination for our Nation and world.
  Do for our legislators more than they can ask or imagine.
  We pray in Your holy Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
