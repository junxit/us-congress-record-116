# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 31

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 3:02 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House 
disagrees to the amendment of the Senate to the joint resolution (H.J. 
Res. 31) making further continuing appropriations for the Department of 
Homeland Security for fiscal year 2019, and for other purposes, agrees 
to the conference asked by the Senate on the disagreeing votes of the 
two Houses thereon; and appoints Mrs. Lowey, Ms. Roybal-Allard, Mr. 
Price of North Carolina, Ms. Lee of California, Messrs. Cuellar, 
Aguilar, Ms. Granger, Messrs. Fleischmann, Graves of Georgia, and 
Palazzo, be the managers of the conference on the part of the House.

                          ____________________
```
