# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 1112

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 3:02 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Novotny, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bill, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 1112. An act to amend chapter 44 of title 18, United 
     States Code, to strengthen the background check procedures to 
     be followed before a Federal firearms licensee may transfer a 
     firearm to a person who is not such a licensee.
```
