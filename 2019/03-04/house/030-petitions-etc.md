# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       3. The SPEAKER presented a petition of Mr. Gregory D. 
     Watson, a citizen of Austin, TX, relative to requesting that 
     the U.S. House of Representatives restore its former practice 
     -- last implemented on January 12, 2016 -- to publish in the 
     House's section of the Congressional Record the full and 
     complete verbatim text of a Presidential State of the Union 
     Address delivered in the House chamber; which was referred to 
     the Committee on House Administration.
```
