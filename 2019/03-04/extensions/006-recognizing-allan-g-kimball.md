# RECOGNIZING ALLAN G. KIMBALL

**Speaking:** Shimkus, John (S000364) [R-IL]

```
                      RECOGNIZING ALLAN G. KIMBALL

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JOHN SHIMKUS

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, March 4, 2019

  Mr. SHIMKUS. Madam Speaker, I rise to recognize Mr. Allan G. Kimball, 
who is retiring after serving for 21 years as the Theatre Director of 
Southeastern Illinois College.
  Mr. Kimball has been directing shows since 1977, working with several 
high school theatres and at Southeastern Illinois College. He has 
passionately worked hard to assist many students, encouraging them and 
nurturing their talents. His efforts have resulted with Mr. Kimball 
receiving two prestigious Illinois theatre awards during his career.
  The community at Southeastern Illinois College has been greatly 
impacted by his service. His hard work and dedication prompted the 
college to name their theater stage after Mr. Kimball. The theatre will 
now be a symbol of his significant role at the college and a reminder 
of his passion and dedication.

[[Page E236]]

  Madam. Speaker, I would like to congratulate Mr. Kimball for his many 
years of service. I wish him and his family the very best in the 
future.

                          ____________________
```
