# RECOGNIZING REPRESENTATIVE STEVE HOLLAND

**Speaking:** Kelly, Trent (K000388) [R-MS]

```
                RECOGNIZING REPRESENTATIVE STEVE HOLLAND

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. TRENT KELLY

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, November 15, 2019

  Mr. KELLY of Mississippi. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
former Mississippi State House Representative, Steve Holland.
  Representative Holland served in the State House of Representatives 
for 36 years and proudly represented Mississippi's 16th district. 
During his extensive career, Representative Holland served on many 
committees, including: the Committee on Public Health and Human 
Services, Appropriations, Congressional Redistricting, Gaming, 
Judiciary A, Judiciary En Banc, Budget, Legislative Reapportionment, 
Medicaid, Local and Private Legislation, Tourism, Transportation, Fees 
and Salaries of Public Officers, Ways and Means, and Youth and Family 
Affairs.
  Representative Holland is a true Mississippian who prioritized 
legislation that would create a brighter future for Mississippi. I 
thank him for his service, and I hope he enjoys his well-deserved 
retirement.

                          ____________________
```
