# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, Creator of all things, make haste to minister to our 
needs. Lead us from self-inflicted injuries as You seek to use our 
lawmakers for Your glory. In all of their labors, guard and guide our 
Senators until Your will is done on Earth even as it is done in Heaven. 
May Your peace reign in their hearts, now and always, as they become 
more aware of the inexhaustible riches of Your mercies.
  We pray in Your great Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
