# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.Res. 6

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, proceedings 
will resume on questions previously postponed.
  Votes will be taken in the following order:
  The portion of the divided question on House Resolution 6 comprising 
title II, by the yeas and nays; and
  Approval of the Journal, if ordered.
  Pursuant to clause 9 of rule XX, the first vote will be a 15-minute 
vote. Any additional votes in this series will be a 5-minute vote.

                          ____________________
```
