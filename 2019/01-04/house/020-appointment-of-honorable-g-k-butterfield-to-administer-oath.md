# APPOINTMENT OF HONORABLE G.K. BUTTERFIELD TO ADMINISTER OATH OF OFFICE TO HONORABLE WALTER JONES OF NORTH CAROLINA

**Measures:** H.Res. 22

```
APPOINTMENT OF HONORABLE G.K. BUTTERFIELD TO ADMINISTER OATH OF OFFICE 
              TO HONORABLE WALTER JONES OF NORTH CAROLINA

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to the provisions of H. Res. 22, 
116th Congress, the Chair appoints the Honorable G.K. Butterfield of 
North Carolina to administer the oath of office to the Honorable Walter 
Jones of North Carolina.
```
