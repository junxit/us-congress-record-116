# CELEBRATING GINNY WALKER'S 100TH BIRTHDAY

**Speaking:** Foxx, Virginia (F000450) [R-NC]

```
               CELEBRATING GINNY WALKER'S 100TH BIRTHDAY

  (Ms. FOXX of North Carolina asked and was given permission to address 
the House for 1 minute.)
  Ms. FOXX of North Carolina. Madam Speaker, I rise to wish Mrs. Ginny 
Walker a happy 100th birthday. It has been a delight getting to know 
Mrs. Walker and her family since they began splitting their time 
between homes in Greensboro and Land Harbor in North Carolina's Fifth 
District.
  Mrs. Walker is a mother of two, grandmother of three, great-
grandmother of six, and beloved by all of them. Shortly after moving to 
North Carolina, her husband, Delmer, started Delta Electronic Plating 
Company, and she went back to school to learn how to keep books and 
even helped in the plant and driving trucks.
  Mrs. Walker remains an engaged citizen to this day, especially in 
bridge circles and First Baptist Church, where everyone enjoys her 
pies.
  At 100, I hope to be as full of life as Mrs. Walker, and I wish her a 
wonderful birthday and many, many more to come.

                          ____________________
```
