# CELEBRATING THE DANVILLE-BOYLE CHAMBER OF COMMERCE

**Speaking:** Guthrie, Brett (G000558) [R-KY]

```
           CELEBRATING THE DANVILLE-BOYLE CHAMBER OF COMMERCE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. BRETT GUTHRIE

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, March 5, 2019

  Mr. GUTHRIE. Madam Speaker, I rise today in celebration of the 
Danville-Boyle Chamber of Commerce 100-year celebration.
  I am proud to represent the City of Danville and Boyle County in 
Congress. Danville is one of the most vibrant communities in the 
Commonwealth of Kentucky, boasting a family-friendly environment with 
many thriving businesses. Danville has been named the Most Beautiful 
Small Town in America and one of the best places to retire in America. 
This would not be possible without the great work of the Danville-Boyle 
Chamber of Commerce, which has supported the local business community 
for 100 years.
  I want to congratulate the Danville-Boyle Chamber of Commerce for 
their success and commitment to the Danville community over the last 
century. Here's to the next 100 years.
```
