# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive Message transmitted by the President to the Senate on March 
5, 2019 withdrawing from further Senate consideration the following 
nomination:

       CALVIN R. TUCKER, OF PENNSYLVANIA, TO BE A GOVERNOR OF THE 
     UNITED STATES POSTAL SERVICE FOR A TERM EXPIRING DECEMBER 8, 
     2023, VICE CAROLYN L. GALLAGHER, TERM EXPIRED, WHICH WAS SENT 
     TO THE SENATE ON JANUARY 16, 2019.
```
