# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 2479, S. 2480

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. HOEVEN (for himself, Mr. Bennet, Mr. Braun, and 
             Ms. Smith):
       S. 2479. A bill to provide clarification regarding the 
     common or usual name for bison and compliance with section 
     403 of the Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act, and for 
     other purposes; to the Committee on Health, Education, Labor, 
     and Pensions.
           By Mr. WYDEN (for himself, Mr. Crapo, Mr. Merkley, Mr. 
             Risch, Mr. Bennet, and Mr. Gardner):
       S. 2480. A bill to amend title 31, United States Code, to 
     reauthorize the payment in lieu of taxes program through 
     fiscal year 2029; to the Committee on Energy and Natural 
     Resources.
```
