# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 1980, H.R. 4863

```
Committee Meetings
MISCELLANEOUS MEASURE
Committee on House Administration: Full Committee held a markup on H.R. 
1980, a bill to establish in the Smithsonian Institution a 
comprehensive women's history museum, and for other purposes. H.R. 1980 
was ordered reported, as amended.
UNITED STATES EXPORT FINANCE AGENCY ACT OF 2019
Committee on Rules: Full Committee began a hearing on H.R. 4863, the 
``United States Export Finance Agency Act of 2019''. Testimony was 
heard from Chairman Waters, and Representatives Stivers and Lucas.

Joint Meetings
  No joint committee meetings were held.
```
