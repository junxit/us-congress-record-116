# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Gracious God, our rock of ages in life's shifting sands, give our 
lawmakers this day the abiding assurance of Your presence.
  May they strive to be Your instruments, accomplishing Your will, 
living by Your power, and being used by You for Your glory. Enable them 
to experience Your contentment and peace facing life's chaos with quiet 
hearts and vibrant faith.
  Lord, refresh their spirit, quicken their thinking, reinforce their 
judgment, and empower them to fulfill Your purposes on Earth.
  We pray in Your mighty Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
