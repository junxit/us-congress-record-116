# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,
       37. The SPEAKER presented a petition of Board of Governors 
     of the Bar Association of Puerto Rico, relative to Resolution 
     No. 15, to denounce the migration policies of the 
     administration of Donald Trump as a violation of human rights 
     and as harmful to the access of justice and to demand the 
     government of Puerto Rico to respond according to the 
     democratic institutions of Puerto Rico; which was referred to 
     the Committee on the Judiciary.
```
