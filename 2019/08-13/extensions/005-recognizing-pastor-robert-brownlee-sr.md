# RECOGNIZING PASTOR ROBERT BROWNLEE, SR.

**Speaking:** Kelly, Trent (K000388) [R-MS]

```
                RECOGNIZING PASTOR ROBERT BROWNLEE, SR.

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. TRENT KELLY

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, August 13, 2019

  Mr. KELLY of Mississippi. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
Pastor Robert Brownlee, Sr. of Louisville, Mississippi.
  On August 25, Pastor Brownlee will be celebrating 30 years in the 
Gospel Ministry. Pastor Brownlee currently serves at Agape Baptist 
Church in Louisville, Mississippi. He previously served at Union Chapel 
Mission Baptist Church in Louisville, Mississippi, New Zion Church in 
Houston, Mississippi, and Bluff Creek Church in Phoebe, Mississippi.
  During his 30 years, Pastor Brownlee has done incredible things for 
the state. He led his community through Helping Hand Ministries, 
participated in health fairs, clothing drives, disaster recovery, 
mentored the youth, organized summer feeding programs, led jailhouse 
ministry, and led foreign mission trips.
  Pastor Brownlee is a husband, father, and friend to many. I thank him 
for his many years of service, and I pray that God continues to bless 
and work through him.
```
