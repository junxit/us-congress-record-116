# TRIBUTE TO JEFFERSON CHA

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO JEFFERSON CHA

● Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I would like to take the 
opportunity to express my appreciation to Jefferson for his hard work 
as an intern in my Washington, DC, office. I recognize his efforts and 
contributions to my office, as well as to the State of Wyoming.
  Jefferson is a native of California. He is a graduate of UCLA, where 
he studied history. He has demonstrated a strong work ethic, which has 
made him an invaluable asset to our office. The quality of his work is 
reflected in his great efforts over the last several months.
  I want to thank Jefferson for the dedication he has shown while 
working for me and my staff. It is a pleasure to have him as part of 
our team. I know he will have continued success with all of his future 
endeavors. I wish him all my best on his journey.●

                          ____________________
```
