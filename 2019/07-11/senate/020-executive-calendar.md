  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the next nomination.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read the nomination of John P. 
Pallasch, of Kentucky, to be an Assistant Secretary of Labor.

consent to the Pallasch nomination?
  Mrs. FISCHER. Mr. President, I ask for the yeas and nays.
