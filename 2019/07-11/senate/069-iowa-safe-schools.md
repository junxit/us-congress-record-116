# IOWA SAFE SCHOOLS

**Speaking:** Ernst, Joni (E000295) [R-IA]

```
                           IOWA SAFE SCHOOLS

● Ms. ERNST. Mr. President, I would like to recognize Iowa Safe 
Schools as they get set to begin their annual Pride Camp on July 15 in 
Des Moines. This summer program is designed to serve as an opportunity 
for LGBTQ students from around the state of Iowa to learn, to network, 
and to have lots of fun.
  Thank you to Iowa Safe Schools for pursuing your mission of providing 
a safe environment and community for Iowa's LGBTQ youth to learn and 
grow. I am sending my best wishes to all of this year's attendees! 
Please, have a wonderful and productive time.●

                          ____________________
```
