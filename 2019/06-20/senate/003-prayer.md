# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Ruler of all, we honor You and bear witness to Your mighty power. Do 
for our lawmakers more than they can ask or imagine. Let Your holy word 
be a lamp to their feet and a light for their path. Give them a wisdom 
that clears a path through complexity.
  Lord, sanctify their thoughts, words, and actions until their 
dominant desire is to please You.
  We pray in Your holy Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
