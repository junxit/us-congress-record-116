# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 2892

**Measures:** H.R. 2892

```
           By Ms. KENDRA S. HORN of Oklahoma:
       H.R. 2892.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, clause 12 provides Congress with the 
     power to raise and support armies. Article I, Section 8, 
     clause 13 provides Congress with the power to ``provide and 
     maintain'' a navy.
```
