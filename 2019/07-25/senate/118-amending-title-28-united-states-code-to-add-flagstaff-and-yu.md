# AMENDING TITLE 28, UNITED STATES CODE, TO ADD FLAGSTAFF AND YUMA TO THE LIST OF LOCATIONS IN WHICH COURT SHALL BE HELD IN THE JUDICIAL DISTRICT FOR THE STATE OF ARIZONA

**Speaking:** Scott, Rick (S001217) [R-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 1569

```
AMENDING TITLE 28, UNITED STATES CODE, TO ADD FLAGSTAFF AND YUMA TO THE 
LIST OF LOCATIONS IN WHICH COURT SHALL BE HELD IN THE JUDICIAL DISTRICT 
                        FOR THE STATE OF ARIZONA

  Mr. SCOTT of Florida. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Senate proceed to the immediate consideration of H.R. 1569, which was 
received from the House and is at the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 1569) to amend title 28, United States Code, 
     to add Flagstaff and Yuma to the list of locations in which 
     court shall be held in the judicial district for the State of 
     Arizona.


[[Page S5115]]


  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mr. SCOTT of Florida. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
bill be read a third time and passed and the motion to reconsider be 
considered made and laid upon the table.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (H.R. 1569) was ordered to a third reading, was read the 
third time, and passed.
```
