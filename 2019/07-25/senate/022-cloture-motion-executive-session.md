# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. McCONNELL. Madam President, I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk read the nomination as 
follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of Peter D. Welte, of North Dakota, to be United States 
     District Judge for the District of North Dakota.
         Mitch McConnell, Kevin Cramer, Mike Crapo, John Kennedy, 
           Thom Tillis, Richard C. Shelby, James M. Inhofe, Rob 
           Portman, Johnny Isakson, John Thune, John Boozman, 
           Marco Rubio, Cory Gardner, Steve Daines, Pat Roberts, 
           Lindsey Graham, John Hoeven.

                          ____________________
```
