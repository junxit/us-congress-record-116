# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** McConnell, Mitch (M000355) [R-KY]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. McCONNELL. I send a cloture motion to the desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     do hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination 
     of John P. Pallasch, of Kentucky, to be an Assistant 
     Secretary of Labor.
         Mitch McConnell, Roger F. Wicker, John Barrasso, David 
           Perdue, James E. Risch, Mike Crapo, Roy Blunt, Johnny 
           Isakson, Richard Burr, Pat Roberts, John Cornyn, John 
           Hoeven, Steve Daines, John Boozman, Thom Tillis, Kevin 
           Cramer, Shelley Moore Capito.

                          ____________________
```
