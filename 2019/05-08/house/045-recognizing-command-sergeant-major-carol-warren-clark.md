# RECOGNIZING COMMAND SERGEANT MAJOR CAROL WARREN-CLARK

**Speaking:** Carter, Earl L. "Buddy" (C001103) [R-GA]

```
         RECOGNIZING COMMAND SERGEANT MAJOR CAROL WARREN-CLARK

  (Mr. CARTER of Georgia asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. CARTER of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Command 
Sergeant Major Carol Warren-Clark on her retirement after serving over 
30 years in our United States Armed Forces.
  She has dedicated her time in the military to healthcare, 
administering it in nearly every capacity imaginable to keep our 
soldiers healthy.
  In Afghanistan, she was responsible for medical evacuation plans. At 
Walter Reed National Military Medical Center, CMS Warren-Clark directed 
all inpatient services.
  She also led a medical team for the 57th Presidential inauguration.
  Her latest position has been as command sergeant major for the Winn 
Army Community Hospital located in the First Congressional District of 
Georgia, where her ability to increase efficiency and readiness has 
shined. By decreasing administrative costs 5 percent at the hospital 
and boosting soldier deployment readiness 5 percent, she has made 
significant contributions to every soldier and our military as a whole.
  Mr. Speaker, I thank Command Sergeant Major Warren-Clark for her 
commitment and her service to our Nation.
  Enjoy your retirement, and congratulations.

                          ____________________
```
