# Recognition of the Minority Leader (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                   Recognition of the Minority Leader

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Democratic leader is 
recognized.
  Mr. SCHUMER. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that I give my 
remarks under leader time and that the vote occurs immediately after.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
```
