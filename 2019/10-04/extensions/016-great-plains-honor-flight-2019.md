# GREAT PLAINS HONOR FLIGHT 2019

**Speaking:** Buck, Ken (B001297) [R-CO]

```
                     GREAT PLAINS HONOR FLIGHT 2019

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. KEN BUCK

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 4, 2019

  Mr. BUCK. Madam Speaker, in honor of America's heroic veterans, the 
Honor Flight Network conducts annual trips to Washington, D.C. This is 
the first ever High Plains Honor Flight, following the conclusion of 
the Northern Colorado Honor Flight which had taken more than 3,000 of 
our nation's heroes to visit and reflect at their war memorials over 
the past decade. On September 15, 2019, High Plains Honor Flight 
Northern held an Honor Flight that gave many of our courageous veterans 
this extraordinary opportunity. I am pleased to recognize the September 
I5, 2019 Honor Flight honoring World War II, Korean War, and Vietnam 
War veterans of Colorado.
```
