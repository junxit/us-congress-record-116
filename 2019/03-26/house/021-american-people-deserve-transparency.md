# AMERICAN PEOPLE DESERVE TRANSPARENCY

**Speaking:** Collins, Doug (C001093) [R-GA]

```
                  AMERICAN PEOPLE DESERVE TRANSPARENCY

  (Mr. COLLINS of Georgia asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. COLLINS of Georgia. Madam Speaker, over the past few weeks, I 
have released the transcripts of the interviews from Peter Strzok, 
Bruce Ohr, and Lisa Page from the Judiciary Committee's investigation 
into apparent wrongdoing at the FBI and DOJ. I have said I would make 
additional transcripts from the investigation public, and I am here 
today to keep that promise.
  As I stated then, our interview transcripts were pertinent to 
congressional investigation. But the 115th Congress ended, the 
investigation was closed, and copies were shared with certain Members 
of Congress.
  Madam Speaker, I request that the link www.dougcollins.house.gov/
papadopoulos be placed in the Record so the American people can review 
the transcript of George Papadopoulos. The American people deserve to 
know the origins of the probe against President Trump's campaign.
  I will continue to release as many transcripts as possible. The 
American people deserve transparency.

                          ____________________
```
