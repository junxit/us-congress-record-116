# TO INFORM THE SENATE THAT A QUORUM OF THE HOUSE HAS ASSEMBLED AND OF THE ELECTION OF THE SPEAKER AND THE CLERK

**Speaking:** Hoyer, Steny H. (H000874) [D-MD]

**Measures:** H.Res. 2

```
 TO INFORM THE SENATE THAT A QUORUM OF THE HOUSE HAS ASSEMBLED AND OF 
               THE ELECTION OF THE SPEAKER AND THE CLERK

  Mr. HOYER. Mr. Speaker, I offer a privileged resolution and ask for 
its immediate consideration.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

                               H. Res. 2

       Resolved, That the Senate be informed that a quorum of the 
     House of Representatives has assembled; that Nancy Pelosi, a 
     Representative from the State of California, has been elected 
     Speaker; and that Karen L. Haas, a citizen of the State of 
     Maryland, has been elected Clerk of the House of 
     Representatives of the One Hundred Sixteenth Congress.

[[Page H8]]

  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
