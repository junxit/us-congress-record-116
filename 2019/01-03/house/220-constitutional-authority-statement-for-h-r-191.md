# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 191

**Measures:** H.R. 191

```
           By Mr. MASSIE:
       H.R. 191.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This legislation is authorized by Article I, Section 6, 
     Clause 1 of the Constitution: ``The Senators and 
     Representatives shall receive a compensation for their 
     services, to be ascertained by law, and paid out of the 
     treasury of the United States.''
```
