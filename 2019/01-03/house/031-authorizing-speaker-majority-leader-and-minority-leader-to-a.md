# AUTHORIZING SPEAKER, MAJORITY LEADER, AND MINORITY LEADER TO ACCEPT RESIGNATIONS AND MAKE APPOINTMENTS DURING THE 116TH CONGRESS

**Speaking:** Hoyer, Steny H. (H000874) [D-MD]

```
  AUTHORIZING SPEAKER, MAJORITY LEADER, AND MINORITY LEADER TO ACCEPT 
      RESIGNATIONS AND MAKE APPOINTMENTS DURING THE 116TH CONGRESS

  Mr. HOYER. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that during the

[[Page H198]]

authorized to accept resignations and to make appointments authorized
```
