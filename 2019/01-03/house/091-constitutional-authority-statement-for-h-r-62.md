# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 62

**Measures:** H.R. 62

```
 By Mr. BANKS: 
H.R. 62. Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
to the following: The constitutional authority of Congress to 
enact this legislation is provided by Article I, section 8 of the 
United States Constitution, specifically clause 18 (relating to 
the power to make all laws necessary and proper for carrying out 
the powers vested in Congress).
```
