SENATE RESOLUTION 3--TO ELECT CHUCK GRASSLEY, A SENATOR FROM THE STATE 
OF IOWA, TO BE PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE OF THE SENATE OF THE UNITED STATES

considered and agreed to:
