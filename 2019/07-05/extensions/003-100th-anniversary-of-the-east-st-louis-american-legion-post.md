# 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE EAST ST. LOUIS AMERICAN LEGION POST 53

**Speaking:** Bost, Mike (B001295) [R-IL]

```
    100TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE EAST ST. LOUIS AMERICAN LEGION POST 53

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. MIKE BOST

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, July 5, 2019

  Mr. BOST. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor the 100th anniversary 
of East St. Louis American Legion Post 53. During the last century, 
this post has provided countless local veterans with services, support, 
and fellowship. Such commitment to our heroes has helped the American 
Legion become one of the largest non-profit organizations in the United 
States.
  After World War I ended with the signing of the Armistice in November 
1918, veterans came back to America wanting to remain a close-knit 
community. After Congress chartered the American Legion later that 
year, Post 53 in East St. Louis became a pillar of the national 
organization. As a Marine and the father of a Marine, I am so grateful 
for all of the work the American Legion at Post 53 has done over the 
years to bring our heroes together and ensure they receive the benefits 
they deserve.
  Madam Speaker, please join me in recognizing this momentous occasion 
and commend the East St. Louis American Legion Post 53 for the great 
achievements they have achieved over the last 100 years.

                          ____________________
```
