# REMOVAL OF NAMES OF MEMBERS AS COSPONSORS OF H.R. 3193

**Speaking:** Garamendi, John (G000559) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 3193

```
         REMOVAL OF NAMES OF MEMBERS AS COSPONSORS OF H.R. 3193

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the names of 
Representative Kildee from Michigan and Representative Luria from 
Virginia be removed as cosponsors of H.R. 3193, the Transportation 
Emergency Relief Funds Availability Act, of which I am the sponsor.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from California?
  There was no objection.
```
