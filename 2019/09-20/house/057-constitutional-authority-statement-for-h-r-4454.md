# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4454

**Measures:** H.R. 4454

```
           By Mr. SCHIFF:
       H.R. 4454.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Disclosing Official Spending at Presidential Businesses Act

     Clause 7, and as well as Article I, Section 8, Clause 18, the 
     Necessary and Proper Clause.
```
