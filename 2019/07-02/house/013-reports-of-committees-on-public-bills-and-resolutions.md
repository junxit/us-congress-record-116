# REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.R. 2440

```
         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XIII, reports of committees were delivered to 
the Clerk for printing and reference to the proper calendar, as 
follows:

       Mr. DeFAZIO: Committee on Transportation and 
     Infrastructure. H.R. 2440. A bill to provide for the use of 
     funds in the Harbor Maintenance Trust Fund for the purposes 
     for which the funds were collected and to ensure that funds 
     credited to the Harbor Maintenance Trust Fund are used to 
     support navigation, and for other purposes (Rept. 116-136, 
     Pt. 1). Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on the 
     state of the Union.


                         Discharge of Committee

  Pursuant to clause 2 of rule XIII, the Committee on the Budget 
discharged from further consideration. H.R. 2440 referred to the 
Committee of the Whole House on the state of the Union.

                          ____________________
```
