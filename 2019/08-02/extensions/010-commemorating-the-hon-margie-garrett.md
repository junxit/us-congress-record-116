# COMMEMORATING THE HON. MARGIE GARRETT

**Speaking:** Barragan, Nanette Diaz (B001300) [D-CA]

```
                 COMMEMORATING THE HON. MARGIE GARRETT

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. NANETTE DIAZ BARRAGAN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, August 2, 2019

  Ms. BARRAGAN. Madam Speaker, we lost a pillar of the Compton 
community and a dedicated champion for our students, Hon. Margie 
Garrett. Although she will be greatly missed by her friends, family, 
and our community, we take comfort in knowing that her accomplishments 
in her mission for educational excellence has forever left a mark on 
the life of every child in the Compton Unified School District. Let us 
honor her legacy by providing everyone with the highest quality of 
education they deserve.

                          ____________________
```
