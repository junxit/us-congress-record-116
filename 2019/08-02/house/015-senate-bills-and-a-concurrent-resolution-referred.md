# SENATE BILLS AND A CONCURRENT RESOLUTION REFERRED

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 24, S. 347, S. 394, S. 929, S. 1196, S. 2052

```
           SENATE BILLS AND A CONCURRENT RESOLUTION REFERRED

  Bills and a concurrent resolution of the Senate of the following 
titles were taken from the Speaker's table and, under the rule, 
referred as follows:

       S. 347. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 40 Fulton Street in 
     Middletown, New York, as the ``Benjamin A. Gilman Post Office 
     Building''; to the Committee on Oversight and Reform.
       S. 394. An act to amend the Presidential Transition Act of 
     1963 to improve the orderly transfer of the executive power 
     during Presidential transitions; to the Committee on 
     Oversight and Reform.
       S. 929. An act to amend title 28, United States Code, to 
     redefine the eastern and middle judicial districts of North 
     Carolina; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
       S. 1196. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 1715 Linnerud Drive in Sun 
     Prairie, Wisconsin, as the ``Fire Captain Cory Barr Post 
     Office Building''; to the Committee on Oversight and Reform.
       S. 2052. An act to authorize the honorary promotion of 
     Colonel Charles E. McGee to brigadier general in the United 
     States Air Force; to the Committee on Armed Services.
       S. Con. Res. 24. Concurrent Resolution recognizing the 50th 
     anniversary of the Food and Nutrition Service of the 
     Department of Agriculture; to the Committee on Agriculture.

                          ____________________
```
