# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 1595, H.R. 2203

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 10:14 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bills, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 1595. An act to create protections for depository 
     institutions that provide financial services to cannabis-
     related legitimate businesses and service providers for such 
     businesses, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 2203. An act to increase transparency, accountability, 
     and community engagement within the Department of Homeland 
     Security, provide independent oversight of border security 
     activities, improve training for agents and officers of U.S. 
     Customs and Border Protection and U.S. Immigration and 
     Customs Enforcement, and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
