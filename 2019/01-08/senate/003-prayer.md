# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Merciful God, enthroned far above all other powers, we need You to 
exercise Your might for our Nation during this challenging season. As 
we wrestle with the stalemate of this partial government shutdown, 
inspire our lawmakers to do what is best for our Nation and world. 
Remind them that Your power is far above any conceivable command, 
authority, or control. Help them to appreciate their accountability to 
You, as You guide them to contribute to unity and finding common 
ground. Be near to those who are the collateral damage of this impasse, 
supporting them with Your wisdom, power, mercy, and grace.
  We pray in Your sovereign Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
