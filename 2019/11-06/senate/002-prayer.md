# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, thank You for not leaving us alone in a challenging 
world. You remain our refuge in every storm. We call You when troubles 
come, and You rescue us even from self-inflicted wounds. You clean up 
our debris, restoring us to Your sweet fellowship.
  As our lawmakers commit this day to You, help them navigate through 
its turbulence. May Your Spirit impinge on their minds, guiding them by 
the light of Your truth. Lord, be the resource they need to be faithful 
stewards of Your unfolding providence.
  We pray in Your gracious Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
