# TRIBUTE TO CHARLOTTE KINNOMAN

**Speaking:** Daines, Steve (D000618) [R-MT]

```
                     TRIBUTE TO CHARLOTTE KINNOMAN

● Mr. DAINES. Mr. President, this week I have the honor of 
recognizing Charlotte Kinnoman of Lake County for her dedication to 
Montana's first responders during Christmas.
  When Charlotte met a law enforcement officer at an event with her 
family, she learned of the sacrifices our first responders make to 
protect Montana's families and communities.
  After that meeting, and at just 12 years old, Charlotte decided she 
wanted to give back to our first responders. She has been writing 125 
Christmas cards a day to give to every first responder she meets. She 
even has a goal of writing 12,000 total. Charlotte's mom and classmates 
have also been helpful in writing Christmas cards to make sure they are 
spreading holiday cheer all across Big Sky Country.
  I commend Charlotte for her compassion. Charlotte is a great role 
model for all young Montanans, and I thank her for supporting our first 
responders this Christmas season.●

                          ____________________
```
