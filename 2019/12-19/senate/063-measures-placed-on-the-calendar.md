# MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR

**Measures:** H.R. 397, H.R. 1759, H.R. 4018

```
                    MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR

  The following bills were read the second time, and placed on the 
calendar:

       H.R. 397. An act to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 
     to create a Pension Rehabilitation Trust Fund, to establish a 
     Pension Rehabilitation Administration within the Department 
     of the Treasury to make loans to multiemployer defined 
     benefit plans, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 1759. An act to amend title III of the Social Security 
     Act to extend reemployment services and eligibility 
     assessments to all claimants for unemployment benefits, and 
     for other purposes.
       H.R. 4018. An act to provide that the amount of time that 
     an elderly offender must serve before being eligible for 
     placement in home detention is to be reduced by the amount of 
     good time credits earned by the prisoner, and for other 
     purposes.
```
