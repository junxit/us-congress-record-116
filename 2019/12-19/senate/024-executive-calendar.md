```
                           EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Braun). The clerk will report the next 
nomination.
  The senior assistant bill clerk read the nomination of Mary Kay 
Vyskocil, of New York, to be United States District Judge for the 
Southern District of New York.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The question is, Will the Senate advise and 
consent to the Vyskocil nomination?
  Mrs. MURRAY. I ask for the yeas and nays.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there a sufficient second?
  There appears to be a sufficient second.
  The clerk will call the roll.
  The senior assistant bill clerk called the roll.

from Georgia (Mr. Isakson).

(Mr. Klobuchar), the Senator from Vermont (Mr. Sanders), and the 
Senator from Massachusetts (Ms. Warren) are necessarily absent.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Are there any other Senators in the Chamber 
desiring to vote?
```
