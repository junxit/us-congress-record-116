# RECOGNIZING MS. LISA WILLENBERG

**Speaking:** Hill, J. French (H001072) [R-AR]

```
                    RECOGNIZING MS. LISA WILLENBERG

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. J. FRENCH HILL

                              of arkansas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, December 19, 2019

  Mr. HILL of Arkansas. Madam Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
congratulate Lisa Willenberg for being selected as the new Chancellor 
of the University of Arkansas Community College at Morrilton (UACCM).
  The college was established in 1963 and Ms. Willenberg serves as the 
college's fourth Chancellor and the first woman in this position.
  She earned her Bachelor of Business Administration from the 
University of Central Arkansas and Master of Education at the 
University of Arkansas.
  Ms. Willenberg has served at Morrilton for 27 years, first as a 
general accountant and, in 2011, becoming Vice Chancellor for Finance 
and Operations--handling the college's finances, human resources, 
information technology and physical plant.
  Under her leadership, the college has increased its fiscal reserves, 
improved the college's financial scoring and constructed new campus 
facilities to benefit the students.
  Congratulations to Chancellor Lisa Willenberg on being selected for 
this prestigious position, and I look forward to the school's success 
for years to come

                          ____________________
```
