# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S.J. RES. 59

**Speaking:** Moran, Jerry (M000934) [R-KS]

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 59

```
               MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S.J. RES. 59

  Mr. MORAN. Madam President, I understand there is a joint resolution 
at the desk, and I ask for its first reading.

[[Page S6048]]

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the title of the joint 
resolution for the first time.
  The senior assistant legislative clerk as follows:

       A joint resolution (S.J. Res. 59) expressing the sense of 
     Congress on the precipitous withdrawal of United States Armed 
     Forces from Syria and Afghanistan, and Turkey's unprovoked 
     incursion into Syria.

  Mr. MORAN. I now ask for a second reading, and in order to place the 
joint resolution on the calendar under the provisions of rule XIV, I 
object to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection having been heard, the joint 
resolution will be read for a second time on the next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```
