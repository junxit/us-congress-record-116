# RECOGNIZING GIRL SCOUT GOLD AWARD RECIPIENTS

```

Quakertown; Kelly A. Sassaman, Levittown; Jasmine L. Scavo-Treiber, 
Yardley; Alaina G. Tarallo, Yardley; Alesandra Temerte, Jamison;
```
