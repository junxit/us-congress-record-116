# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 3788

**Measures:** H.R. 3788

```
           By Mr. BARR:
       H.R. 3788.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 12 and 13, which gives 
     Congress the power ``To raise and support Armies,'' and ``To 
     provide and maintain a Navy.''
```
