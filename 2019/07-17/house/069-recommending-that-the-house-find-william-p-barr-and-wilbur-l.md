**Measures:** H.Res. 497

                      JR., IN CONTEMPT OF CONGRESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on agreeing to the resolution (H. Res. 
497) recommending that the House of Representatives find William P. 
Barr, Attorney General of the United States, and Wilbur L. Ross, Jr., 
Secretary of Commerce, in contempt of Congress for refusal to comply 
with subpoenas duly issued by the Committee on Oversight and Reform, on 
which the yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the resolution.
  This is a 5-minute vote.
