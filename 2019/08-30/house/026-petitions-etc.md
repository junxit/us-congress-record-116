# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       38. The SPEAKER presented a petition of Mr. Gregory D. 
     Watson, a citizen of Austin, TX, relative to requesting that 
     Congress enact legislation which would revoke the naturalized 
     American citizenship of a person not born in the United 
     States who, after such naturalized citizenship was conferred, 
     was convicted of a felony violation of Federal or State law 
     transpiring before or subsequent to the acquisition of such 
     naturalized citizenship, and for whom all legal appeals have 
     been exhausted, with an option to deport such person to his 
     or her country of origin--if known--upon release from 
     imprisonment for such conviction; which was referred to the 
     Committee on the Judiciary.
```
