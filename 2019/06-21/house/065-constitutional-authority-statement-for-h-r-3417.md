# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 3417

**Measures:** H.R. 3417

```
            By Mr. NEAL:
        H.R. 3417.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority on which this bill rests is in 
     the power of Congress to lay and collect taxes as enumerated 
     in Article I, Section 8.
```
