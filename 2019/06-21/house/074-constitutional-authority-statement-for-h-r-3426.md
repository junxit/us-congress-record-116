```

       H.R. 3426.

     to the following:
       Section 8, Clause 3 of the United States Constitution.
       ``The Congress shall have the Power . . . To regulate 
     Commerce with foreign Nations, and among the several States, 
     and with the Indian Tribes. . . .''
```
