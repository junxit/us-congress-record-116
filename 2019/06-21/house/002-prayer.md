# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Patrick J. Conroy, offered the following 
prayer:
  God, our Father, we give You thanks for giving us another day.
  Bless the Members of the people's House as they gather at the end of 
another week in the Capitol. Endow each with the graces needed to 
attend to the issues of the day with wisdom, that the result of their 
efforts might benefit the citizens of our Nation and the world.
  We also ask Your blessing leading into this weekend upon a world both 
celebrating sport with the Women's World Cup competition, and facing 
heightened, even dangerous, tensions in the Middle East.
  May the best of the human spirit be manifest in all the actions 
undertaken by those in positions of responsibility.
  May all that is done be for Your greater honor and glory.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
