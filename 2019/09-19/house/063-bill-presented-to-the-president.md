# BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 1200

```
                    BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Cheryl L. Johnson, Clerk of the House, reported that on September 18, 
2019, she presented to the President of the United States, for his 
approval, the following bill:

       H.R. 1200. To increase, effective as of December 1, 2019, 
     the rates of compensation for veterans with serviceconnected 
     disabilities and the rates of dependency and indemnity 
     compensation for the survivors of certain disabled veterans, 
     and for other purposes.
```
