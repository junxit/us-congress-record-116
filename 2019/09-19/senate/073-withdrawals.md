     SHARPE, RETIRED, WHICH WAS SENT TO THE SENATE ON JANUARY 23,

     FEDERAL EMERGENCY MANAGEMENT AGENCY, DEPARTMENT OF HOMELAND 
     SECURITY, VICE BROCK LONG, RESIGNED, WHICH WAS SENT TO THE 
     SENATE ON MAY 13, 2019.
