# CONGRATULATING LOYALSOCK LANCERS MARCHING BAND

**Speaking:** Keller, Fred (K000395) [R-PA]

```
             CONGRATULATING LOYALSOCK LANCERS MARCHING BAND

  (Mr. KELLER asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. KELLER. Mr. Speaker, on behalf of the people of Pennsylvania's 
12th Congressional District, I congratulate the Loyalsock Lancers 
Marching Band on their recent State championship at the 2019 Cavalcade 
of Bands.
  The Lancers 2019 show entitled, ``Heart of the Machine,'' Tin Man's 
journey to finding his heart, has been noted as leaving fans on their 
feet cheering during halftime events at football games and during their 
competition season.
  The Loyalsock Lancers Marching Band has a rich history of success, 
winning the 2017 Cavalcade of Bands, being 20-time Tournament of Bands 
Region 4 champions, and marching in the National Outback Bowl parade. 
The band is the only band in North America to open for the performing 
group DRUMLine Live.
  Even given this rich history of achievement, band director Ryan 
Bulgarelli said that the 2019 Lancers are possibly the best in school 
history. Again, I congratulate the Loyalsock Lancers Marching Band on 
their recent State championship.

                          ____________________
```
