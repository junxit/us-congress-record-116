# REQUEST TO CONSIDER HOUSE AMENDMENT TO SENATE AMENDMENT TO H.R. 1865, NATIONAL LAW ENFORCEMENT MUSEUM COMMEMORATIVE COIN ACT

**Speaking:** McGovern, James P. (M000312) [D-MA], Roy, Chip (R000614) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.Res. 708, H.R. 1865

```
 REQUEST TO CONSIDER HOUSE AMENDMENT TO SENATE AMENDMENT TO H.R. 1865, 
         NATIONAL LAW ENFORCEMENT MUSEUM COMMEMORATIVE COIN ACT

  Mr. McGOVERN. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that it be in 
order at any time to take from the Speaker's table the bill, H.R. 1865, 
with the Senate amendment thereto, and to consider in the House, 
without intervention of any point of order, a motion offered by the 
chair of the Committee on Appropriations or her designee that the House 
concur in the Senate amendment with the amendment submitted for 
printing by Representative Lowey of New York in the portion of the 
Congressional Record designated for that purpose in clause 8 of rule 
XVIII; that the Senate amendment and the motion be considered as read; 
that the motion be debatable for 1 hour equally divided and controlled 
by the chair and ranking minority member of the Committee on 
Appropriations; that the previous question be considered as ordered on 
the motion to adoption without intervening motion or demand for 
division of the question; and that House Resolution 708 be laid on the 
table.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Massachusetts?
  Mr. ROY. Mr. Speaker, I object.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Objection is heard.

                          ____________________
```
