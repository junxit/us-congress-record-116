Committee Meetings
AI AND THE EVOLUTION OF CLOUD COMPUTING: EVALUATION HOW FINANCIAL DATA

Administrative Law held a hearing entitled ``Online Platforms and

Testimony was heard from Rohit Chopra, Commissioner, Federal Trade 
Commission; and public witnesses.

  No joint committee meetings were held.
