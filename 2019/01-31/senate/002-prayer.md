# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Lord of the harvest, we continue to seek You, for we desire to do 
Your will. You are the strength of our lives.
  As our lawmakers strive to walk uprightly, provide them with the 
harvest of truth, justice, and righteousness. May they cultivate such 
ethical congruence that their rhetoric will be undergirded by right 
actions.
  Lord, keep them aware of Your continuous presence as they find in You 
fullness of joy. Show them the path to life as Your truth brings them 
to a safe harbor.
  We pray in Your merciful Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
