# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 944

**Measures:** H.R. 944

```
            By Mrs. MURPHY:
        H.R. 944.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to Article I, Section 8, which authorizes Congress to 
     regulate commerce among the states, and to make all laws 
     necessary and proper to carry out that power.
```
