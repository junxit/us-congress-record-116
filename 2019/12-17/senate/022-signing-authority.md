# SIGNING AUTHORITY

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD], Blunt, Roy (B000575) [R-MO]

```
                           SIGNING AUTHORITY

  Mr. THUNE. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the senior 
Senator from South Dakota and junior Senator from Tennessee be 
authorized to sign duly enrolled bills or joint resolutions during 
today's session of the Senate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. THUNE. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The bill clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. BLUNT. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
