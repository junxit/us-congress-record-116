# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Spirit of God, who brought creation out of the void, light from 
darkness, and order from chaos, everything under Heaven belongs to You. 
Lord, use our lawmakers for Your glory. May their daily experiences of 
joy and sorrow, pleasure and pain, victory and defeat, bring honor to 
Your Name. Remind our Senators that no evil can stop the unfolding of 
Your purposes and providence. Lead them this day with Your merciful 
hands, providing for their needs. Bless all who labor for liberty, 
protecting them with the shield of Your love.
  We pray in Your holy Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
