# CELEBRATING GINNY TAYLOR'S 100TH BIRTHDAY

**Speaking:** Foxx, Virginia (F000450) [R-NC]

```
               CELEBRATING GINNY TAYLOR'S 100TH BIRTHDAY

                                  _____
                                 

                           HON. VIRGINIA FOXX

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, March 11, 2019

  Ms. FOXX of North Carolina. Madam Speaker, I rise to wish Mrs. Ginny 
Taylor a happy 100th birthday. It has been a delight getting to know 
Mrs. Taylor and her family since they began splitting their time 
between homes in Greensboro and Land Harbor in North Carolina's Fifth 
District.
  Mrs. Taylor is a mother of two, grandmother of three, great-
grandmother of six, and beloved by all of them. Shortly after moving to 
North Carolina, her husband, Delmer, started Delta Plating, and she 
went back to school to learn how to keep books and even helped in the 
plant and driving trucks for the company.
  Mrs. Taylor remains an engaged citizen to this day, especially in 
bridge circles and First Baptist Church, where everyone enjoys her 
pies.
  At 100, I hope to be as full of life as Mrs. Taylor, and I wish her a 
wonderful birthday and many, many more to come.

                          ____________________
```
