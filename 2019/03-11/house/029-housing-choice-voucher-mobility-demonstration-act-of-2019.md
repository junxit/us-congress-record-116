**Speaking:** Hartzler, Vicky (H001053) [R-MO]

       HOUSING CHOICE VOUCHER MOBILITY DEMONSTRATION ACT OF 2019

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on the motion to suspend the rules and 
pass the bill (H.R. 1122) to authorize the Secretary of Housing and 
Urban Development to carry out a housing choice voucher mobility 
demonstration to encourage families receiving such voucher assistance 
to move to lower-poverty areas and expand access to opportunity areas, 
on which the yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.

gentlewoman from California (Ms. Waters) that the House suspend the 
rules and pass the bill.
