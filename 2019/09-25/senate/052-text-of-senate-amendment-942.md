# Text of Senate Amendment 942

**Speaking:** Paul, Rand (P000603) [R-KY]

```
  SA 942. Mr. PAUL submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill H.R. 4378, making continuing appropriations for fiscal 
year 2020, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the 
table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place in division A, add the following:

     SEC. ___. REDUCTION IN RATE FOR OPERATIONS.

       The rate for operations provided by section 101 is hereby 
     reduced by 2 percent.
```
