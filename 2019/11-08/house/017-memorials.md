# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** H.Res. 160

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       145. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the House of 
     Representatives of the State of Michigan, relative to House 
     Resolution No. 160, urging Congress and the President to 
     support federal funding for ports, harbors, and critical 
     marine infrastructure in the Great Lakes region including the 
     Soo Locks reconstruction project; which was referred to the 
     Committee on Transportation and Infrastructure.
```
