# REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.R. 182, H.R. 737, H.R. 1023, H.R. 1088, H.R. 2427, H.R. 3195, H.R. 4258, H.R. 4863

```
         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XIII, reports of committees were delivered to 
the Clerk for printing and reference to the proper calendar, as 
follows:

       Ms. WATERS: Committee on Financial Services. H.R. 4863. A 
     bill to promote the competitiveness of the United States, to 
     reform and reauthorize the United States Export Finance 
     Agency, and for other purposes; with an amendment (Rept. 116-
     271). Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on the 
     state of the Union.
       Mr. GRIJALVA: Committee on Natural Resources. H.R. 182. A 
     bill to extend the authorization for the Cape Cod National 
     Seashore Advisory Commission (Rept. 116-272). Referred to the 
     Committee of the Whole House on the state of the Union.
       Mr. GRIJALVA: Committee on Natural Resources. H.R. 737. A 
     bill to prohibit the sale of shark fins, and for other 
     purposes (Rept. 116-273). Referred to the Committee of the 
     Whole House on the state of the Union.
       Mr. GRIJALVA: Committee on Natural Resources. H.R. 1023. A 
     bill to authorize the Director of the United States 
     Geological Survey to conduct monitoring, assessment, science, 
     and research, in support of the binational fisheries within 
     the Great Lakes Basin, and for other purposes (Rept. 116-
     274). Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on the 
     state of the Union.
       Mr. GRIJALVA: Committee on Natural Resources. H.R. 1088. A 
     bill to authorize the Society of the First Infantry Division 
     to make modifications to the First Division Monument located 
     on Federal land in Presidential Park in District of Columbia, 
     and for other purposes; with amendments (Rept. 116-275). 
     Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on the state of 
     the Union.
       Mr. GRIJALVA: Committee on Natural Resources. H.R. 2427. A 
     bill to amend the Chesapeake Bay Initiative Act of 1998 to 
     reauthorize the Chesapeake Bay Gateways and Watertrails 
     Network; with an amendment (Rept. 116-276). Referred to the 
     Committee of the Whole House on the state of the Union.
       Mr. GRIJALVA: Committee on Natural Resources. H.R. 3195. A 
     bill to amend title 54, United States Code, to provide 
     permanent, dedicated funding for the Land and Water 
     Conservation Fund, and for other purposes; with an amendment 
     (Rept. 116-277). Referred to the Committee of the Whole House 
     on the state of the Union.
       Mr. NADLER: Committee on the Judiciary. H.R. 4258. A bill 
     to authorize the Marshal of the Supreme Court and the Supreme 
     Court Police to protect the Justices, employees, and official 
     guests of the Supreme Court outside of the Supreme Court 
     grounds, and for other purposes (Rept. 116-278). Referred to 
     the Committee of the Whole House on the state of the Union.
```
