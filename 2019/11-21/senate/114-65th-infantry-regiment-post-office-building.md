# 65TH INFANTRY REGIMENT POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 2325

```
              65TH INFANTRY REGIMENT POST OFFICE BUILDING

  A bill (H.R. 2325) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 100 Calle Alondra in San Juan, Puerto Rico, 
as the ``65th Infantry Regiment Post Office Building,'' was ordered to 
a third reading, was read the third time, and passed.
```
