# JEANNETTE RANKIN POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** H.R. 1972

```
                 JEANNETTE RANKIN POST OFFICE BUILDING

  The bill (H.R. 1972) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 1100 West Kent Avenue in Missoula, Montana, 
as the ``Jeannette Rankin Post Office Building,'' was ordered to a 
third reading, was read the third time, and passed.
```
