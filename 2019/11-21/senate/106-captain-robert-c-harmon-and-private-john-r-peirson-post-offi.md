# CAPTAIN ROBERT C. HARMON AND PRIVATE JOHN R. PEIRSON POST OFFICE BUILDING

**Measures:** S. 2712

```
   CAPTAIN ROBERT C. HARMON AND PRIVATE JOHN R. PEIRSON POST OFFICE 
                                BUILDING

  The bill (S. 2712) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 430 South Knowles Avenue in New Richmond, 
Wisconsin, as the ``Captain Robert C. Harmon and Private John R. 
Peirson Post Office Building,'' was ordered to be engrossed for a third 
reading, was read the third time, and passed.
  (The bill is printed in the Record of Monday, December 2, 2019)


 =========================== NOTE ===========================

Avenue in New Richmond, Wisconsin, as the ``Captain Robert C. 
Harmon and Private John R. Peirson Post Office Building,'' was
```
