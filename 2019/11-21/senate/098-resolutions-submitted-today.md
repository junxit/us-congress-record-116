# RESOLUTIONS SUBMITTED TODAY

**Speaking:** Fischer, Deb (F000463) [R-NE]

**Measures:** S.Res. 437, S.Res. 438, S.Res. 439, S.Res. 440, S.Res. 441

```
                      RESOLUTIONS SUBMITTED TODAY

  Mrs. FISCHER. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
now proceed to the en bloc consideration of the following Senate 
resolutions, which were submitted earlier today: S. Res. 437, S. Res. 
438, S. Res. 439, S. Res. 440, and S. Res. 441.
  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the 
resolutions en bloc.
  Mrs. FISCHER. I ask unanimous consent that the resolutions be agreed 
to, that the preambles be agreed to, and that the motions to reconsider 
be considered made and laid upon the table, all en bloc.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The resolutions (S. Res. 437, S. Res. 438, S. Res. 439, S. Res. 440, 
and S. Res. 441) were agreed to.
  The preambles were agreed to.
  (The resolutions, with their preambles, are printed in today's Record 
under ``Submitted Resolutions.'')

                          ____________________
```
