# HONORING SIGMON TAYLOR PHOTOGRAPHY FOR RECEIVING THE 2019 JEFF MILLER AWARD

**Speaking:** Luria, Elaine G. (L000591) [D-VA]

```
 HONORING SIGMON TAYLOR PHOTOGRAPHY FOR RECEIVING THE 2019 JEFF MILLER 
                                 AWARD

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. ELAINE G. LURIA

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 16, 2019

  Mrs. LURIA. Madam Speaker, I rise today to honor and recognize Monica 
Sigmon and Michael Taylor of Sigmon Taylor Photography for receiving 
the 2019 Jeff Miller Award through the Hope House Foundation.
  Sigmon Taylor Photography is honored for taking professional 
photographs for the Hope House's Annual Fund Campaign. Their high-
quality images captured the individuals the Hope House serves and 
helped change the way those individuals are perceived by displaying 
dignity, life, power, and love through visually powerful artwork.
  Monica Sigmon and Michael Taylor exemplify leadership, integrity, 
imagination, and optimism through business and community service.

                          ____________________
```
