                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 64

  At the request of Ms. Klobuchar, the names of the Senator from 
Maryland (Mr. Van Hollen) and the Senator from Maine (Ms. Collins) were 
added as cosponsors of S. 64, a bill to prohibit brand name drug 
companies from compensating generic drug companies to delay the entry 
of a generic drug into the market, and to prohibit biological product 
manufacturers from compensating biosimilar and interchangeable 
companies to delay the entry of biosimilar biological products and 
interchangeable biological products.
