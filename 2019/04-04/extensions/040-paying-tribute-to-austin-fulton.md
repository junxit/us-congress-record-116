# PAYING TRIBUTE TO AUSTIN FULTON

**Speaking:** Curtis, John R. (C001114) [R-UT]

```
                    PAYING TRIBUTE TO AUSTIN FULTON

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN R. CURTIS

                                of utah

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 4, 2019

  Mr. CURTIS. Madam Speaker, I rise today to pay tribute to the service 
and work of Austin Fulton. Austin has played an instrumental role in 
setting up my Congressional office. He previously worked for 
Representative Jason Chaffetz and helped through the successful 
transition after I was elected to Congress. He was later promoted from 
Staff Assistant to Legislative Correspondent, where he worked 
diligently on a variety of policy issues, including housing, veterans, 
and space policy.
  Austin had a positive attitude and was willing to help with any task 
no matter the scope. I am grateful for all his service, and I wish him 
the best of luck in his next chapter. Despite his departure, he will 
always be a member of Team Curtis.

                          ____________________
```
