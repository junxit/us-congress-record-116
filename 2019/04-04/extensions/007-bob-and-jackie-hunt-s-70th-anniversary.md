# BOB AND JACKIE HUNT'S 70TH ANNIVERSARY

**Speaking:** Webster, Daniel (W000806) [R-FL]

```
                 BOB AND JACKIE HUNT'S 70TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. DANIEL WEBSTER

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 4, 2019

  Mr. WEBSTER of Florida. Madam Speaker, I am honored to congratulate 
Bob and Jackie Hunt of Leesburg, Florida on their 70th anniversary of 
marriage.
  Bob and Jackie are lifelong residents of Lake County Florida. Bob 
served in the Navy during World War II. After the war, Bob returned to 
Florida and joined the Citrus industry. Jackie was a Licensed Practical 
Nurse (LPN) and working together, they raised and put their three 
children through college.
  Marriage and strong families are indispensable contributors to the 
prosperity of our nation. Bob and Jackie, I am honored to recognize 
your loyalty and faithfulness to one another and your family. I offer 
my sincerest congratulations for 70 years of marriage. May God continue 
to bless them and their family in the years to come.

                          ____________________
```
