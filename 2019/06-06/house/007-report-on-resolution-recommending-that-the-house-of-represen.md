```
  REPORT ON RESOLUTION RECOMMENDING THAT THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES 
FIND WILLIAM P. BARR, ATTORNEY GENERAL, U.S. DEPARTMENT OF JUSTICE, IN 
CONTEMPT OF CONGRESS FOR REFUSAL TO COMPLY WITH A SUBPOENA DULY ISSUED 
                   BY THE COMMITTEE ON THE JUDICIARY

  Mr. RASKIN, from the Committee on the Judiciary, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 116-105) on the resolution recommending 
that the House of Representatives find William P. Barr, Attorney 
General, U.S. Department of Justice, in contempt of Congress for 
refusal to comply with a subpoena duly issued by the Committee on the 
Judiciary, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be 
printed.
```
