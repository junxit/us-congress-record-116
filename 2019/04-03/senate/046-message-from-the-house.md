# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 540, S. 725

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 12:33 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Novotny, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bill, without amendment:

       S. 725. An act to change the address of the postal facility 
     designated in honor of Captain Humayun Khan.

  The message also announced that the House has passed the following 
bill, in which it requests the concurrence of the Senate:

       H.R. 540. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 770 Ayrault Road in 
     Fairport, New York, as the ``Louise and Bob Slaughter Post 
     Office''.

                          ____________________
```
