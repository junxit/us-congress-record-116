# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, give our lawmakers the power to live with purity. Remind 
them that for each test and temptation, You have provided a way of 
escape. When they stumble, help them to receive the forgiveness of Your 
abounding grace. May they permit Your spirit to control their minds and 
hearts, continually delivering them from evil.
  Lord, we cannot live with integrity in our strength alone, so keep us 
united with You.
  We pray in Your great Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
