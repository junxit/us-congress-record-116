                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 61

  At the request of Mr. Grassley, the name of the Senator from Michigan 
(Mr. Peters) was added as a cosponsor of S. 61, a bill to amend the 
Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act to allow for the personal 
importation of safe and affordable drugs from approved pharmacies in 
Canada.

  At the request of Ms. Klobuchar, the name of the Senator from 
Wisconsin (Ms. Baldwin) was added as a cosponsor of S. 120, a bill to 
protect victims of stalking from gun violence.

(Mrs. Fischer) was added as a cosponsor of S. 183, a bill to amend the
