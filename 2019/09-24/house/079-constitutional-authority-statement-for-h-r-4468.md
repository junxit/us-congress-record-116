# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4468

**Measures:** H.R. 4468

```
            By Mr. EVANS:
        H.R. 4468.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
        Under Article 1, Section 8, Clause 18 of the U.S. 
     Constitution, Congress has the power ``to make all Laws which 
     shall be necessary and proper for carrying into Execution the 
     foregoing Powers, and all other Powers vested by this 
     Constitution in the Government of the United States, or any 
     Department or Officer thereof.''
```
