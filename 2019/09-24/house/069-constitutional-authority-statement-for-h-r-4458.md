# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4458

**Measures:** H.R. 4458

```
            By Mr. McHENRY:
        H.R. 4458.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
        Article 1, Section 8, Clause 4: ``To coin Money, regulate 
     the Value thereof, and of foreign Coin, and fix the Standard 
     of Weights and Measures.''
```
