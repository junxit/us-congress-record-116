# BUDGETARY EFFECTS OF PAYGO LEGISLATION

**Measures:** H.R. 450

```
                 BUDGETARY EFFECTS OF PAYGO LEGISLATION

  Pursuant to the Statutory Pay-As-You-Go Act Of 2010 (PAYGO), I hereby 
submit, prior to the vote on passage, for printing in the Congressional 
Record, that H.R. 450, the Preventing Crimes Against Veterans Act of 
2019, would have no significant effect on direct spending or revenues, 
and therefore, the budgetary effects of such bill are estimated as 
zero.
```
