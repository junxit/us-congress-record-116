# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  O God who rides the wings of the wind, You are powerful, yet patient. 
Thank you for showing Your mercy to those who love You. We praise You 
for Your presence at our National Prayer Breakfast this morning and for 
the transforming manifestation of Your power.
  Today, continue to lead our Senators along fresh paths of 
understanding, providing them with insights to solve the problems that 
impede our national progress. May they look to You to direct their 
steps as You surround them with the shield of Your divine favor.
  We pray in Your majestic Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
