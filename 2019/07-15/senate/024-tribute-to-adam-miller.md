# TRIBUTE TO ADAM MILLER

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO ADAM MILLER

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Adam Miller, an 
intern in my Rapid City, SD, office, for all of the hard work he has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
several weeks.
  Adam is a graduate of Canton High School in Canton, SD. Currently, he 
is attending Black Hills State University in Spearfish, SD, where he is 
double majoring in political science and history. He is a hard worker 
who has been dedicated to getting the most out of his internship 
experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Adam for all of the 
fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
