# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Father of all, give us the wisdom to cultivate reverential awe for 
You. Inspired by this wisdom, empower our Senators to strive to please 
You. May our lawmakers remember that You are an ever-present help 
during turbulent times, eager to deliver those who call on Your Holy 
Name.
  Lord, sustain our Senators with Your might. Continue to bless and 
keep us all. Make Your face to shine upon us and be gracious to us. 
Lift the light of Your countenance upon us and give us Your peace.
  We pray in Your sacred name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
