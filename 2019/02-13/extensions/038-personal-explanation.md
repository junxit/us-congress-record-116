# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Nadler, Jerrold (N000002) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.Res. 86, H.Res. 105, H.R. 450, H.R. 507, H.R. 840

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JERROLD NADLER

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, February 13, 2019

  Mr. NADLER. Madam Speaker, I was unavoidably detained on February 7, 
2019, and, as a result, I missed four votes. Had I been present, I 
would have voted ``aye'' on roll call vote no. 68, ordering the 
previous question providing for consideration of H.R. 840, the 
``Veterans Access to Child Care Act;'' ``aye'' on roll call vote no. 
69, on agreeing to H. Res. 105, the rule providing for consideration of 
H.R. 840 and adopting H. Res. 86, providing for the expenses of the 
Select Committee on the Climate Crisis and Modernization of Congress; 
``aye'' on roll call vote no. 70, final passage of H.R. 450, the 
``Preventing Crimes Against Veterans Act;'' and ``aye'' on roll call 
vote no. 71, final passage of H.R. 507, ``Put Trafficking Victims First 
Act.''
```
