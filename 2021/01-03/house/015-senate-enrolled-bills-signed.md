# SENATE ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

**Measures:** S. 371, S. 1310, S. 5076

```
                      SENATE ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

  The Speaker pro tempore, Mr. Connolly, on Thursday, December 31, 
2020, announced his signature to enrolled bills of the Senate of the 
following titles:

       S. 371.--An Act to provide regulatory relief to charitable 
     organizations that provide housing assistance, and for other 
     purposes.
       S. 1310.--An Act to strengthen the participation of elected 
     national legislators in the activities of the Organization of 
     American States and reaffirm United States support for 
     Organization of American States human rights and anti-
     corruption initiatives, and for other purposes.
       S. 5076.--An Act to authorize the Sergeant at Arms and 
     Doorkeeper of the Senate to delegate authority to approve 
     payroll and personnel actions.

                          ____________________
```
